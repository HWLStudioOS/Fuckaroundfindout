## What we decide at Day 30

Three possible decisions:

1. **Keep all 6 platforms running.** Metrics on target across the board. Continue into Days 31-60. Add the YouTube channel readiness work in Week 5.
2. **Cut one platform.** If TikTok is dead (avg views < 200 for 8 posts) OR LinkedIn is dead (no DM activity from buyer-shaped people, profile views flat), pause that platform, redirect time.
3. **Re-audit the format.** If overall reach is bottom-quartile across all 6 platforms, the issue is the format, not the platform. Hook architecture revisit. Topic-to-tier reshuffle. Possibly reshoot Pin 2 with a different visual approach.

The decision happens on Day 30. Not earlier (impatience), not later (drift).

## Failure modes to watch for (per codex)

- **H21 fading:** by Day 18 you're back in client work and Week 3 slips. Mitigation: Sunday-night planning is non-negotiable. Block calendar. Treat it as a client meeting with yourself.
- **H8 status risk firing:** Korena or another LOR contact sees Pin 2 (origin piece) and reacts. Mitigation: have one prepared response ("the work continues, the writing is a side product of the thinking"). Don't pull the post unless they request directly. Kipling film is H8-safe by design (no face, no business-arc reference).
- **Curse-of-competence creep:** every reel feels not-quite-good-enough and shipping slips. Mitigation: B+ shipped beats A+ delayed. Pinned posts get replaced over time. Iteration is the bug-fix, not perfection at v1.
- **Score-licensing strike:** the Kipling film's score gets fingerprinted and IG mutes it. Mitigation: confirm licensing or pre-emptively swap for a properly-licensed track via Artgrid/Musicbed BEFORE Day 1.
