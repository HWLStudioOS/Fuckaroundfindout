## 1. The reset in one paragraph

Rob commissioned a luxury brand build. The sales team needs a sales channel. Both are right, and they are not the same job. This plan runs them as two lanes on two accounts: @creepersnursery becomes the working trade channel (stock, season, people, proof), @creepersinstallation stays the quiet luxury shopfront (detail, texture, process). The production model changes from big quarterly shoots to continuous light production: fortnightly Surrey shoot days, monthly New Forest, Murray Street quick-hits, and a team capture system that feeds Stories daily. Same retainer. Full-day crewed productions, Chelsea-style, are quoted separately.

## 2. Two accounts, two ICPs

### @creepersnursery (12.8K), B2B wholesale
Audience: garden designers, landscapers, landscape architects, trade buyers.
Job: make the sales team's phone ring. Brand woven in, not leading.
What this audience actually wants: what's looking good this week, sizes and quantities, specimen sourcing capability, proof the nursery delivers.

### @creepersinstallation (2.3K), HNW end-clients + the designers who serve them
Audience: high-net-worth homeowners, their designers, Landform-tier collaborators.
Job: justify the premium. The "Chanel of horticulture" vision lives here.
NDA constraint: never the property, always the element. A single root ball, a texture, a crane lift against sky, hands in soil. Restraint is the aesthetic anyway.

## 3. Content pillars

### @creepersnursery, four pillars

1. **Stock & Season** (sales lead). Weekly Stock Walk, Fresh In drops, specimen spotlights with size/quantity signals and a "call the office" CTA. The sales team nominates one stock line per week to push.
2. **The Working Nursery** (BTS). Dispatch mornings, lorries loading, potting benches, weather, the yard at 6am. The day-to-day texture Anna asked for.
3. **People of Creepers** (variety). Rotating faces: sales team, growers, drivers, office. Fixes "Michael's Instagram". Michael keeps Monday Minute because it works, but he is one voice of many.
4. **Proof & Provenance** (brand, lower frequency). Chelsea medals (two golds, one silver, five-star stand), royal contracts, 38 years. Always tied back to sales where possible: "the stock behind the medals, available now."

### @creepersinstallation, three pillars

1. **Detail & Texture** (NDA-safe). Macro plant detail, finished surfaces, material moments. ALD restraint, no decoration.
2. **The Install** (process). Semi-mature trees arriving, root balls, crane lifts, time-lapses. Anonymised, element-level.
3. **Garden Stories** (permissioned). Public and showable projects: Garden of Hope (Thames Hospice, Chloe Hamblen, the Mackesy Amara sculpture), Royal Windsor, Landform collaborations with Baz Granger and Catherine McDonald.

## 4. Posting model

| Account | Grid | Stories |
|---|---|---|
| @creepersnursery | 3/week: Mon (Monday Minute / People), Wed (Stock & Season), Fri (Working Nursery / Proof) | Daily when team capture supplies it |
| @creepersinstallation | 1/week now, ramping to 2-3/week by August as the capture system beds in | As material allows |

Grid stays clean and considered. Stories carry the daily volume. Everything scheduled a week ahead in Buffer, approved at the fortnightly meeting.

## 5. Production engine

- **Fortnightly Surrey shoot day** (Harrison, light solo kit). Banks roughly 8 grid pieces and 20+ story assets per visit, two weeks of nursery content per day.
- **Monthly New Forest** (their biggest nursery). Scale and hero visuals: rows of stock, specimen fields, seasonal change. Feeds both accounts.
- **Murray Street, Wandsworth** (small flower shop). Easy local quick-hits from East London, colour and retail-charm content, gap-filler between Surrey visits.
- **Team capture system.** Install crews and nursery staff shoot iPhone material into a shared drop folder. Harrison delivers a 30-minute iPhone settings + standards session on the first Surrey visit, plus a one-page capture guide. Revive Rob's £5/post staff incentive.
- **Edit and packaging.** David (HWL editor) processes batches, builds the queue, preps captions and carousels for review.
- **Hero film.** "38 Years in the Ground" (2:45, in edit with David) lands as the brand anchor across both accounts when ready.
