# Gym and fibre workspace decision, 10 August 2026

## Gym

Recommendation: choose MuscleWorks Bethnal Green, join it, then cancel Elevate Fitness. Do not create a gap between memberships.

- MuscleWorks Bethnal Green: £39 per month by direct debit, £44 for one month upfront, 20,000 sq ft, 24/7 and built for serious bodybuilding. Source: https://muscleworksgym.co.uk/bethnal-green/
- Third Space City: £245 per month for one-club access plus a £100 joining fee. It adds a pool, sauna, steam room, ice bath and classes, but those are not required for the current bodybuilding-plus-VO2 goal. Source: https://www.thirdspace.london/clubs/city/
- The cost gap is £206 per month before Third Space's joining fee. MuscleWorks is the better fit unless Harrison explicitly values the recovery and hospitality layer enough to pay for it.

## Interim fibre workspace

Immediate recommendation: trial Techspace Shoreditch for one day, run real upload and download tests from the desk, then choose a monthly option only after the proof.

1. Techspace Shoreditch, Luke Street: 1Gbps up and down throughout the space. Day pass £35 plus VAT, eight days per month £230 plus VAT, unlimited £350 plus VAT. On-demand booking is available. Confirm whether the hot desk can use Ethernet before relying on it for large footage uploads. Source: https://www.techspace.co/locations/shoreditch/the-studio-at-shoreditch
2. SHED Haggerston, 8 Lee Street: fast fibre, £40 including VAT per day, £190 for ten days per month, £260 full membership. It is closer to a creative production environment, but the site does not publish the line speed. Test it before committing. Source: https://shedlondon.com/what-we-do/deskspace/
3. Veda Studios, Wick Lane: advertised 1Gbps upload and download, 24/7 access and meeting rooms. Current pricing and desk availability are not published, so it needs a direct enquiry. Source: https://www.coworkinglondon.com/listings/419-wick-lane-e3-2pw-veda-studios-2/

Fallback: Hackney Depot offers hot desks at £155 including VAT for up to 70 hours per month or £280 including VAT for unrestricted nomad access, but it does not publish the connection speed. It is not shortlisted until the line is verified. Source: https://www.hackneydepot.com/packages

## Decision rules

- A claimed fibre line is not enough. Test real upload speed, download speed, latency and a representative RAW-footage transfer from the actual desk.
- Ask whether Ethernet is available and whether the connection is symmetric and dedicated or contended.
- Trial before monthly commitment.
- Keep the building full-fibre request alive in parallel. The desk is an interim throughput fix, not a reason to abandon the home connection.
