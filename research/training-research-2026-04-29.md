# 50K Build for Race to the Stones (11 July 2026): Evidence-Based Reference

Research run 29 April 2026. Used as the basis for `training/index.md`. Links at end of each section. Anything that contradicts this in future plans needs an explicit override.

10 weeks 1 day from 30 Apr 2026. 26km benchmark Sat 2 May. Flat course, ~720m vert across 50km, UK July sun. Athlete: 25M, 86.9kg, 1:45 HM, recurrent ITB/patellar tendinopathy, ~3,000km lifetime, gym-strong base, lapsed strength.

---

## 1. 50K-Specific Periodization

**Headline:** A 50K is a marathon-adjacent event, not a 100K. Don't borrow the volume bomb. Pyramidal intensity distribution with a polarized peak, peak weekly volume in the 60–80km range (NOT 100km+), longest single run 32–36km, and 1–2 race-pace specific sessions in the final 4 weeks.

- [Casado et al. 2022, TID systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35418513/), pyramidal in base, polarized in pre-comp.
- [Filipas et al. 2025, TID network meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39888556/), polarized produces largest VO2max/TT gains short-term in trained athletes.
- [Skurka × David Roche interview](https://andrewskurka.com/david-roche-interview-ultra-road-marathon-training/) + [TrailRunner SWAP plan](https://www.trailrunnermag.com/training/training-plans-training/50-to-100-mile-training-plan-for-lower-mileage-athletes/), lower-volume ultra approach: 4–5 days/week, fast strides 3–4x/week, shorter long runs with intensity.
- [Koop / CTS Trainright](https://trainright.com/ultrarunning/), Tuesday medium-long beats forced weekend B2B for low-base athletes.

## 2. Heat Acclimation for July UK Trail

**Headline:** Adaptations come fast, plasma volume 5–7 days, full induction 8–14 days. Don't start before week 8. Post-run sauna 30 min @ ~85–90°C, 4x/week for 3 weeks (final session ~36–48h pre-race) is highest-evidence, lowest-disruption.

- [Stanley et al. 2021, post-exercise sauna](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7862510/), full HA-equivalent adaptations, +32% TTE.
- [Kissling 2024, meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13102-024-01038-6), sauna and hot-water immersion equally effective.
- [Périard, Racinais 2024, condensed HA](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/japplphysiol.00775.2024), 4×75min/2 days elicits much of 8-day adaptation.
- [Koop heat acclim cheat sheet](https://trainright.com/ultrarunners-heat-acclimation-cheat-sheet/), heat decay starts at 1 week, last hard exposure ≥2–3 days pre-race.

## 3. Knee-Safe Strength Programming (PFPS / Patellar Tendon History)

**Headline:** 2024 evidence has shifted from "eccentric only" to **isometric + heavy slow resistance + hip abductor strength**. Spanish squats (isometric) for irritable patellar tendons. Hip abduction work is the single best predictor of ITBS resolution. Minimum effective dose: 2x/week, 30–40 min, 4–6 working sets.

- [Sancho 2024, patellar tendinopathy systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12259690/), isometric immediate analgesia, HSR matches eccentric long-term.
- [Charles 2024, ITBS systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11377285/), hip abductor strengthening most consistent intervention.
- [Iversen 2023, minimalist resistance training](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-023-01949-3), 1 set 6–15 reps at 30–80% 1RM maintains strength, reduces injury risk.
- [Mend Colorado, Spanish squat clinical use](https://www.mendcolorado.com/physical-therapy-blog/2025/9/29/the-power-of-the-spanish-squat-for-knee-pain/), load tolerance + tendon adaptation.

## 4. Concurrent Training (Run + Lift + Recomp)

**Headline:** Interference effect for hypertrophy is largely a myth at this volume; explosive strength suffers when sessions are within 3h. Recomp + endurance + race PB simultaneously is biologically slow, prioritise race performance, accept maintenance lifting, recomp aggressively post-race. Protein 1.8–2.0 g/kg/day, 4 feeds. Modest deficit only (~250 kcal).

- [Schumann 2022, concurrent training meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8891239/), no impairment to hypertrophy or max strength in trained men with ≥6h separation.
- [Wang 2024, optimising concurrent training](https://journals.lww.com/md-journal/fulltext/2024/12270/optimizing_concurrent_training_programs__a_review.22.aspx), same-day worst, ≥3h acceptable, ≥6h optimal.
- [Witard 2025, protein nutrition for endurance athletes](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12152099/), 1.8 g/kg baseline, >2.0 g/kg in deficit, 4×0.4 g/kg feeds.

## 5. Taper Science for 50K

**Headline:** 2-week taper, exponential decay, intensity preserved. Volume to ~60% week 9, ~40% week 10. Carb load 36–48h pre-race at 10g/kg/day. Sodium pre-load 24–36h before. 50K taper closer to marathon than to 100-miler.

- [Spilsbury 2021, disciplined marathon taper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8506252/), 1.3–3.1% performance gain.
- [RunnersConnect modern carb-loading](https://runnersconnect.net/carb-loading-runners/), 10–12 g/kg for 36–48h, no glycogen-depletion phase.
- ACSM hydration guidelines, sodium preload 60–80 mmol/L 1–2h pre-race expands plasma volume.

## 6. Adherence Systems (Skip Pattern is a Context Problem, Not a Motivation Problem)

**Headline:** Anchor strength to fixed cue (post-AM-run, same place, same day), use implementation intentions ("If Mon 17:00, then lift"), use external accountability (booked physio, gym buddy, paid weekly check-in). HRV-guided autoregulation only works for ≥5 days/week of data, for inconsistent training, fixed schedule beats HRV adjustment.

- [Sheeran 2024, implementation intentions meta-analysis](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/15/16/12457), d = .14–.31 on PA adherence.
- [Düking 2025, HRV mobile-device monitoring](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/26/1/3), needs ≥5 measurements/week to be reliable.
- [James Clear, habit triggers](https://jamesclear.com/habit-triggers).

## What I'd Do Differently from a Generic 50K Plan, 5 Calls

1. **Cap peak volume at ~75km, not 90–100km.** A generic plan over-prescribes for a 26km-base athlete. Tendon and adherence risk is the binding constraint.
2. **One mid-week medium-long run instead of forced weekend B2B.** Tuesday 14km steady at marathon-ish pace gives "tired legs" benefit without the Sat-Sun compression.
3. **Strength is the highest-ROI intervention given his history, make it sacred.** 2x/week non-negotiable, first thing protected when life intrudes.
4. **Heat acclimation starts week 8, not earlier.** Adaptations decay 7–14 days without exposure. 12 sauna sessions in final 3 weeks is the sweet spot.
5. **Reframe "lose 3kg" as "lose 2kg by race + retention."** A 3kg cut while building 50K fitness is too aggressive. Save autumn for body comp.
