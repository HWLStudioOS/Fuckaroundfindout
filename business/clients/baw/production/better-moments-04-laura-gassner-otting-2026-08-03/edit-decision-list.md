## Why this cut

The original episode launch led with following your passion. One of the two existing Laura shorts already packages the Four Horsemen of Success, and the other packages "Whose goal is this?" The Better Moment therefore needs a larger argument.

The four Cs provide that argument. They are memorable, diagnostic and useful to both employees and managers. The success material earns the framework. Consonance gives it a name. The final life-stage section stops the framework becoming another universal career prescription.

The short "Whose goal is this?" section remains in the long edit because it is the necessary hinge between external success and a personal scorecard. It is not reused as the week's teaser.
