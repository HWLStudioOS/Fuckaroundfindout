When I tell people I work in construction, they picture mud, scaffolding and bad weather.

The reality looks more like advanced manufacturing.

My team assembles buildings from precision components made in a factory. Precast concrete panels weighing tens of tonnes. Steel sections engineered to the millimetre. Delivered to site and assembled like a kit of parts, in a fraction of the time traditional construction takes.

This is Design for Manufacture and Assembly. DfMA. One of the most technical areas of modern construction, and it's how the UK's biggest infrastructure is being built now.

The people who thrive in it don't always come from construction. Mechanics. CNC operators. Logistics teams. Ex-forces. People who like precision, problem-solving and working with their hands on something that matters.
