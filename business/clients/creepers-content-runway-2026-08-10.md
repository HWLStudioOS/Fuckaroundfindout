# Creepers content runway, 10 August 2026

## Decision

The calendar now runs as a six-week working runway, not a thirteen-week promise.

Each week has three committed nursery posts. A fourth installation post is optional and only runs when the image, permission and idea are strong enough. An empty optional slot is better than filler.

## What changed

- Future posts are tied to a named capture day before they are treated as banked.
- Nursery content leads with stock, trade knowledge, working process and named people.
- Sales-supporting posts use plant names, supplied sizes and honest availability counts where those details matter.
- Installation content is proof, not a second high-cadence feed. Named projects remain permission-gated.
- The week of 3 August remains a verified publication ledger rather than a rewritten plan.
- Today's lowest-lift post is the finished five-slide `Why choose a nursery?` carousel, built entirely from photographs already in the Creepers Figma file.

## Live working plan

- Calendar: https://creepers-content-calendar.vercel.app
- Active weeks: 3 August to 13 September 2026
- Core rhythm: three nursery posts per week
- Optional rhythm: one installation proof slot per week
- Capture days: S5 on 11 August, NF3 on 19 August, S6 on 25 August, S7 on 8 September, plus NF4 on 16 September for the next runway

## Guardrails

- Do not schedule a post from footage that does not exist.
- Do not invent plant availability. The office supplies names, sizes and counts.
- Do not use a project without explicit image and publication permission.
- Do not force a fourth post to satisfy a number.
- Nothing is published or sent without Harrison's approval.
