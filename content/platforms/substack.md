## What publishes

### Cadence
**One essay per fortnight.** Aligned to the waterfall cycle. Roughly 800-1,500 words.

### Format
The Substack essay is **Form A** from `waterfall.md`. The longform version of whatever the fortnight's big idea is. Cinematic essay (Form B) becomes its visual companion at the bottom of the post. Field-note reel (Form C) becomes the post's preview hook.

### Structure (proposed template)
```
[TITLE, 6-10 words, declarative]
[SUBHEAD, one line, the thesis in compressed form]

[Cold-open paragraph, a scene or specific moment, 2-4 sentences]

## [Section header, the first reframe]

[2-4 paragraphs of the argument with specifics]

## [Section header, the second reframe or counter]

[2-4 paragraphs]

## [Section header, the implication]

[2-3 paragraphs]

[Final paragraph, the line. The one a reader would quote.]

---

[Embedded cinematic essay video]

---

[Footer: "If this resonated, the next one's on [topic]. Subscribe."]
```

### Voice
Same as everywhere else, but with more room to breathe. Per `voice-dna.md` + `wisdom-canon.md`. No em dashes.

## Subscriber-acquisition mechanics

**The link-in-bio play.**
- IG bio link → Substack subscribe page.
- LinkedIn featured section → Substack subscribe page.
- TikTok bio → Substack subscribe page.

That's the entire funnel for v1.

**The end-of-essay subscribe ask.**
- Soft. One line. "Next one drops in two weeks. Subscribe if you want it in your inbox."
- No popup. No exit intent. No paywall (for v1).

**The cross-platform tease.**
- Every Tuesday a new essay publishes, that week's IG editorial post quotes the strongest paragraph and says "full essay at harrisonliving.substack.com" in a first comment.
- TikTok caption mentions the essay once per cycle ("longer version on my Substack, link in bio").
- LinkedIn editorial post Tuesday morning is *literally a paragraph from the essay,* not a different argument.

## What we measure

| Metric | 30-day | 90-day | 180-day | 365-day |
|--------|--------|--------|---------|---------|
| Subscribers | 50 | 250 | 800 | 2,000 |
| Open rate | 50% | 45% | 42% | 40% |
| Essays published | 2 | 6 | 12 | 24 |
| Replies per essay (the real signal) | 3 | 10 | 25 | 60 |
| Paid subscribers (when launched) | – | – | – | 50 |

Substack is the slowest-compounding platform. Don't measure it weekly. Quarterly cadence.

## What kills Substack

- Publishing inconsistently (the algorithm-less platform punishes inconsistency hardest)
- Treating it as a content dumping ground (every IG caption republished)
- Writing for SEO not for the reader who actually opened the email
- Adding a paid tier too early
- Notes feature usage replacing essay output (Notes is the new IG; don't get sucked in for v1)
