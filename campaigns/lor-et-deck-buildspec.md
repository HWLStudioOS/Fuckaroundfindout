SECTION 01 / The Brief.

## Slide 5 — PRIORITIES (What We Heard)
Label WHAT WE HEARD. Headline: Four things to fix from last year.
01 Widen the lens — Last year ran narrow on apprenticeships. This year covers every early-careers route, graduate to placement, across all five disciplines, not the roles people already picture.
02 Plan it, don't scramble — One campaign mapped to the September open. Warm-up from June, applications open in September, the new cohort welcomed in October. Sequenced, not reactive.
03 Show the people, not the roles — The key roles matter, and so do the faces. Named early-careers voices and the managers they will work for, on camera, before anyone applies.
04 Fix the handoff — Last cycle lost 17 of 18 register-interest leads at the handoff. This year the funnel is instrumented end to end, with a named owner and the leak measured.

## Slide 6 — SECTION DIVIDER
SECTION 02 / Strategy.

## Slide 7 — INTRODUCTION (Strategy)
Label STRATEGY. Headline: More than / you think.
P1: The hero is breadth, made literal through real people. Four early-careers routes, five discipline families, every one shown by a named person doing the actual job. Not a careers grid. A cast.
P2: Status comes from impact, not the setting. We make office, digital, commercial and design roles aspirational through scale, named landmark projects and visible decisions, not by dressing up a desk.
P3: Manufacturing sits out. That ground is already shot for the Assembly Specialist campaign, so it is spent here. The video lane is open. AtkinsRealis runs only written profiles, so we out-execute, not imitate.

## Slide 8 — PRIORITIES (6-up, Pillars)
Label PILLARS. Headline: The six content pillars.
01 The roles you didn't picture — Unexpected desk, digital, data, design and commercial roles, led first to detonate the hi-vis cliche.
02 What the role actually is — One real person per discipline, each with their own day-in-the-life. Breadth from the volume of the cast.
03 Who you'll work for — The hiring line manager on camera, naming what good looks like. A candidate meets a human before the internal step.
04 Find your route — A light navigational spine. A persona-match sorter and saveable route maps that turn breadth into a personal recommendation.
05 Real projects, real impact — Status through scale, named landmark projects and the actual tools on screen. Aspiration earned by showing the work.
06 The myth-bust — The sector-level truth, claimed as LOR's own position. Air cover for the parent, teacher and careers-staff decision-maker.

## Slide 9 — INTRODUCTION (The Edge)
Label THE EDGE. Headline: Who you'll / work for.
P1: Nobody in the competitive set does this. The hiring line manager, on camera, saying what the role is and what good looks like. Uncontested white space in UK construction early talent.
P2: It also fixes the leak. Last year 17 of 18 register-interest leads were lost at the handoff to internal recruitment. The single hard baseline we carry forward.
P3 (short): Pairing every day-in-the-life with a named-manager companion means a candidate meets a real human before the internal step. The relationship starts on camera, not after the form.

SECTION 03 / The Content Plan.

## Slide 11 — PLATFORM CARDS (Channels)
Label CHANNELS.
LinkedIn (Primary) — Named-voice native video and saveable carousels, posted from employees and managers, not the company page. Led for graduates, degree apprentices and the parents who shape their choice.
Instagram / Reels (Discovery) — Where most graduates look for career content. Grad and placement reach, vertical day-in-the-life, with Meta retargeting back into the funnel before the open.
TikTok (Watching brief) — Organic test only, no spend. Raw, employee-shot day-in-the-life posted by the cast themselves. Revisited only if the other channels underperform. *(Tag: "Watching brief". Reflects Emma's own framing.)*
YouTube (Library) — The long-form home. Each named day-in-the-life cut to one to three minutes, ending on its apply path, plus the one-day "every route" supercut.

## Slide 14 — 3-CARD TIMELINE (Calendar)
Label THE CALENDAR.
June to August / Warm-up — Break the cliche before applications open. Myth-bust air cover, the first day-in-the-life drops, the "find your route" sorter. The CTA is "what's your next move," never apply yet.
September / Open — Applications open. Every CTA switches to a named apply path into the populated hub, never a bare button. Full cast on rotation, the one-day supercut.
October / Welcome — The new cohort's first weeks, captured and posted. Reviews banked for next cycle. Handoff completion is the number that matters, against last year's floor.
