# Live demo prompt — Year One workshop, 9 June 2026

Run in a FRESH terminal, in the `HWL META` tab. Paste, hit enter, let it stream. The audience watches the engine read your real files and build five drafts in your voice. That's the whole trick.

**Idea of the fortnight (baked in below):** "You don't build a funnel, you build a world."

## The prompt (paste this)

```
Read content/strategy.md, content/voice-dna.md, content/publishing-rules.md and content/waterfall.md.

Then take this idea and run it through my waterfall:

"In 2026 the funnel is dead. It used to be wide top, narrow middle, buyers at the bottom. AI broke distribution — people find you from any angle now: a reel, a Substack essay, an X thread, a YouTube video. So it is not a funnel, it is a world. However someone steps in, from any edge, everything points at the same place: you. You do not push people down a funnel. You build a world they can enter from anywhere. Attention is the only currency left that matters."

Give me five drafts, in my voice, obeying my publishing rules. No questions first, just build:

1. Substack essay — title + the opening 150 words
2. Cinematic script — 60 seconds, Form B
3. Field-note reel — 45 to 75 seconds, talking head, Form C
4. LinkedIn editorial — stanza-style post
5. X thread — 5 to 7 tweets, the spine of the essay

End by flagging anything that brushed a publishing rule.
```

## Notes
- **Run it once before 7pm** so you've seen the output and it can't surprise you live.
- Narrate the magic beat out loud: "watch it read my actual files" — that's the inside-my-machine moment.
- **Fallback:** keep the pre-run output (or a recording) in a tab. Never let the climax depend on the wifi.
- If the audience throws their own idea, just swap the quoted paragraph and re-run. Same prompt, any idea.
