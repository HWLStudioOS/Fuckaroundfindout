## perspective.co, the product

Mobile-first funnel builder for paid traffic. Explicitly NOT a link-in-bio tool. Positioning line: "98.5% of your audience is scrolling on their phones." Every Linktree / Stan / Beacons competitor was built desktop-first then squished onto mobile. Perspective is mobile-native.

ICP is sharply defined: lead-gen agencies, recruiting agencies, coaches/consultants, service businesses running cold paid ads. Anyone whose unit economics depend on converting a Meta click into a booked call.

Pricing (EUR, billed annually):

- Base €47.59/mo, 2 funnels
- Grow €147.84/mo, 10 funnels + A/B testing + automation
- Expand €297/mo, 20 funnels + white-label + dynamic personalization
- Scale €969/mo, 60+ funnels + dedicated CSM

All plans include CRM, analytics, email, integrations with Calendly / Cal.com / HubSpot / GHL.

Differentiator vs Linktree / Stan / Beacons: those are creator monetisation storefronts (sell digital products, collect tips, link out). Perspective is a paid-traffic conversion engine: quizzes, multi-step forms, VSL gates, qualification logic, lead scoring.

Wrong tool for "I just want a tidier IG bio link." Right tool for "I'm spending £30/day on Meta and need to qualify cold leads into a booked call."

## Why they run so many ads (and what they look like)

Perspective spends ~€250,000/month on Meta and recoups within 7 days. They run 100+ creative variations per cycle on a 7-day kill/scale window.

Canonical creative pattern, observable across Niels' organic content (which gets re-cut into ads via the Thought Leadership Ad mechanism he teaches):

> result-screenshot hook → "here's how" reverse-engineer → mention of Perspective in passing → CTA to a free funnel template or quiz

Example hooks: "I generated $560k with this funnel," "Here's our viral funnel strategy (got 70k impressions, $21k sales)," "How agencies are printing $1M/Month using this AI Funnel."

The mechanism: organic LinkedIn post earns engagement and validates the hook → edit a CTA in → boost as a Thought Leadership Ad → move the same creative to Meta as a conversion campaign → land on a Perspective-built quiz funnel. He calls this "organic-to-paid validation" and it's the core operational loop.

## The canonical Perspective funnel architecture

1. **Hook (5 seconds).** Single-screen mobile page. Big claim, visual, one CTA. Sub-1-second load (they claim 2.5x conversion vs 5s loads).
2. **Micro-commitment quiz (3-5 questions).** NO email upfront. Low-friction qualifying questions ("What's your biggest challenge with X?"). Each click builds commitment. 28% cold-traffic conversion, 40-50% on quiz funnels.
3. **Personalization branch.** Quiz answers fork the funnel: different headlines, proof, offer for different segments.
4. **Education / VSL.** Short video sales letter framing the problem and the mechanism (not the offer).
5. **Proof.** Testimonials, case study numbers, social proof block.
6. **Lead capture.** Name + email + phone, only now, after commitment is high.
7. **Calendar booking.** Calendly/Cal.com embedded inline, no redirect. Some funnels split: book-a-call (high intent) vs newsletter (low intent).
8. **Automated follow-up.** Email / WhatsApp sequence inside Perspective's CRM, retargeting via Meta pixel.

## Five moves Harrison can make

### Move 1: "Documentary Audit" funnel as the Instagram-bio destination

Replace the current basic website link. Mobile-first, 4 questions:

- "What does your brand make?" (hospitality / product / sport / agency / other)
- "What's the goal?" (brand film / content engine / campaign)
- "Budget range?" (£5k / £10-25k / £25k+ / unsure)
- "When do you want to film?"

Then VSL block: 90-second Arsenal cut + Creepers cut + one-line positioning. Then calendar embed for a 20-min Discovery call.

Why it works: converts the current ToF surge from the Arsenal viral (still inside the 30-day attribution window) before the IG algorithm forgets him.

Expected outcome: 2-5 qualified inbound calls in the first 30 days from existing follower base alone, at zero ad spend.

Cost: Perspective Base plan, €48/mo.

### Move 2: parallel "Creator Track" funnel

Same mobile-first SPA, different offer: email capture for a weekly Field Notes newsletter ("How I make documentary content that travels, written from inside the work"). Lead magnet = a 5-minute breakdown of the Arsenal edit (frame choices, music sync, story beats) gated behind email.

Why it works: Year-One members and content peers want craft breakdowns, not pitches. Builds the audience-building track separately so it doesn't muddy the studio funnel.

Expected outcome: 200-500 newsletter subs from the viral spillover. Durable owned-audience asset.

### Move 3: organic-to-paid validation loop (steal the loop, skip the spend)

The Arsenal reel is already validated organically (444k combined views). Take the top three viral cuts of the next 60 days, post organically, watch which one over-indexes, THEN consider whether to put £300-500 behind it as a boost pointing to the Documentary Audit funnel.

Why it works: skips the expensive creative-testing phase Perspective runs at €250k/mo. Harrison gets the same signal from his own viral rhythm.

Expected outcome: one validated paid creative by July, with a real CPL benchmark before scaling spend.

### Move 4: Calendly-in-funnel, not "email me" or "DM me"

Calendly embedded inline at step 7. Why it works: every email/DM intro costs Harrison 20-40 minutes of reply work. Kerri already flagged email volume on 20 May (memory: `project-kerri-email-volume`). A funnel-gated booking pre-qualifies and removes the inbox tax.

Expected outcome: same number of conversations, 70% less admin, better-fit leads.

### Move 5: skip the agency tier

Harrison doesn't need Expand or Scale. Base (€48/mo, 2 funnels) handles the Studio funnel + Creator funnel cleanly. Upgrade only if he adds a third track (Laurence-style productized upsell, say) or needs A/B testing once volume justifies it (probably 500+ funnel visits/week minimum).

## Honest take on paid ads for Harrison's positioning

Paid-ads-on-funnel is a tool built for one specific economic model: commodity service with a clear unit economic (recruiter charges €5k per placed hire, can spend €500 to book a qualified call).

Harrison's positioning is the opposite: premium, scarcity-led, "I'm choosy about clients" documentary craft where the brand asset is exclusivity. Running aggressive Meta ads to a "Book a free strategy call" funnel actively damages that positioning. It signals capacity-hungry agency, not in-demand studio.

The funnel itself (Move 1) is correct and high-leverage regardless of ad spend, because it converts inbound traffic he's already earning.

The paid ads layer is wrong for him right now. The right play:

1. Build the funnel.
2. Capture the viral surge organically.
3. Post 2-3 more Arsenal-quality cuts to keep ToF spiking.
4. Reserve paid budget (if any) for ONE thing only: a £200-500/month retargeting layer to people who visited the Documentary Audit funnel but didn't complete. That's it.

The full Niels playbook is for a different sport. Steal the funnel mechanics. Leave the €250k/mo Meta thesis.

## Recommendation, summarised

**Build Move 1 this week or next.** Documentary Audit funnel on Perspective Base (€48/mo) replacing the IG bio link. This is the move that converts the existing Arsenal-driven traffic and changes nothing about the positioning.

**Don't start paid ads yet.** Run the organic-to-paid validation loop for 60 days first. Then maybe a £200-500/month retargeting layer, nothing more.

**Move 2 (Creator funnel) is optional and later.** Worth it when the personal-brand audience needs a newsletter destination, probably August-September after a few more weeks of content cadence.
