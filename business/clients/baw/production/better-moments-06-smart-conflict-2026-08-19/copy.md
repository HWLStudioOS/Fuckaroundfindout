## Revised carousel caption

Use this only after Harrison approves the revised carousel artwork.

Conflict confidence is only half the story.

Some people can tolerate the discomfort of a hard conversation. Others will do almost anything to avoid one. But confidence also interacts with a second question: are you trying to get it right, or prove that you are right?

That distinction changes the game.

Relational conflict is singles tennis: I am right, you are wrong, I win, you lose. Task conflict is doubles: you and me on the same side of the net, working against the problem.

Alice Driscoll's practical rule works at both ends of the scale:

Get curious, not furious.

If you run hot, suspend judgement and prepare one real question. If you tend to avoid, get curious about what might happen if you share what you actually think.
