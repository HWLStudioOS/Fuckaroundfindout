## Website or LinkedIn article draft

### What happens to the empty black pots?

A working nursery handles a lot of pots. Once the black plastic pots are empty and ready to leave the site, the important thing is giving them a practical route onwards.

At Creepers, the team separates those pots and delivers them to Plastic Expert for recycling. Matt Taylor manages the process. Depending on the load, the current return is usually between £50 and £100 per tonne.

The payment is modest. The useful part is having a repeatable route for a material that otherwise builds up quickly around a nursery.

This is one small piece of the operational work behind the plants. Sorting, stacking, loading and getting the material to the right specialist is not glamorous, but it is real.

We will share more of these practical nursery systems as we document the people and processes behind Creepers.
