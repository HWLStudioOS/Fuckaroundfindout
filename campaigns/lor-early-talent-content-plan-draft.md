## 1. CONTENT PILLARS

Four pillars. Each carries the one DfMA narrative, each leans toward one persona, each has a funnel job.

**Pillar 1 — The Factory Floor (DfMA reveal).** The uncopyable asset. CEMC Worksop, components made before they reach site. Lead persona: 16-18 school leaver. Job: kill the "dirty, low-status" frame `[R: CITB 4.2/10 trade appeal among 14-19s, 71% of under-25s turned off]`. Factory/"how it's made" content is viral-native, 200k-500k likes cold `[R: Hamilton Homebuilders modular tour 243k+ likes]`. No tier-one competitor fronts a factory `[R: Mace/Skanska/Balfour/Costain/Multiplex front none; only sub-scale modular specialists do]`. Risk: factory can read as "indoors, repetitive" — counter by tracking the component from floor to a recognisable landmark (Everton / HS2).

**Pillar 2 — Pay and Progression (the real number).** The conversion engine. Starting salary on screen, the Level-to-degree ladder, debt-free vs the ~£50k loan. Lead persona: school leaver + in-trade upskiller. Highest-converting trades content is a real person stating a real number `[R: Chelsea Fenton; Andrew Brown debt/six-figure DITL]`. `[A] Depends on LOR clearing publishable salary/progression figures — if not, this pillar weakens to relative framing. See Section 8.`

**Pillar 3 — The Journey (named Early Talent voice).** The named human carrying LinkedIn. "Two years ago I was choosing between A-levels and an apprenticeship. Today I'm doing X on a real LOR project." This is Harrison's trajectory angle, not "apply now." A named individual gets ~561% more reach and ~5x engagement vs the same content on a company page `[R: Ordinal]`. Proven in-category by Balfour Beatty's named-voice model.

**Pillar 4 — How To Apply (named recruiter, process clarity).** The consideration-to-handoff layer. A named LOR recruiter on process, tips, dates, destination. Carries the warm handoff so leads survive the HWL-to-LOR boundary `[R: Balfour Beatty's Lisa + Jonathan two-layer split maps exactly onto HWL-awareness / LOR-application]`.

**Optional fifth pillar (reserve):** Parents and Teachers. School-leaver decision is gatekept (80.75% say parents help decide) `[R: AllAboutSchoolLeavers]`. Reach persona (a) through gatekeepers who live on LinkedIn. `[A] Run as a Pillar 4 sub-thread in v1, not a full fifth pillar.`

## 2. FORMAT PLAYBOOK (per channel)

**LinkedIn (70-80% of paid, lead channel).** Primary unit: short vertical native video from the named voice, never reshared from the LOR page `[R: pages sit at 1-2% of feeds]`. Workhorse: native document carousels, 6-9 slides (Hook→Before→Problem→Solution→How-to→Result→CTA), carousels lead engagement 7.0% and dominate saves `[R: Socialinsider]`. Judge video on completion + first-hour comments, not views (views down 36% YoY). Cadence ~2-4 named-voice posts/week, Tue-Thu 08:00-10:00 UK. **Golden hour is the real lever:** the 30-40 reshare network fires inside the first 30-60 min and COMMENTS; 3+ commenters in the first hour ~5.2x reach `[R: meet-lea]`. Needs a pre-written comment kit + Slack/Teams trigger. Hooks (sub-10 words, contrarian/numbers-led/curiosity-gap, beat the field 2.3x): "You don't need a degree to build the tallest things in Britain." / "[£X] a year. No debt. Degree at the end." / "Why are final-year engineers turning down the big grad schemes for this one?"

**Instagram / Reels (+ Meta retargeting).** Vertical Reels (same cut-downs) plus named quote-cards as bulk reshare units `[R: Mace quote-card format]`. Reaches persona (a) where they are (76% of Gen Z use IG for career content). Hooks: self-identification ("If you're finishing school this year and everyone keeps saying go to uni, watch this"), proof-first interrupt (open on factory floor, overlay "This building was made in a factory before it reached site").

**TikTok — organic warm-up only (no paid).** Unpolished handheld named-apprentice DITL + factory reveals. "Look like a student, talk like a student." 15-45s, cut every 2-4s, hard 3-sec hook, 60%+ completion target (50-60% drop off in first 3 sec). 46% of Gen Z have landed a job via TikTok.

**YouTube — long-form DITL home.** 1-3 min named DITL (Hook→Context→Experience→Outcome). The searchable home every cut-down links back to `[R: Balfour Beatty's win was routing audience to YouTube via direct video CTA]`. Unilever Machine Operator factory-showcase is the structural template for the DfMA hero.

**Captions non-negotiable everywhere:** burned-in, 48px+ (52-60 ideal), 5-7 words/line, two lines, centre third, clear of top 20%/bottom 25% UI, animated phrase-by-phrase. 85%+ watch sound-off; captions carry the DfMA explanation.

## 7. MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK

Contract number with LOR is **cost-per-qualified-applicant + source-attributed application count**, not reach. UK constraint is qualified-applicant scarcity (~140 applications per grad vacancy already).

Channel benchmarks (platform-specific, never one blended CTR): LinkedIn CTR good >0.52% / excellent >0.7% (~£6.60-7.65/click); Meta CTR good >0.84% / excellent >1.10% (~£0.26-0.40/click); short-form 3-sec retention >60-65%; LinkedIn named-voice post 3+ first-hour commenters → ~5.2x reach.

Backward-planning: click-to-apply ~6.1%; session-to-application ~0.5%, ~60% of started applications abandoned (why register-interest must be low-friction). Register-interest: lead form ~6-13% (low intent) vs landing page ~3-5% (20-40% higher qualified rate) — **recommend a light landing page, not a lead form** for a 16-22 awareness audience.

Per phase: awareness = reach/retention/completion/saves; consideration = carousel save rate, landing-page conversion, CTR >35%; application = source-attributed count, cost-per-qualified-applicant.

2025 baseline: the only hard one carried is the handoff failure — **17 of 18 register-interest leads lost at the HWL-to-LOR boundary last cycle.** `[A] LOR's own CTR/CPC/application/cost-per-hire must come from LOR before targets can be set against baseline.`

Three failure-mode checks (named handoff owner, weekly): self-reported "where did you hear" is wrong ~80% of the time (cross-check only); tracking silently breaking at handoffs (re-audit after any change); lead-form vanity volume masking poor qualified conversion (measure cost-per-qualified-applicant).
