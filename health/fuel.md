# Fuel protocol: Birthday Run validated, RTTS 50K reference

**Status:** Validated Sun 3 May 2026 on the 26km Birthday Run. Plan executed clean, zero GI issues, fitness signal positive. This document is now the canonical fuel architecture for the build to RTTS 50K (Sat 11 July).

---

## What was validated on 3 May

- **51g/hr in-run rate** (145g over 2h44m run, pace 6:19/km avg).
- **Multi-source carb mix:** Maurten Drink Mix 160 + Maurten Gel 160 + SAP 40G + Maurten Gel 100 Caf.
- **40g unit dose** (first introduction): SAP 40G and Maurten Gel 160 both sat clean.
- **Maya 2× 500ml flask handoff at 1:00:** worked operationally, locks in for 50K aid station rehearsal.
- **Caffeine timing:** 80mg breakfast + 100mg Caf gel at 2:00 (peaks ~3:00 = finish).
- **Sodium ~525mg/hr** (cool May morning, moderate sweat at 86.9kg): no cramp signal.

GI: zero distress. Mild gas only ("sweaty farts") which is normal glucose-fructose absorption + trapped wind, not a flag.

## Pre-run protocol (validated, carries to 50K with race-week scaling)

### Saturday (day before)
- Carb-conscious eating, 5-7 g/kg across the day.
- Dinner ~19:00: ~120g carbs, low fibre. White rice + lean protein + cooked veg + honey/banana.
- 21:00: 500ml water + 1 scoop Marchon (750mg sodium pre-load).
- Bed 22:00.

### Sunday morning (working from 09:00 start)
- 06:30: wake. 250ml water + pinch salt.
- 06:45: breakfast. ~90g carbs (oats + banana + honey + toast + black coffee, ~80mg caffeine). 2h15m before start.
- 08:00: mix start flasks. 2× 250ml each with ½ scoop Marchon.
- 08:30: half banana + 100ml water.
- 08:55: final sip.

### Start carry
- 2× 250ml flasks: water + ½ scoop Marchon each (750mg sodium total in 500ml).
- Pocket: 1× SAP 40G (the 0:30 gel).
- Watch, vest, anti-chafe.

## Maya handoff (validated operational template)

**She brings, pre-mixed at home:**
- 1× 500ml soft flask: Drink Mix 160 (40g carbs)
- 1× 500ml soft flask: water + 1 scoop Marchon (750mg sodium)
- Maurten Gel 160
- Maurten Gel 100 Caf
- 1× SAP 40G (backup)
- Small towel, wet wipes

**Handoff procedure (~30-60 sec, walking):**
1. Slide both 500ml flasks into vest
2. Hand her your two empty 250ml flasks
3. Pocket the 3 gels
4. Move

For the 50K, this template scales to aid stations (refill flasks, grab one extra gel, walk through).

## Post-run (validated window)

### Within 30 min
- 500ml water + 1 scoop Marchon
- 30g protein + 80g carbs (Greek yogurt + granola + berries + 2 eggs on toast, OR whey shake + banana + 2 boiled eggs + 2 slices toast with jam)

### Within 2 hrs
- Proper meal: rice/potatoes + chicken/salmon + cooked veg
- Fluids until urine pale

## Gut training progression to RTTS 50K

The 26km at 51g/hr is W1 baseline. Each subsequent long run steps the rate by 5-10g/hr at the [Jeukendrup increment-rule limit](https://www.gssiweb.org/docs/default-source/sse-docs/jeukendrup_sse_178.pdf), with consolidation at each level.

| Week | Date | Long run | Target rate | Total CHO | New variable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **W1** | **3 May** | **26km** | **51g/hr ✓** | **145g ✓** | **40g unit dose, drink mix as fluid CHO. Validated.** |
| W2 | Sat 9 May | 16km | 50g/hr | ~80g | Hold. Recovery week. |
| W3 | Sat 16 May | 22km | 60g/hr | ~135g | First step up. 1 extra solid hit. |
| W4 | Sat 23 May | 26km | 65g/hr | ~180g | Hold rate, add race pace last 5km. |
| W5 (down) | Sat 30 May | 18km | 60g/hr | ~110g | Hold. |
| W6 | Sat 6 Jun | 28km | 70g/hr | ~215g | Step rate. Race pace last 6km. |
| W7 | Sat 13 Jun | 30km | 75g/hr | ~245g | Hold rate. Long sleeves on warm days. |
| W8 | Sat 20 Jun | 32km | 80g/hr | ~280g | Race target rate. Heat acclim block. |
| W9 | Sat 27 Jun | 22km @ race pace | 80g/hr | ~175g | Hold race rate at race pace. |
| Race | Sat 11 Jul | 50km | 80g/hr | ~440g | Trained. |

By race day: 7 sessions ≥60g/hr, 4 ≥75g/hr. Matches the 12-session adaptation target inside the 10-week build.

## Scaling to RTTS 50K, Sat 11 July

Same brand stack, same 30-45 min interval rhythm, scaled.

| Variable | Birthday Run (validated) | RTTS 50K |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | 26km | 50km |
| Time | 2h44m | 5h00-6h00 |
| Carb rate | 51g/hr | 80g/hr (gut trained by then) |
| Total carbs | 145g | ~440g |
| Caffeine | 180mg total | 300-400mg split into 3 doses (pre-race + ~2:00 + ~4:00) |
| Sodium pre-load | 750mg (1 scoop) Sat eve | 1500-2000mg (2-3 scoops) Fri eve |
| In-race sodium | ~525mg/hr | 500-700mg/hr (warmer July) |
| Hydration | 500ml start, 1L after handoff | Refill at every aid station, ~every 10km |
| Gels carried | 1 + Maya brings 2 + 1 backup | 8-10 + aid station gels |
| Pre-race carb load | None special | 8-10 g/kg Wed-Fri, low fibre from Thu lunch |

What carries from validation:
- Brand stack you trust
- 30-45 min interval rhythm anchored to elapsed time
- 2:1-ish multi-transportable carb mix
- Caffeine reserved for back third
- Maya handoff protocol = aid station handoff protocol

What you add for race day:
- Three-day carb load Wed-Fri (700-870 g/day)
- Heat acclim already banked W8-W10
- Pacing discipline: 6:25-6:35 first 10km
- Spare gels: carry 2 more than you plan to use
