## Role-lead 12-action base

Use this for building services, design management and procurement, adapting the proof item to the role.

1. Receive the real input.
2. Identify the live project item and revision.
3. Open or navigate the model, document, package or tracker.
4. Capture the cleared screen or document insert.
5. Point out the decision point.
6. Compare the real options.
7. Consult the relevant colleague.
8. Make or explain the decision.
9. Record, approve or update the output.
10. Communicate the outcome.
11. Show or identify the site consequence.
12. Hand the real output onward.

### P11 building services

- Real model, screen, system or site check.
- The decision and people involved.
- Where the consequence becomes visible on site.
- Incoming and outgoing project interfaces.

Building services stays protected. It is the least understood role and the hardest to fill.

### P13 design management

- Approved clash, revision or coordination issue.
- The resolution and who had to align.
- The delay, rework or uncertainty avoided.
- Incoming and outgoing project interfaces.

### P14 procurement

- Package brief, tracker or order with confidential prices removed.
- Cost, quality, lead-time or certainty trade-off.
- The decision and site consequence.
- Incoming and outgoing project interfaces.

## P10 continuity

- Match visit-one artefact, revision, sender, receiver, hand or screen direction, camera height, framing, PPE and action start/end.
- Capture wide, medium and tight.
- Capture the receiving side of every genuine interface.
- Use up to three takes with five-second handles only for rehearsed or non-transactional movement that can be repeated safely.
- Capture real approvals, submissions, sends and sign-offs once. Use safe cutaways before and after. Never create a duplicate live transaction for the camera.
- If the interface is digital, film the real workflow. Do not invent a physical baton.
- If the one-project test passes but the literal chain fails, collect four real decisions and their project consequences.
- If the one-project test fails, stop the current P10 claim and escalate the copy decision.

## Screen and privacy check

- Select the exact screen, document and revision during the pre-interview.
- Name the clearance owner.
- Close email, Teams, calendars and notifications. Use Do Not Disturb.
- Remove candidate data, personal details, supplier pricing, contact details, passwords, QR codes, security plans and access information.
- Film only the approved window or view.
- Test refresh, flicker and legibility before the contributor starts.
- Review the insert at full size before releasing the contributor.
- Capture a safe backup angle.

"We can blur it later" is not permission.
