# Demo Prompt, Final

Use this inside the clean workshop folder.

Before running:

- Close private folders.
- Keep `capture/`, `money/`, `council/`, `business/`, and client folders out of view.
- Use the clean content files only.

## Prompt

```text
Read these files first:

- content/strategy.md
- content/voice-dna.md
- content/publishing-rules.md
- content/waterfall.md

Then use them to run the content cascade below.

Idea of the fortnight:

A film I made just did 444,000 views. The next thing I posted reached almost no one. The big number is a vanity metric. The small number is the one that tells the truth. What I actually watch, and why.

Create five outputs:

1. A Substack essay draft, 700 to 900 words.
2. A cinematic essay script, 60 to 120 seconds.
3. A field-note reel script, 45 to 75 seconds.
4. A LinkedIn editorial post.
5. An X thread, 5 to 7 tweets.

Rules:

- Drafts, not finished posts.
- No em dashes.
- No engagement bait.
- No motivational fluff.
- No "I just started a business" angle.
- Keep it as field notes from real work.
- Make the central thesis clear: reach is not the same as trust.
- Use short sentences.
- Give me the strongest line from each output at the end.
```

## What To Say While It Runs

This is not impressive because the prompt is complicated. It is impressive if the output carries the context.

Watch for three things:

- Does it remember the voice?
- Does it preserve the strategy?
- Does it produce drafts worth editing?

## What To Say After It Runs

Do not pretend everything is perfect.

Say:

> This is where the founder comes back in. I would keep the useful lines, cut the generic lines, sharpen the first line, and then decide what is worth shipping.

The lesson:

> The engine gets me to a strong first draft in minutes instead of a blank page in hours.
