## Editorial spine

One argument, in the order the cut runs it:

1. Space feels fixed. It is one of the few things at work you can actually change, and you do not need a budget or a designer.
2. Three needs come before any design decision: agency, growth and connection.
3. Having a say over your surroundings is not soft. In the nursing-home study it tracked with survival, and small acts of control spill into the rest of life.
4. Your office is already sending a message. Cubicles versus corner offices, and the conference room nobody dares use, tell people how much control they really have.
5. The most damaging mistake is jumping to layout, paint and technology before the three needs.
6. Free moves: give the team the room and let them set it up, be intentional about the friendship factories, move the couch nobody sits on, and go somewhere new tomorrow.

## Why this cut

The public episode launched on the "three things every office steals from you" hook and the book's chapter tour. That framing is broad. The Better Moment needs one argument a leader can act on this week, and Cathal's own forward on 18 August (Dan Moscrop, "Your Office Doesn't Need More Amenities", with his note "would it be cool to do an episode from an office that really gets the office right for employees?") tells us which one: the office is a message about agency and connection, not a list of amenities.

Klotz's answer to Annette's question is that article in his own words. The nursing-home study and the cubicles-versus-corner-offices passage make it concrete. The friendship-factory exchange covers connection, so the cut proves two of the three needs directly; growth is named but not developed in this conversation, and the copy is written so it does not over-claim a three-part framework the cut does not teach.

The awe and Sydney Harbour Bridge material, the Heath brothers and Superquinn passage, the Airbnb rearranging story and the "nobody cares" closer are all good and all left out. They pull the mini-episode towards memoir and away from the office.
