### Edit notes (from SRT verification)

- **A→B splice is Cathal-to-Cathal mid-turn.** It reads as one continuous pivot on paper but the same voice will pop on a hard cut. Bridge with a short crossfade or room tone.
- **B→C splice is invisible.** C opens self-framed ("Now we ask all of our guests these final three questions") and Helen's 45:13 gremlins back-reference lands because B precedes it. No treatment needed.
- **The trimmed middle (23:55.44 to 26:26.80, Cathal's micromanager story + Helen's cult-of-a-manager warning, 2:31) does NOT fit back in.** Restoring it puts tape at 15:22 before the cold open. It's bench material for a short instead.
