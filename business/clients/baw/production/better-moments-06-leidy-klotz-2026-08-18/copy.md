## Teaser caption, Better at Work Instagram (Wednesday)

Go to the meeting room 20 minutes early.

That is the whole tip, and there is an experiment behind it. Two groups negotiate. One has been sitting in the room for 20 minutes first. The other walks in cold. The group with the home court gets the better outcome.

Leidy Klotz calls it feeling in control of spaces you cannot control. It costs nothing and it works for the interview, the pitch and the difficult conversation.

Your office is already telling people how much say they have. The question is whether it is saying what you meant.

## Notes for the review

- Book subtitle is as read on air by Cathal. Confirm against the listing before it goes into any description.
- "Start with agency, competence and connection" is Klotz's on-air wording in the biggest-mistake answer; the book and the rest of the conversation say growth. The copy uses growth throughout and leaves competence to the audio.
- The nursing-home figure is quoted as "50 per cent more likely to still be alive" at 18 months, and Klotz immediately caveats that the magnitude does not track across studies. The copy says "far more likely" rather than repeating the number as a headline stat.
- The couch and kitchen-chair lines are Cathal's, verified against Klotz's isolated mic. "Removing obstacles, that's definitely free" and "go someplace new" are Klotz's. The copy attributes accordingly.
