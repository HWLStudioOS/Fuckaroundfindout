# LOR Early Talent 2026 — Content Plan
## "More Than You Think" (corrected spine)

The hero is the breadth of careers and the people in varied roles across all four routes (graduate, degree apprentice, school-leaver apprentice, placement/intern) and five discipline families (engineering, digital/BIM/data, commercial/QS, design, business/finance). Manufacturing/DfMA appears nowhere as a pillar and at most as one faint supporting line, because LOR already shot and posted that for the separate Assembly Specialist campaign and it is spent ground here [R].

HWL owns top-of-funnel. LOR-internal owns application and hire. The only hard 2025 baseline carried is the handoff loss: 17 of 18 register-interest leads lost at the handoff [R]. Every tactic below is built to shrink that gap.

---

## 1. CONTENT PILLARS

The anchor is range made literal through people, not a careers grid. AtkinsRealis is already running the closest in-market version (five named profiles under #InGreatCompany), but as written profiles, not native video. The video lane is open, so these pillars are the engine to out-execute, not imitate [R].

**Pillar 1 — "The roles you didn't picture."** The unexpected desk, digital, data, design and commercial roles, led first to detonate the hi-vis cliche. Lean: graduate + placement, IG/TikTok-native. Job: Awareness, stops the scroll. Evidence: ~7% of 16-18s pick construction first, 71% of under-25s turned off; the gap is that the office/professional side is invisible [R]. Lead with the surprise, not a defence of the building site.

**Pillar 2 — "What the role actually is" (named DITL).** One real person per discipline, each with their own day-in-the-life. Breadth comes from the volume and variety of the cast (Berkeley/PLOY model). Lean: all four routes, all five disciplines. Job: Consideration, answers Emma's exact brief. Evidence: 73% of grads more likely to apply after employee video, 50% cite DITL, DITL candidates move ~2x faster; authentic employee-shot beats glossy hero films [R].

**Pillar 3 — "Who you'll work for" (named-manager content).** The hiring line manager on camera: what the role is, what good looks like, tips for succeeding. Nobody in the competitive set does this [R]. Lean: parents/gatekeepers + grads + degree apprentices, LinkedIn. Job: Consideration to handoff trust, candidate meets a named human before the LOR-internal step. Evidence: manager relationship is a top driver of offer acceptance; pairing each DITL with a manager companion directly attacks the 17-of-18 leak [R].

**Pillar 4 — "Find your route / what's your next move."** The light navigational spine that makes "more than you think" literal. A persona-match sorter + saveable route maps (grad / degree apprentice / school-leaver / placement). EY "navigable world" logic, kept low-cost. Lean: all routes, decision-point framing; school-leavers/degree apprentices skew parent-forwardable. Job: Consideration to sorting, converts undifferentiated breadth into a personal recommendation, bridges to Gradcracker/Bright Network. Evidence: EY Adventure Awaits won RAD 2024 Digital Experience for Early Talent, pathways was the most-engaged zone; carousels lead LinkedIn engagement ~7% and dominate saves [R].

**Pillar 5 — "Real projects, real impact" (status without the setting).** Make office/digital/commercial roles aspirational via scale, named landmark projects, decision-making power and visible impact, not by dressing up the desk. Show the actual tools on screen. Lean: grads + degree apprentices, LinkedIn + YouTube. Job: Consideration to conviction. Evidence: Gen Z weights meaning (80% meaningful work, 89% purpose) over prestige (37%); the tech is genuinely different (BIM/Revit/Navisworks, drone surveys, digital twins), showing it beats claiming it. The single place a faint DfMA texture could sit, one discipline among many, never the hero [A].

**Pillar 6 — "The myth-bust" (citable air cover, privatised).** The sector-level truth, claimed as LOR's own position. Lean: parents/gatekeepers + careers staff, LinkedIn. Job: Awareness/credibility for the adult decision-maker. Evidence: CIOB/CIC call for a national campaign showing design/surveying/PM/sustainability/procurement/finance roles; ~2/3 of 16-24s view construction positively but only ~1/3 would consider it; no employer brand has claimed this myth-bust [R].

## 2. FORMAT PLAYBOOK PER CHANNEL

Hooks are sub-10-word, contrarian / numbers-led / curiosity-gap (~2.3x the field) [R].

**LinkedIn (led: grads, degree apprentices, parents/gatekeepers).** ~2-4 named-voice posts/week, Tue-Thu 08:00-10:00. Never reshare from the company page; post natively from named employees and managers (~561% more reach, ~5x engagement vs company page). Golden hour: 30-40 person network COMMENTS in the first 30-60 min (3+ first-hour commenters ~5.2x reach), with a comment kit + Teams trigger; advocacy decays ~70% in six weeks without a system. Document carousels for saveable route maps + discipline families. Judge video on completion + first-hour comments, not views. Hooks: "She didn't think this was a construction job." / "15 disciplines. One graduate scheme. Most people name two." / "Your manager is the question nobody asks. Here's mine." / "Four routes in. Most parents have heard of one."

**Instagram / Reels (led: grad + placement discovery, + Meta retargeting).** 76% of grads use IG for career content vs 34% LinkedIn. 15-45s, cut every 2-4s, 60%+ completion, 3-sec hook decides everything. Burned-in captions 48px+, 5-7 words/line, centre third; 85%+ watch sound-off. Hooks: "POV: it's 9am and you're rotating a £400m building." / "I'm a designer. At a construction company. Yes, really." / "Day one vs the bit nobody warned me about."

**TikTok-organic (warm-up only, no paid).** No contractor runs native vertical DITL well here; 46% of Gen Z report securing a job via TikTok. Raw, employee-shot, "no two days are the same"; authenticity is the lever, not gloss. Hooks: "Things I didn't know existed until I joined." / "What's your next move? Take the route quiz." / "A QS explains what a QS actually does, in 30 seconds."

**YouTube (long-form DITL home).** Horizontal 1-3 min cut of each named DITL as the "what it looks like when you get here" library, each ending on the route's apply path. Civil Service Fast Stream "one host, many roles" supercut is the breadth-in-one-watch template (18,056 views in <48h; 3,200+ extra target applications). Titles: "A real day at LOR: the digital grad" / "Every early-careers route at LOR, in one day."

## 3. PERSONA / ROUTE ANGLES

Segment by decision-maker, not just age: grads and placements self-serve (IG/TikTok, review-led); school-leavers and degree apprentices are gatekeeper-mediated (parents #1 at 42%, careers staff 28%, teachers 25%) [R].

| Route | Discipline lean | Channel weight | Hook refraction | Decision-maker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graduate | eng, digital/BIM, commercial/QS, design, business | IG/Reels-led, LinkedIn credibility, YouTube home | "I didn't realise you could do THIS here" | self-serves |
| Degree apprentice | eng, digital/data, commercial | LinkedIn-led (parent + candidate) | "Earn, study, build something real, no student debt" | candidate + parent |
| School-leaver apprentice | mixed functions incl. business/digital/commercial (not only trade) | TikTok/Reels warm-up, LinkedIn parent-facing | "What's your next move at 18?" + forwardable | parent/teacher decisive |
| Placement / intern | digital, QS, design, eng | IG-led, RateMyPlacement/Higherin proof | "The year that turns into a job offer" | candidate, review-driven |

Discipline spread is enforced at casting: every route has at least one non-engineering face so breadth is visible inside each route, not only across them. Open the public series on the unexpected disciplines (digital/BIM, commercial/QS, design); let engineering and the apprenticeships sit inside a visibly broad set. Placement is a deliberate pipeline (cheapest long-run grad channel, re-hired into grad roles); seed LOR placement reviews on Higherin toward the student-voted rankings.

## 6. PRODUCTION CAPTURE LIST (~4 shoot days)

Spread across disciplines and locations, not a factory day. Volume of authentic per-person footage over a single polished anchor. Build a proprietary varied real-role footage/stills bank (the This is Engineering library, 5.7M downloads, displaced the stereotype this way).

- **Day 1 — Office / digital + design studio:** digital/BIM grad on a live Revit/Navisworks model (Pillar 5 evidence); designer making a real call; business/finance grad. Desk roles shot for agency, not glamour. 3 DITL protagonists + tool detail.
- **Day 2 — Commercial / project rooms:** QS trainee building a cost plan; degree apprentice (parent-forwardable cut); manager interviews #1-2.
- **Day 3 — Site / engineering (modern, not muddy):** graduate engineer on a named landmark; drone-survey / digital-twin link shot as texture not theme; school-leaver apprentice in a non-trade function; manager interviews #3-4.
- **Day 4 — Placement + pickups + fair-ready capture:** placement DITL; pickups for the discipline-families carousel and the supercut; modular fair clips + talking-head capture for the golden-hour system.

Cross-day rule: tag every clip by pillar + persona at capture. No DfMA/factory day; if modern-methods appears, one faint shot inside the digital/design material, never its own day.

## 7. MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK

Contract number is cost-per-qualified-applicant + source-attributed application count, not reach.

Benchmarks: LinkedIn CTR good >0.52% / excellent >0.7% (~£6.60-7.65/click); Meta CTR good >0.84% / excellent >1.10% (~£0.26-0.40/click); click-to-apply ~6.1%; landing page 3-5% (higher qualified than lead form); short-form 60%+ completion; LinkedIn video on completion + first-hour comments (3+ ~5.2x reach), not views.

Per phase: warm-up (Jun-Aug) = completion, saves, first-hour comments, sorter completions, hub visits, CTR vs benchmark, no application target yet; open (Sept) = source-attributed applications, cost-per-qualified-applicant, click-to-apply, webinar-attendee-to-apply; welcome (Oct) = offer-acceptance + handoff completion rate (the direct counter to 17-of-18).

Per persona/route: applications by route and discipline, so Emma can dial UP hard-to-fill engineers and DOWN over-recruited roles via targeting. Per channel: LinkedIn on completion + comments + attribution; IG/Meta on CTR + click-to-apply + cost-per-applicant; TikTok organic on completion + hub referral; YouTube on watch-through + apply-path clicks.

Baseline: the only hard 2025 carry is 17 of 18 leads lost at the handoff. Primary v1 success test: the handoff completion rate moves materially off that floor, instrumented end-to-end.
