# Creepers Content Strategy — Kallaway Sharpening Pass

> Revision of 22 June 2026, written from Dublin. Builds on `creepers-content-strategy-2026-06-09.md` and `creepers-content-plan-2026-06.md`. Cuts the grid from ~6/week to a hard 4/week ceiling. Applies Kane Kallaway's hook/structure framework. Not yet client-shared — internal for Harrison's review.

## 1. The cut and the why

The previous calendar carried 73 posts over 13 weeks (~5.6/week). The grid had crept past what the existing strategy says is sustainable without inflating production: "Without David carrying derivatives, the realistic sustainable grid is 2x nursery + 1x install, not 5 posts plus daily Stories." We have David, so 5 was technically survivable, but it was diluting the hook engineering on every post and pushing too much atmosphere into a feed that's meant to be sales-supporting first.

**New ceiling: 4 grid posts per week. 52 posts over 13 weeks.**

This is on-thesis with Kallaway: "Each post thoughtfully crafted. 80% of your effort on the opening." He averages 1.7 videos per week on his own channels. A 4/week tightly-hooked grid will outperform a 6/week mixed one, especially in trade audiences who reward authority over volume.

It is also closer to what was actually agreed with Anna and Sarah in the June plan: 3 @nursery + 1 @install ramping to 2-3 @install by August. We had over-built. This brings it back.

## 2. The Kallaway frame, applied

Kallaway's load-bearing claims and how each one routes into Creepers work:

| Kallaway | Application |
|---|---|
| Hook = visual + spoken + text overlay, layered, first 3 seconds. 80% of effort. | Every Reel ships with all three. Visual = motion or scroll-stop frame. Spoken = curiosity-opening first line (not Michael's name and title). Text = bold overlay of the claim, not the caption. |
| Six hook archetypes: Fortuneteller, Experimenter, Teacher, Magician, Investigator, Contrarian. | Every post tagged with the archetype it leans on. Stop posting without naming one. |
| Hook → dance → end-hook. Curiosity loops re-opened mid-piece. | Reels over 20s include a re-hook at the ~40-50% mark. Not "and here's the next thing", an actual mental itch re-opened. |
| Uncommon idea on a large audience. | Mine RHS journals, ISA arboriculture papers, trade-press, designer interviews for one uncommon fact per week. Repackage for the largest applicable audience (designer-curious end clients, not just hardcore trade). |
| Content as infrastructure, not art. | The 4/week grid is a repeatable workflow. Sales nomination feeds Wed Fresh In. Fortnightly Surrey feeds Mon + Wed + Fri. Monthly NF feeds Tue. Nothing on the grid is "we'll see what we get." |
| Native rhythm per platform. | LinkedIn comes off the IG weekly grid (it was misshaped for the IG cadence). Becomes fortnightly batched pilots, sized to LinkedIn's rhythm. |

The two accounts inherit different archetype mixes:

- **@creepersnursery (trade):** Teacher + Investigator weighted. Case-study specimens going into named gardens. Insider sourcing reveals. "Most designers don't know X about Y" framing. Trade buyers reward authority and specificity.
- **@creepersinstallation (HNW):** Magician + Experimenter weighted. Silent visual scroll-stops. Crane lifts, time-lapses, before/afters with text-overlay hooks. The page is restraint as aesthetic — Magician archetype fits this exactly.

## 3. The 4-a-week shape

| Day | Account | Series | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | @creepersnursery | Monday Minute / People / Proof | Authority anchor (Teacher / Investigator) |
| Tue | @creepersinstallation | The Install / Detail & Texture / Garden Stories | Visual hook (Magician / Experimenter) |
| Wed | @creepersnursery | Stock & Season — Fresh In | Sales spine (non-negotiable, fed live) |
| Fri | @creepersnursery | The Working Nursery / Proof | Trade rhythm (proven Order-Out format) |

**Default ratio: 3 nursery + 1 install per week.**

**Install ramp:** From W7 (w/c 27 July), @install bumps to 2/week on weeks where there is strong material — hero film, permissioned project, Cost & Value pin, Designers We Supply repost. The bump comes from the Mon @nursery slot (becomes a second @install) on those weeks, not from adding a fifth grid post. Ceiling stays at 4.

This matches the agreed brief: "1/week now, ramping to 2-3/week by August."

**LinkedIn:** off the weekly grid. Treat as a fortnightly pilot batch. Six pilot posts over 13 weeks (Pilot #1-6), each one earned by a hero or repurposable moment. Posted on a Wednesday, not part of the 4-count. Status visible in the calendar but tagged "Pilot, off-grid."

## 4. What got cut, and why

| Cut | Why |

| All weekly LinkedIn pilots (was 11 posts) | Misshaped for IG cadence. LinkedIn rewards different rhythm and length. Moves to fortnightly batched pilots, 6 total over the quarter. |

| "Tagged for a project" (W3 Thu) | Atmosphere, no payload. The quiet flex doesn't carry without specificity. |
| "From our fields to a garden" (W1 Thu) | Duplicates Monday's NF drone Reel within the same week. |
| "S1 macro set" (W2 Thu, already deleted) | Duplicate of W1 Tue carousel. |
| "Bark, leaf, steel, soil" / repeat macro sets | Detail & Texture became wallpaper. Keep two macro carousels in the quarter, not seven. |

The 21 posts cut break down: 11 LinkedIn (moved off-grid to 6 fortnightly batch), 8 weak install/atmosphere doubles, 2 already-deleted by Harrison on 22 June.

## 5. What got sharpened

Every kept post now carries:

1. **Archetype tag** (one of the six)
2. **Visual hook idea** (first frame, motion or scroll-stop)
3. **Spoken hook idea** (the first sentence)
4. **Text overlay** (the bold claim that the post pays off)
5. **Re-hook beat** (for Reels over 20s)

These five live in the production notes for each post (separate file, not in the client-facing calendar — the calendar shows the result, not the engineering).

## 6. Cadence by account, summary

- **@creepersnursery:** 3 posts/week — Mon, Wed, Fri. Mon falls to Tue (= becomes @install) on install-ramp weeks.
- **@creepersinstallation:** 1/week (W1-W6), then 2/week (W7-W13). 19 install posts total over 13 weeks.

Final tally: 33 @nursery + 19 @install + 6 LinkedIn pilots = **52 grid posts + 6 LinkedIn = 58 across the quarter.** Down from 73.
