## Power State

- He is productive.
- He has exercised.
- He has abstained from masturbation for a while.
- He has eaten well.
- He has slept well.
- He has had great conversations.

- Power state is not just achievement. It is clean inputs, movement, connection, discipline, meaningful work, and self-respect.

Likely shame pattern:

- Wake late.

- Doom scroll.
- Procrastinate.
- Eat poorly.
- Avoid work.

- Self-attack.
