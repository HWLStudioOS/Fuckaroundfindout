## Fact base

- Show is the inaugural RHS Sandringham Flower Show, Wed 22 to Sun 26 July 2026, 10am to 6pm, Sunday to 5pm. https://www.rhs.org.uk/shows-events/rhs-sandringham-flower-show
- RHS Royal Legacy Garden: designed by Catherine MacDonald in collaboration with His Majesty The King, landscaper Gadd Brothers, plant providers Creepers and Deepdale Trees. Royal Feature Garden, unjudged. https://gardensdb.com/gardens/the-rhs-royal-legacy-garden
- RHS Pollywiggle Garden: designed by Joe and Laura Carey of Carey Garden Design Studio, landscaper Neil Askew Landscape, plant providers Creepers and Katie's Garden Plant Centre. Part of RHS Bringing Nature Home. Pollywiggle is an old Norfolk word for tadpole. Moves to Pensthorpe Nature Reserve, Norfolk, after the show as a wildlife education garden. https://gardensdb.com/gardens/the-rhs-pollywiggle-garden
- Medals: six Gold, 11 Silver-gilt, five Silver, three Bronze across all categories, announced 22 July. Neither Creepers-supplied garden appears in the medal list. Best Show Garden and Best Construction went to the RNIB Talking Books Garden, Paul Hervey-Brookes. https://www.prolandscapermagazine.com/2026/07/22/rhs-sandringham-flower-show-2026-medals-revealed/
- Careys are Norfolk-based and came to Sandringham from a Gold at Chelsea 2026 on the Addleshaw Goddard "Flourish in the City" garden, which Creepers also supplied. Left out of this caption deliberately, it drags the post to Chelsea. Repo records the Chelsea award as "Gold plus best in category", press coverage says "Best in Show", so the exact wording needs checking before it is used anywhere. `campaigns/creepers-chelsea-hero-film.md`
