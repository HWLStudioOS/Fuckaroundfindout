Chosen on the things a photograph does not show.

A specimen tree is bought on a picture and a price more often than it should be. The ones that establish well were read in the row first. The unions where the main stems meet. The spacing and balance of the crown. Whether the stem stands on its own without leaning on its cane. And the rootball, firm, fibrous and in proportion to the crown, because that balance is what decides how a tree settles after lifting.

None of it is quick. A tree grown with room and time shows it in the wood.
