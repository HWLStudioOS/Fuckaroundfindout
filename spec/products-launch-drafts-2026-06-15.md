# Launch drafts for the two products

Two assets, two surfaces, kept apart on purpose. The Diagnosis goes on the buyer surface
(LinkedIn + studio bio). The Content Engine goes on the peer surface (Year One Slack).
Never the two muddied. Post the Diagnosis only after `legibility-diagnosis` is on prod and
the bio link points at `hwlstudio.com/#diagnosis`. Post the Content Engine after it is
deployed and you have its URL.

## 1. Legibility Diagnosis, LinkedIn (buyer surface)

Field-notes POV, not "take my quiz". Link goes in the first comment, never the body.

Post body:

```
Most serious organisations are illegible to their own market.

The work is real. The senior team knows it. The market sees a different company than the one inside the building.

You notice it from inside the work. A business doing world-class things, whose own people describe it three different ways when you ask. The story never resolves into one story, so every new buyer, hire and partner starts from zero.

It is rarely a content problem. It is a legibility problem. A frame, written down, that survives Monday morning.

So I built a short diagnosis for it. Five dimensions, twelve questions, three minutes. It reads how legible your organisation actually is and names the gap if there is one. No pitch attached. Most who run it will not need us, and it says so.

If your organisation is doing better work than the market gives it credit for, it is worth three minutes.
```

First comment (the link):

```

```
