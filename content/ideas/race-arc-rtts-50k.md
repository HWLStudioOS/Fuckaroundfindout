## Piece 2: RACE DAY CAPTURE (Sat 11 July, not a post)

This feeds Piece 3. If nobody shoots it, there is no film. Sort the shooter before Friday: Maya, Jackson, or Mackenzie at start or finish, phone is fine.

**Shot list, in priority order:**

1. **Pre-dawn kit.** Friday night or Saturday 4am: race number, taped feet, gels laid out, coffee. Static shots, low light. 2 minutes of effort.
2. **The start.** Crowd in the half-light, the first climb onto the Ridgeway. Ask whoever drops you off to film you disappearing into the field of runners.
3. **Self-capture mid-race.** Phone stays on you. Three to five 10-second clips across the day: the trail empty in both directions, an aid station, the watch face at 25k and 40k. Plus **voice memos**: one at halfway, one at the low point, one in the last 5k. Say whatever is true. These become the voiceover material and they cannot be recreated afterwards.
4. **The finish at Avebury.** Whoever is there films the last 200 metres and the stones. Do not pose. Whatever state arrives is the state that gets filmed.
5. **After.** Feet, food, face. One minute.

**The Triumph and Disaster rule:** the film gets made whatever happens. Finish strong, finish broken, DNF at 38k. Kipling's two impostors get treated just the same, that IS the piece. There is no result that cancels the shoot.

Garmin pulls (splits, HR, elevation) can overlay later. The data is captured automatically, ignore it on the day.
