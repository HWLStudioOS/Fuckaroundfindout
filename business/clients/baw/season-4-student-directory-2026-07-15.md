# Better@Work Season 4 student directory

**Prepared:** 15 July 2026  
**Audience:** Final-year and master's students  
**Format:** Four-page A4 guide, designed as a single distributable PDF  
**Source:** Public Better@Work Acast feed and YouTube channel, checked 15 July 2026

## Editorial approach

The first page gives students six useful starting points. The remaining three pages carry the complete Season 4 release directory in chronological order. Each entry names the topic, speaker and a direct public link.

Thirty-three releases have a full YouTube video. The Marcus Collins interview was not published as a full video on the Better@Work YouTube channel, so its row links to the canonical podcast episode instead.

## Design direction

- Working route: **Work, Annotated**.
- A4 portrait, four pages.
- Off-white and near-black base, yellow as a signal colour, controlled deep violet, one warm annotation colour.
- Instrument Serif for display ideas, Space Grotesk for labels and body copy.
- Capsule labels, editorial numbering and lightweight annotation devices.
- No university branding until the receiving institution is confirmed.
