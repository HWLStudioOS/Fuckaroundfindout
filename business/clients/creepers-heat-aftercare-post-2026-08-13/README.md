## Copy basis

The advice is deliberately narrow: newly planted trees and shrubs are the first watering priority in dry heat; water slowly at the base so moisture reaches the rootball; early morning or evening is preferable; check below the surface rather than relying on a light sprinkle. This was checked against the RHS watering and heatwave guidance.
