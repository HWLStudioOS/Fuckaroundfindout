# Research Queue

These are external research topics needed to pressure-test Harrison's strategy.

## Initial Evidence Anchors

### B2B Content And AI, 2026

- Content Marketing Institute's 2026 B2B research reports that its survey drew 1,229 global responses, with 1,015 B2B marketers reported in the article.
- CMI frames the strongest teams as building marketing fundamentals first, then using AI to add creative and operational leverage.
- CMI reports that 45% of B2B marketers included AI-powered marketing tools among their top three areas for increased 2026 investment.
- CMI reports AI-assisted content has improved content quality for 58% of respondents and content performance for 39%.
- Source: https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/b2b-research/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research

### Search, LLM Visibility, And Content Budgets

- Clutch and Conductor's February 2026 release says 87% of content marketers plan to increase content budgets in 2026.
- It also reports that one in four content marketers now say LLMs are the primary audience for most of their content.
- Source: https://clutch.co/press-releases/state-of-content-marketing-ai-survey

### AI As Baseline

- HubSpot's 2026 State of Marketing page frames AI as "the baseline, not the differentiator."
- It reports that 61% of marketers believe marketing is undergoing its biggest disruption in 20 years due to AI.
- It reports that 80% of marketers use AI for content creation and 75% use it for media production.
- Strategic implication for Harrison: selling "AI content" alone is probably too generic. The stronger offer is likely AI-enabled marketing operations, governance, creative direction, and measurable content systems.
- Source: https://www.hubspot.com/state-of-marketing

## AI Operations For Marketing

- What is the difference between a GBP 5,000 audit and a GBP 45,000 transformation engagement?
- What deliverables make an AI ops engagement feel concrete and board-safe?

## Audience-Led Services

- Who is successfully using a small expert audience to sell high-ticket services?

- Which audience segments would plausibly buy from Harrison?

## Product Ladder

- What can be built from existing client work without breaching confidentiality?

## Delegation And Operations

- What weekly cadence would allow Harrison to work months ahead?
