## Recall protocol per book

Same shape for every book on the list. The book is not finished until all four steps are done.

1. **During reading:** Fold corners (existing habit) and write a one-line note in `learning/library.md` under "Active reads" with current page and current best quote.
2. **On finishing:** Seed 10-15 drill items into `learning/recall-queue.md` using the existing format. Each item has question, answer, where-to-use hook.
3. **Within one week of finishing:** Voice memo, 3 minutes, teaching the thesis to camera (or audio only). The memo lives in `learning/voice-memos/` (create when first needed) or transcribed inline in `learning/library.md`.
4. **One-page synthesis:** Thesis in two sentences, three quotes, three applications (where this changes a decision Harrison is currently making). Lives at the bottom of the entry in `learning/library.md`.

## Failure modes

- Reading without recall. The codex named this. Folding corners is not enough.
- Sprinting through the short books and stalling on the long ones. Stalin and Life of Greece are 70% of the page count.
- Treating this as casual hobby reading. Rory Stewart recall is the bar. He can quote Herodotus and Gibbon while walking through Afghanistan. If after 12 months Harrison can't quote Durant while walking the dog, the system failed.
- Skipping the synthesis step. The synthesis is where the book turns into compounding capability.
