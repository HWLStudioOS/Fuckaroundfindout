# LOR Retainer Options

**Status:** proposal on the table at LOR, awaiting board response. No chase before mid-May. See `campaigns/lor-retainer.md` for live state.

Three tiers, each tied to the Augmented Strategic Content Engine flagship. All assume LOR-specific scoping; the same three-tier model can be reused for any future client.

## Tier 1: Strategic advisement, £8,500/month

- Monthly or bi-weekly calls.
- Strategic content/comms advisement.
- Content ideas and direction.
- Guidance on what to try and why.
- Light review of implementation.
- No production support.

**For:** organizations with an internal comms/marketing team capable of executing, who need senior strategic direction.

## Tier 2: Middle option, £11,000/month

- Everything in Tier 1.
- Plus: light implementation review.
- Periodic creative direction on key pieces.
- Workshop facilitation when needed.
- Light governance support.

**For:** organizations with execution capability but uncertain quality or rhythm.

## Tier 3: Fractional Head of Content, £15,000/month

- Strategic advisory.
- Active implementation.
- Production support.
- Managing content priorities.
- Briefing production.
- Reviewing output.
- Helping the comms team operate the strategy.

**For:** organizations where Harrison is operationally embedded as the strategic content/comms lead, working alongside their team.

## Decision dependency at LOR

- Board review of the February 2032 strategy work.
- Board review of the social media strategy from the workshop.
- Corporate decision-making speed.

LOR may prefer the comms retainer structure with internal cost-recovery from project leads. Project leads already reach out to Harrison directly, which suggests internal demand without a formal retainer. This is a feature; the retainer makes it predictable.

## When to use this offer model for other clients

Reusable for any future client where the engagement is "we're going to ship serious content/comms work and need senior leadership." Adjust the tier-2 and tier-3 rates to client scale. The structure (advisory / middle / fractional head) is the template.
