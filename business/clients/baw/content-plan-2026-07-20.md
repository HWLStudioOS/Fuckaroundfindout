## Better Moments #2 shape

Working title:

**The four questions that separate strategy from planning | Better Moments #2 | Roger Martin**

Target runtime: 12-14 minutes.

The cut should follow one argument:

1. Strategy is what you do, not what you say.
2. Planning deals in initiatives. Strategy deals in choices.
3. "What would have to be true?" turns debate into a test.
4. AI gives the modal answer, not the distinctive one.
5. Start with the hardest choice, not the tenth task.

Do not include the longer acquisition failure story. It is excellent, but it pulls the mini-episode away from the practical spine and pushes the runtime over the Helen benchmark.

Hero social clip: **AI is a mode-seeking device**, tight 75-second cut from the existing Roger launch pack.
