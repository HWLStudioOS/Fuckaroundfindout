# Content Strategy

**Status:** open. Per codex Hypothesis 10, the public voice should not be forced into a single niche yet.

## The audience question

Codex says honestly: Harrison doesn't know exactly who his future audience is.

Candidates from codex Round 2 + 7:
- Founders.
- Marketers.
- Corporate comms leaders.
- Agencies.
- Creators.
- Like-minded people interested in tech, AI systems, fitness, gaming, learning, history, consumer products.

**Operating rule:** the commercial wedge can be narrow (B2B comms / AI-augmented content systems) while the personal voice stays broad. Don't force a single niche before reps prove which one converts.

## The status risk (codex H8)

Harrison fears that "I just started a business 6 months ago" content punctures the mature, competent perception that gets him into LOR-grade rooms.

**Resolution:** don't do "I just started" content. Build public authority around observed patterns, field notes, systems, and sober analysis from client work, without overstating tenure or exposing confidential details.

## Voice ingredients (from codex 08)

- Field notes rather than guru advice.
- Frontier curiosity without hype.
- Personal performance with humility.
- AI systems through the lens of real work.
- Builder energy, not influencer theatre.
- Kiwi warmth and directness.
- Taste-led content and culture.
- Love-hate honesty about social media.

## Safe content pillars

- AI / tech / marketing / social interpreted for practical operators.
- Systems building, how Harrison is using AI to work better.
- Lessons from content engines and client delivery (no confidential names).
- Fitness and personal performance.
- Philosophy, Stoicism, pursuit of better.
- Reading notes and applied learning.

## What we explicitly aren't building right now

- A "Lenny of comms" newsletter (the synthesis suggested this; codex H10 says don't force the niche yet).
- A faceless YouTube channel.
- Engagement-bait LinkedIn posts.
- Generic AI marketing content.
- "Build in public from day 1" content (status risk).

## When to commit to a single audience

After two of:
- Three consecutive months of weekly posting with measurable engagement.
- A specific buyer cluster (e.g., heads of corporate comms in legacy industries) showing repeated engagement / DM patterns.
- A specific product (e.g., the Blueprint productisation) generating inbound from a specific audience type.

Until then, the public voice ranges across the codex pillars. The next 30 days are a low-stakes test, not a commitment.

## First real test

Once Phase 4 ships (three agents wired), one of the next 30-day moves is a 2-week MVP of one content product. Format and topic deferred until that decision point.
