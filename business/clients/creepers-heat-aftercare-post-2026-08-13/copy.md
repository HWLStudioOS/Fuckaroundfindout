# Caption

37°C today. Start with anything planted this year.

Its roots may still be held close to the original rootball. A light surface sprinkle will not reach the part that matters.

Water slowly and deeply at the base, early or once the heat has eased this evening. Check the rootball itself is moist below the surface. Prioritise large specimens, containers and spring planting.

Aftercare is part of the installation.
