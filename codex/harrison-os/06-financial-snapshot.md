## VAT Watch

- VAT status as of 2026-05-11: not VAT registered.
- Harrison reports revenue around GBP 75,000 and expects to approach GBP 90,000 soon.
- Current GOV.UK guidance says a business must register for VAT if total VAT-taxable turnover for the last 12 months goes over GBP 90,000, or if it expects VAT-taxable turnover to go over GBP 90,000 in the next 30 days.
- GOV.UK also says VAT cannot be shown on invoices until the VAT registration number is received, though prices can be increased to account for VAT that will need to be paid to HMRC.
- Sources:
  - https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat
  - https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat/how-register-for-vat
