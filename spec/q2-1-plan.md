The cross-cutting pattern: **newsletter or column at weekly cadence + book or long essay anthology as durable artefact**, not LinkedIn-first. LinkedIn for distribution, owned channel for compounding. Worth considering whether the field-notes essay graduates to a recurring cadence by end Q3.
