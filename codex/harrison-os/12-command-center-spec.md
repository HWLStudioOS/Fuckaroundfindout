# Harrison Command Center Spec

Date started: 2026-05-11.

This is the first working spec for a single screen that helps Harrison operate with less mental drag.

## Purpose

The command center should answer:

- What matters today?
- What is late or at risk?
- How much cash is real?
- What is the next high-leverage move?
- Am I moving toward the man I say I want to become?

## First Screen Modules

### 1. Today

- Calendar.
- Top three outcomes.
- Training plan.
- Client deadlines.
- One personal priority.

### 2. Money

- Business bank balance.
- Personal cash.
- Credit card balances.
- Accounts receivable.
- Tax buffer.
- Next 14 days cash in/out.
- Late invoice follow-up status.

### 3. Clients

- Better At Work next deliverable.
- Creepers next deliverable.
- Laing O'Rourke next action.
- Open proposals.
- Unblocked / blocked / waiting status.
- Editor-ready task list.
- VA/ops-ready follow-up list.
- Content inventory by client.

### 4. Content

- This week's post.
- Idea inbox.
- Draft queue.
- Published log.
- Proof/case-study assets to collect.
- Delegation queue for clips, carousels, captions, and scheduling.

### 5. Learning

- Weekly research brief.
- Books/podcasts/articles queue.
- One idea to apply this week.
- Notes to convert into content or client systems.

### 6. Health

- Sleep.
- Training.
- Recovery.
- Weight/body composition trend.
- 50 km race countdown.
- Morning movement check.

### 7. Relationship / Life

- Maya check-in.
- Family/friends touchpoints.
- Admin/life tasks.
- Proposal/future-family long-term thread, handled privately and intentionally.
- Out-of-house work/session plan.
- New Zealand / future home long-term thread.

### 8. Attention

- Instagram time.
- Screen-time trend.
- Deep work blocks.
- Doom-scroll trigger notes.
- Serendipity/exposure blocks: coffee shop, Soho House, meetings, shoots, walks.

## Weekly Review

The weekly review should include:

- Money.
- Health.
- Client work.
- Content.
- Relationship.
- Learning.
- Calendar.

## Codex Reminder Candidates

Harrison says he would trust Codex to remind him about almost everything weekly.

Possible reminders:

- Reconcile cash and card charges.
- Follow up late invoices.
- Check VAT/tax buffer.
- Review client delivery status.
- Choose and publish one post.
- Review training and sleep.
- Plan learning inputs.
- Do a relationship/life check-in.

## Research Candidates

Harrison says he would trust Codex to research broadly, even daily.

Potential weekly research streams:

- AI and marketing operations.
- B2B content engines.
- Corporate comms and social strategy.
- LLM search / GEO / AI visibility.
- Tools for solo operators and small agencies.
- Fitness/endurance performance.
- Books, podcasts, and essays aligned to current goals.
- Audience-building examples that do not undermine corporate credibility.

## Automation Boundaries

Harrison jokingly said there is nothing he would not want automated and "automate it all."

Working safety rule:

- Automate collection, reminders, research, summaries, drafts, and checklists aggressively.
- Keep final approval for payments, client messages, public posts, legal/tax decisions, and major relationship communications with Harrison.

## First Build Principle

Start small:

1. One capture inbox.
2. One weekly review.
3. One money dashboard.
4. One client delivery dashboard.
5. One content pipeline.

Do not build a cathedral before the capture habit exists.
