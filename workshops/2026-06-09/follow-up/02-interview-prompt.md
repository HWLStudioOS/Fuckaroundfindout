# The Interview Prompt

This is the most important thing in the pack. Do not skip it to get to the content prompts. The content prompts only work because of what this one builds.

The idea: instead of you trying to explain your business to an AI in one message, you make the AI interview you. It asks, you answer, and at the end it writes your context files for you. I built my own system this way. The interview is the work. Everything else is downstream of it.

Paste this into any AI you use. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, all fine. If you set up a Project (Path A in doc 01), run it in there. If you installed Claude Code or Codex (Path B), run it in your empty folder and it will create the files itself.

## The prompt

```
I run a small business and I want to set up a content system. We will do this in two steps. Do not skip ahead.

STEP 1. INTERVIEW ME.

Ask me questions one at a time. Wait for my answer before asking the next. Push back when my answers are vague or generic. Use plain language, no marketing jargon.

Cover, in whatever order makes sense:

- What my business actually does, who pays me, and what they pay for
- Who I want my content to reach, and what I want those people to do after seeing it
- The real proof I have: projects, numbers, client stories, things I have actually done
- How I talk. Ask me to describe a recent piece of work out loud, then study the way I said it
- Phrases and words I would never use
- What I refuse to post about, and what tone I refuse to take
- What good content in my industry looks like to me, with examples if I have them
- How many hours a week I can honestly give this

Keep going until you genuinely understand my business and my voice. Expect 15 to 25 questions. Quality of understanding matters more than speed.

STEP 2. BUILD MY FILES.

When the interview is done, research how the best AI content systems structure their context files. Then write me four markdown files, complete and ready to save:

1. strategy.md. Who I am, who the content is for, what it should achieve, my content pillars, and what success looks like in 90 days.

2. voice-dna.md. How I write and speak. Sentence rhythm, openers and closers I actually use, words and phrases I never use, with examples pulled from my interview answers.

3. publishing-rules.md. My hard rules. Topics that are off limits, tones I refuse, formats I will and will not make, and any line I never want to cross. Written as rules an AI must obey, not suggestions.

4. waterfall.md. My repurposing system. How one idea per fortnight becomes multiple outputs: a longform piece, a short video script, a talking-head script, a LinkedIn post, a thread. Adapted to the platforms and the hours I told you about.

Write each file in full. No placeholders. If something is thin because my answers were thin, tell me and ask the follow-up question instead of padding it.
```

## Two notes

Answer like you talk. If you polish your answers, your voice file will sound polished and fake. Dictate your answers if you can (see Wispr Flow in doc 06), because spoken answers carry your real voice.

Running two businesses? Run the interview twice and keep two separate folders or projects. One voice per system. Do not blend them.
