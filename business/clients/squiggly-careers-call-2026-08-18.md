## The commercial frame, with the real numbers

From Helen's 24 July email (confidential, keep off any Podcast House channel):

- 4 x Borrowed Brilliance edits (~30 min): £1,200 a month
- 4 x Squiggly Shortcuts edits (~10 min): £800 a month
- Publishing and distribution (RSS, Spotify, Apple, YouTube, ad-hoc ad uploads): £600 a month
- **Podcast House total: £2,600 a month (£31,200 a year), studio hire charged separately on top**

The envelope maths:

- £45-50k stated all-in minus £31.2k PH fee leaves **£13.8k to £18.8k a year for studio hire and extras**, roughly £1,150 to £1,550 per monthly batch day. Inference from Helen's own numbers, not a confirmed rate.
- If the studio line continues direct with PH unchanged, the room left for HWL inside their envelope is **£26k to £36k a year, £2,200 to £3,000 a month**. Every point of that is below the £3,400 floor.
- Conclusion: **their budget is their current spend, not an upgrade budget.** Any real version of what they described wanting (care, editorial, a producer in the room) costs more than the envelope. The proposal has to say that plainly or it starts the relationship on a lie of omission.
- Framed monthly it is small: £3,650 versus the £2,600 PH fee is **about £1,050 a month more than today** for the whole care layer. Framed annually (£8-13k over) it sounds like a leap. Use the monthly frame.
- VAT: their figures are almost certainly ex VAT (Amazing If is an established ltd with corporate clients, VAT will be neutral to them). Not worth raising; just keep "plus VAT" explicit.

## Recommended number and why

**£3,650 a month plus VAT**, twelve months, review at the end of the first quarter. Scope below. Studio stays direct with Podcast House.

- The July range was £3,400 to £4,100 with Farringdon attendance priced separately. On the call Harrison folded attendance into the monthly pitch, so the proposal has to price it in deliberately. £3,400 plus a producer day (~£400 equivalent) is ~£3,800; £3,650 sits just under that. A visible sharpening against their stated budget, not a capitulation.
- Anchoring £4,100 now (£63-68k all-in with studio) would read as ignoring the budget they gave an hour earlier. Anchoring £3,400 burns the floor as the opener.
- Named retreat position: **£3,400 with Shortcuts on a lighter audio-first edit tier**. That is the one lever offered in writing. Nothing below £3,400 in any shape. Matching the £2,600 prices the care gap at zero and re-sells them the thing they are leaving.
- Stakes: £3,650 x 12 = £43.8k a year. On the ~£11k a month run rate this single deal moves annualised revenue from ~£132k to ~£176k, past the 12-month £150k goal on its own. Also the reason not to price it desperate: phases two and three inherit whatever anchor phase one sets.

## Phase one scope (what the £3,650 buys)

- Producer at the Farringdon recording day each month: Harrison runs the day, timestamps, retakes, intro and outro checks in the room. Podcast House is studio hire only; whatever desk support comes bundled with the room is PH's arrangement.
- All eight episodes edited by Harrison personally with the usefulness filter (the Conor standard, named by them as the benchmark). This is the line that gives Sarah M her hours back; she is currently flagging stumbles and repeats herself.
- Publishing and distribution across Megaphone, Apple, Spotify, YouTube. Titles, descriptions, scheduling. Squiggly Careers as headline brand throughout (they have moved all handles to Squiggly Careers over Amazing If).
- Editorial input on formats, openings, pacing. One consolidated feedback round per episode, routed through Sarah M.
- Monthly performance report: plays vs downloads, watch time and drop-off, listener locations. Stitch the Acast historical data (the lost 5.75M lifetime view) together with Megaphone. Cheap for Harrison (the BaW data agents already do this shape of work), lands directly on Sarah E's open "how do we judge if we're doing a good job" question.
- Music refresh proposal in month one, careful rollout. Same music since 2017; they named it as dated but scary to change.
- Weekly timestamped hooks and pull-quotes to Sarah M for the podcast social slots in her Airtable calendar. Design stays with Jen entirely; do not touch the squad.
- Month-ahead buffer with explicit licence for reactive swaps. They are exactly a month ahead now and want to stay there.
- Deliberately out: social clips and trailers. Their own year of testing says carousels and infographics outperform clips. Clips come back, if ever, as a phase-two experiment with data. Farringdon capture of extra social content also stays out of the monthly.
