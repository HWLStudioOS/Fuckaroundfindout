01 / The PulseBetter at Work · May–June 2026
Executive Summary

The Pulse.

April raised one worry above all others: Spotify had broken its floor. May answered it. Plays are up 29%, consumption hours up 28%, and the platform-specific drop has reversed.

At the same time the show recorded its first clean full month on Acast at 929 downloads, and it held that number while running a week short on episodes. This was a strong month that also closed the open question from the last one.

The honest read up front: the recovery is real and broad, but two things April flagged are still open. Spotify follower growth stayed slow even as plays recovered, and the show missed an episode the week of 4 June. Neither dented the month. Both are worth protecting against.

A note on metrics

Spotify changed its definition of a "play" on 11 June 2026, moving to a 30-second standard. Every Spotify figure here sits before that change, so May is a clean like-for-like against April. From next month, plays are counted differently, and we will flag the discontinuity so the trend is not misread.

The Turnaround

The floor came back.

April's loudest signal was a Spotify-specific drop: 216 plays, below the Season 4 floor. May reversed it. Plays are up 29% and back above the December floor of 249, tracking toward the Season 4 average. Consumption hours rose 28% in step.

We can now see Spotify show-page impressions: roughly 2,632 over the 30 days Spotify retains. New episodes surface through the Home feed (Leidy Klotz 185, 139 from Home); the back catalogue earns impressions through Search (Tasha Eurich 72, 59 from Search). The integration is working as it should post-migration.

One honest caveat

Plays recovered. Followers did not. Net new followers held at +4, the same slow rate April flagged, against a typical +7 to +12. People are listening more, but not subscribing at the same pace. It is the clearest single thing to fix on Spotify, and the plan carries an action.

Women 45–59 on the podcast, senior finance on LinkedIn.
Needs work
Spotify follower velocity.
Plays recovered, follower growth did not (+4, against a +7 to +12 norm). Fix: a clear follow prompt in the outro and show notes.
Cadence.
The week of 4 June had no episode. It cost nothing this month, but do not test it twice. Protect the weekly drop.
Instagram discovery.
Active but inward-facing (73.5% of views from followers, 4 bio-link taps). One reach lever to trial, likely Reels.
YouTube subscriber conversion.
View volume is not yet turning into subscribers at pace. Add an explicit subscribe ask to the shorts that travel.
June–July

The plan.

01

Roger Martin, recorded 12 June, is the next tentpole. Treat the launch as one coordinated day: Acast, YouTube long plus shorts, clips, LinkedIn and the newsletter on the same date.

02

Hold the weekly cadence. No more skipped weeks through the season close.

03

Newsletter. Hold the relaunched cadence and add the signup CTA to every episode description and Cathal's LinkedIn bio.

04

Spotify follow prompt. Ship the outro and show-note follow ask this month and read the follower line next report.

05

YouTube subscribe ask on the shorts that break out, and use the women 45–59 / senior-finance read to guide guest choices.
