# Form watchlist

Verified 13 August 2026. Optimum restored as its own creator 17 August 2026. Read by the `form-lab` agent.

These are Harrison's named form references for his own content. They are watched
for **structural devices**: how a thing opens, how it is cut, how a series is
framed, how a closer lands. Never for topics.

That distinction is load-bearing. `content/system.md` sets the substance rule:
the system never picks Harrison's subject matter, only his containers. An agent
that returns here with "you should make a video about X" has broken the rule and
will be vetoed, as assigned topics have been before.

Harrison's shorthand needed fixing before anything pointed at it. Recorded so a
future run does not reintroduce the errors:

- **RPN is Roberto P. Nickson**, not Nixon. He listed "RPN" and "Roberto Nixon" as two people. They are one.
- **Kirk Lepiten**, not "Kirk Lepetin".
- **Oliur is UK-based**, not Australian.
- **Optimum is Optimum Tech** ([@optimumtech](https://www.youtube.com/@optimumtech)). A creator like the others. Not Oliur's store, not a product catalogue footnote. Folding him in was cheap.

## How to watch them

Public, published material only: YouTube uploads, public posts, newsletters.
No scraping of private or gated surfaces, no automated account access, no
circumvention of any platform's terms. Public RSS, official APIs and web search
are the tools.

The point is not to know what they posted. It is to name the **device** and ask
whether Harrison has substance in the bank that the device would carry.
