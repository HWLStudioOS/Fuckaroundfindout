## Items

- **Software 3.0: From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering**, essay + talk, Andrej Karpathy. [https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/)
  Written up after his Sequoia Ascent 2026 fireside. The core distinction: vibe coding raises the floor (anyone can build anything), agentic engineering preserves the ceiling (professional quality without sacrificing accountability). LLMs are "ghosts, not animals" shaped by data and reward, with no intrinsic motivation. Jagged intelligence peaks in verifiable domains (code, math) and stays rough elsewhere. The line that stays: "You can outsource your thinking, but you can't outsource your understanding." Direct application: directing the Harrison OS agent suite requires aesthetic judgment, not just prompts. That judgment is not automatable.

- **What Remains Scarce After AGI?**, podcast, Dwarkesh Patel with Alex Imas and Phil Trammell. [https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/alex-imas-phil-trammell](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/alex-imas-phil-trammell)
  Episode from 4 June. Economists asking what does not scale when intelligence scales. "One robot turns into many robots next year, but the number of ballerinas is the same." The scarcity argument for creative, embodied, physically present work is made more crisply here than anywhere else in 2026. Challenges the anxiety that AI commoditises a content director or filmmaker. The counterargument is structural economics, not wishful thinking.

- **Choosing to Stay Human**, essay, Ethan Mollick (One Useful Thing). [https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/choosing-to-stay-human](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/choosing-to-stay-human)
  May 26. "Cognitive surrender" documented: BCG consultants using AI vastly outperformed on most tasks, but failed harder on the task AI got wrong. They stopped thinking and took the authoritative-looking wrong answer. The fix is not avoiding AI but being intentional rather than reflexive. The question: which cognitive tasks do you want to keep? Writing in your own voice. Directing clients. Creative calls. Those are the ones. Let AI handle the formatting.

- **Brice Partouche: SATISFY and the adidas launch**, profile, Office Magazine. [https://officemagazine.net/brice-partouche-has-never-cared-much-permission](https://officemagazine.net/brice-partouche-has-never-cared-much-permission)
  The Circle Pit launch: 50 ultra runners and media in a concrete skate park in 40-degree Sonoran Desert heat while hardcore bands played at volume. If adidas had been asked first, the answer would probably have been no. "SATISFY has never operated from consensus logic. That is exactly why the brand matters." The pattern Harrison is tracing in content, without corporate sign-off and with genuine aesthetic risk, is the same one. Taste without permission. Also: adidas now explicitly referencing SATISFY's Moth Tech in describing Nike ACG's new AirFlow NXT. When your taste references get absorbed by the majors, note it.

- **Mediterranean diet, gut microbiota, and VO2max adaptation**, RCT, Frontiers in Nutrition. [https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2026.1795528/full](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2026.1795528/full)
  12-week randomised controlled trial, 60 competitive endurance athletes. Mediterranean diet group improved VO2max by 2.4 vs 1.3 mL/kg/min (control). The mechanism: med diet increases Faecalibacterium and Roseburia, which produce short-chain fatty acids (propionate, butyrate), which mediate 23% of the performance benefit via the gut-muscle axis. Modest effect size but the mechanism is new and the mediation analysis is clean. Applies directly to fueling decisions before the next training block.

## Synthesis

- **Understanding as the non-automatable resource**: Three sources this week land on the same claim from different disciplines. Karpathy (engineering): you can outsource thinking but not understanding, because understanding is what lets you direct the agents. Mollick (behavioral science): cognitive surrender to AI short-circuits skill development; BCG elite consultants failed hardest on the tasks they delegated. Imas and Trammell (economics): post-AGI, what remains scarce is human judgment and physical presence. These are not vague reassurances about human specialness. They are structural arguments. The bottleneck is not intelligence capacity, it is the human judgment that directs intelligence well. Sources: [https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/), [https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/choosing-to-stay-human](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/choosing-to-stay-human), [https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/alex-imas-phil-trammell](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/alex-imas-phil-trammell).

- **Running culture being absorbed by the majors**: Nike ACG explicitly referenced SATISFY's Moth Tech when describing their new AirFlow NXT shirt (dropping June 25). adidas partnered with SATISFY directly, launching with the Circle Pit desert event. When two of the highest-taste references in running culture both become major-brand plays in the same month, the independent brand premium is compressing. This is how subcultures get digested. Worth tracking whether SATISFY's identity holds once the adidas co-branding scales. 1. Q: What are Durant's three biological lessons of history?
   A: (hidden, expand after attempt) Life is competition. Life is selection. Life must breed.
   Source: The Lessons of History, Ch. 3 "Biology and History."

2. Q: Across 3,421 years of recorded history, how many years of peace did the Durants count?
   A: (hidden) Approximately 268. War is the constant, not the exception.
   Source: The Lessons of History, Ch. 11 "History and War."

3. Q: What is the only real check on greed, per Durant?
   A: (hidden) The equally good greed of another. Competition, not virtue.
   Source: The Lessons of History, Ch. 11.

4. Q: Durant on morals: universal or contextual? What shapes them?
   A: (hidden) Contextual. Shaped by the dominant economic mode (hunting, agricultural, industrial). When the mode shifts, morals lag and feel "broken" during the transition.
   Source: The Lessons of History, Ch. 6 "Morals and History."
