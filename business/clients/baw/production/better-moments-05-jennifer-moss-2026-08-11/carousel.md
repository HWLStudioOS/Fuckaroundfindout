# Tuesday carousel

## Campaign idea

**Hope is not a feeling.**

The carousel makes Jennifer's hope model usable before the audience knows an episode is coming. It moves from the three parts of hope into two workplace conditions that weaken it, layoffs and micromanagement.

## Slide copy

### 1. Cover

Eyebrow: A BETTER WAY TO THINK ABOUT HOPE

Headline: HOPE IS NOT A FEELING.

Subhead: It is goals, pathways and agency.

### 2. The model

Eyebrow: THE THREE-PART MODEL

Headline: HOPE NEEDS THREE THINGS.

Body:

- A goal worth reaching.
- More than one route to it.
- Enough agency to make a move.

Callout: Remove one and hope becomes fragile.

### 3. Goals

Eyebrow: 01 / GOALS

Headline: START SMALL ENOUGH TO MOVE.

Body: When work feels impossible, the annual milestone is too far away. Use a goal close enough to finish today. Then make the progress visible.

Callout: One completed hour can restart momentum.

### 4. Pathways

Eyebrow: 02 / PATHWAYS

Headline: PLAN A IS NOT A STRATEGY.

Body: Life is circuitous. Hope grows when people can see more than one credible route forward.

Callout: Write plan B, C and D before the route closes.

### 5. Agency

Eyebrow: 03 / AGENCY

Headline: PEOPLE NEED ROOM TO MOVE.

Body: A goal and four paths still fail if every decision belongs to somebody else.

Callout: Agency turns a route into action.

### 6. Layoffs

Eyebrow: THE COST OF UNCERTAINTY

Headline: LAYOFFS HIT THE PEOPLE WHO STAY.

Body: When people expect the next cut, risk-taking drops. New ideas stop. Energy moves from contribution to self-protection.

Callout: Reskilling can preserve hope and useful talent.

### 7. Compliance

Eyebrow: THE MANAGEMENT TRAP

Headline: COMPLIANCE CANNOT CREATE HOPE.

Body: Micromanagement removes accomplishment and intrinsic motivation. It also raises the manager's own stress.

Callout: Agree the outcome. Give people room.

### 8. Action

Eyebrow: TRY THIS THIS WEEK

Headline: MAKE HOPE OPERATIONAL.

Body:

1. Pick one goal small enough to finish.
2. Write three viable paths.
3. Give someone a decision they own.

Callout: Better Moments #5 with Jennifer Moss lands Thursday.
