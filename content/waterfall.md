# Content Waterfall

**SUPERSEDED, 5 July 2026.** This system demanded 7-10 outputs a fortnight and ~8 hours a week from a solo operator mid-delivery and produced zero posts in the month it existed. The rebuild replaced it with the two-engine loop in `content/system.md` (one video + one written idea per week, everything else derived). The cascade thinking below survives in spirit (one idea, many surfaces) but this file is history, not guidance.

---

How one idea becomes five outputs across IG, TikTok, LinkedIn, Substack, and (later) YouTube. The discipline that turns one good thought into a week of content without making five separate ideas.

**Rule:** every fortnight has ONE big idea. That idea cascades downstream. You do not chase five separate ideas per week. That's the H21 trap.

---

## The big idea

Defined as: a single observed pattern, field-note, or thesis that's worth more than a tweet. Usually surfaced from real work, a client call, a training session, a thing Maya said, a Granola meeting transcript, an essay you read.

Captured in `capture/inbox.md`. Reviewed every Sunday evening. One promoted to "the idea of the fortnight."

---

## The cascade (1 idea → 5 outputs)

```
                    THE IDEA OF THE FORTNIGHT
                              │
       ┌──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┐
       │                      │                      │
   FORM A                  FORM B                FORM C
   Substack essay      Cinematic essay      Field-note reel
   (700-1500 words)    (Tier B, 60-150s)    (Tier A, 45-75s)
   The longform        The poetic           The talking-head
   thesis.             distillation.        first-person take.
       │                      │                      │
       │                      ├── IG Reel            ├── IG Reel
       │                      ├── TikTok             ├── TikTok
       │                      ├── LinkedIn native    ├── LinkedIn native
       │                      └── YouTube (later)    └── (no YouTube)
       │
       ├── LinkedIn editorial (Tier C, the stanza caption + still image)
       ├── X thread (5-7 tweets, the spine of the essay)
       └── Newsletter quote pull (one paragraph for Substack post header)
```

Total outputs per fortnight: **7 to 10 pieces of content from 1 idea.**

## The weekly rhythm

Two-week cycle. Idea per fortnight. Posts every week.

### Week 1 (the build week)

| Day    | Action                                                                                                        | Output                               |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Sun PM | Promote one inbox idea to "fortnight idea". Write Substack draft.                                             | Substack essay v1                    |
| Mon AM | Cinematic essay script written (Form B). Schedule shoot.                                                      | Form B script                        |
| Mon PM | Field-note reel script written (Form C).                                                                      | Form C script                        |
| Tue AM | Substack publishes (or scheduled for Thu). LinkedIn editorial post writes off the essay's strongest argument. | **LinkedIn editorial live** (Tier C) |
| Wed    | Shoot Form C reel. Edit Wed night.                                                                            | (in edit)                            |
| Thu    | Form C posts: IG Reel + TikTok + LinkedIn native upload.                                                      | **3 posts live**, same content       |
| Fri    | Shoot Form B (cinematic). Edit over weekend if no client conflict.                                            | (in edit)                            |

### Week 2 (the cinematic week)

| Day | Action | Output |
|-----|--------|--------|
| Mon | Cinematic essay finishes edit. Caption written. | Ready to publish |
| Tue | Cinematic posts: IG Reel + TikTok + LinkedIn native. Substack republishes if not already live. | **3 posts live**, same content |
| Wed-Thu | One supplementary post: a behind-the-scenes from either piece, or a still + caption from the cinematic. | **1 extra** |
| Fri | Sunday-evening sync: review what landed, capture next idea candidates. | (planning) |

**Total per fortnight: 7 posts live. 1 to 2 supplementary. 1 Substack essay. 1 LinkedIn editorial. 1 X thread (the essay distilled, 5-7 tweets).**

### Why the X thread matters disproportionately

The X thread is small effort but high-leverage:

- **AI rankability.** LLM training data is text-heavy and X-heavy. Showing up in text on a platform that gets hoovered into model training data is how operators stay queryable in 2027. If the only place your thinking exists is video reels, you're invisible to every AI surface.
- **Distribution to the LinkedIn-allergic.** A subset of buyers and operators won't touch LinkedIn but live on X. Same idea, parallel discovery surface.
- **Studio arc grows here later.** X is where HWL Studio eventually has its own voice in v1.1. Establishing the personal account first sets the tone.

The X thread does not need its own platform doc. The format is: extract the essay's spine into 5-7 tweets, post as a thread, link to the full essay in tweet 7. Done in 20 minutes.

## Cross-posting discipline

- **Always native upload.** Never share a TikTok link to IG. Never link out from LinkedIn to a Substack post in the post body, drop the link as a first comment.
- **Caption rewritten per platform.** Same idea, different first line.
  - IG: hook-led, short, no hashtags in body.
  - TikTok: hook-led, even punchier, hashtags in caption are fine.
  - LinkedIn: stanza-style, no hashtags in body, 1-2 in a comment.
  - Substack: title + subhead + essay.
- **Watermarks removed.** If a reel went to TikTok first, re-export without the TikTok watermark for IG. The algorithm punishes other-platform watermarks.
- **48-hour gap between platforms ideal.** Same reel TikTok Tuesday, IG Thursday. Not same day on both.

---

## What this protects against

- The H21 trap: trying to come up with new ideas daily and burning out by Wednesday.
- Platform-specific fatigue: each platform thinks they're getting fresh from you because the format / framing / pacing differs even though the spine is shared.
- The "I haven't posted in two weeks" spiral: the rhythm makes the work systematic, not heroic.

---

## What this requires

- One Substack essay per fortnight (the highest-effort piece). Roughly 3-4 hours of writing.
- One cinematic essay shoot per fortnight. Half a day.
- One field-note reel shoot per week. 30 min.
- Edit time: 2-4 hours per fortnight (Form C is fast; Form B is the heavy edit).

**Total weekly load: ~8 hours of dedicated content time.** Front-loaded Sunday/Monday. Editable around client work.

If that load is unrealistic given current delivery, see `content/production-system.md` for the editor delegation path.
