# Tactical Wedges, May 2026
## Specific arbitrages exploitable by a solo operator with strong AI tooling and £15-25k

Brief: find the actual hidden moves, not archetypes. Date: 10 May 2026. Capital available: £15-25k. Operator profile: solo, strong AI tooling, willing to do unsexy work.

Ranked by realistic 12-month upside and conviction. Each wedge is a concrete move, not a category.

## Wedge 1. The "Botkeeper Refugee" Bookkeeping Reseller for UK Accountants Pre-MTD

**Surface.** UK accounting firms with 50-500 SME clients, especially those owned by solo principals or two-partner shops in the £500k-£3m revenue band. Acute focus on firms scrambling for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD ITSA) which came into force April 2026 for self-employed/landlords above £50k income.

**What's broken.** Botkeeper, the venture-backed AI bookkeeping platform that hundreds of US/UK firms had embedded in their workflow, shut down in early 2026 after eleven years. Firms that had outsourced their entire transaction-coding pipeline to it are now stranded. At the same time, MTD ITSA is forcing tens of thousands of UK landlords and sole traders to switch from spreadsheets to compliant digital records. UK accounting practices report 98% AI usage but only a small share have actually rebuilt their workflow around an AI agent stack.

**The move.** Productised "Bookkeeping-as-a-Workflow" service for UK accountancies. Operator stitches together one of the surviving AI bookkeeping engines (Hellobooks, Truewind, Pilot's API, or a custom n8n + Claude + Xero/QuickBooks pipeline) and resells it as a white-label back office. Firm pays a flat per-client/month fee. Operator keeps the margin and a layer of human QA. Position as "Botkeeper replacement, but boring and reliable, run by humans you can phone."

**Who pays and at what rate.** UK accounting principals. Charge £45-£90/client/month (firm bills their end client £150-£250). At 200 clients across 4-6 firms, that is £108k-£216k revenue at 60-70% net margin once the workflow is built.

**Why a solo can win.** Incumbents go for enterprise contracts and assume a sales motion. Accounting principals trust other operators they can WhatsApp at 7pm on a Sunday. The wedge is not the AI, it is the human accountability layer the firm needs in front of HMRC. Building a vertical reseller in MTD-quarter-end mode is unglamorous; VC-backed competitors will not bother because the clients are too small individually.

**Time to first revenue.** 4-6 weeks. Two firms to validate and one paying.

**2026 timing.** MTD ITSA hits in April 2026. Botkeeper's collapse left a referenceable hole. Combined, this is a one-shot 12-month window before the surviving large platforms (Xero, Sage) bake in equivalent AI capability natively, which the consensus puts at H2 2027.

**Validation move (7 days).** Email 50 ICAEW-listed solo principals offering a free "Botkeeper escape audit" or "MTD client triage." Two replies = wedge is real. Look at r/Accounting UK and the AccountingWEB forums for the specific complaints to lift directly into the cold email.

**Failure modes.** 1) Xero ships native AI bookkeeping and leapfrogs you. Mitigation: position as the human service wrapper, not the engine. 2) HMRC compliance liability scares you off. Mitigation: never sign as the agent of record, you sit behind the firm.

## Wedge 2. AI Citation Monitoring + Repair for Mid-Market UK Brands

**Surface.** UK B2B services companies in the £5m-£100m revenue band that have a marketing lead but no in-house SEO team. Specifically the segment that historically spent £2-8k/month with an SEO agency.

**What's broken.** Google AI Overviews now appear on 38% of local service queries and have cratered organic CTR by ~61% on impacted queries. Industry data shows 73% of B2B sites lost significant organic traffic between 2024 and 2025. Meanwhile when a brand IS cited in an AI Overview, organic CTR is 35% higher. Traffic from ChatGPT converts at 16% versus 1.8% for Google organic. The old SEO agencies are mostly still selling 2022-shaped retainers.

**The move.** "AEO-as-a-service": a £1,200-£2,500/month productised retainer that does three specific things and nothing else. (a) Tracks the client's brand mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews for 50-200 buyer-intent prompts weekly. (b) Identifies the source URLs the LLMs are citing about the client and their competitors. (c) Ships a monthly content + structured-data sprint that gets the client cited in those LLM answers. Tools: Profound, Otterly, or your own Python script wrapping the public APIs.

**Who pays and at what rate.** Marketing managers and founders who have just watched a quarter of organic traffic disappear. Anchor rate £1,800/month. 6 retainers = £130k/year. 12 = £260k.

**Why a solo can win.** AEO agencies are still pitching enterprise. Mid-market is being ignored. Productised scope means one operator + AI can run 8-12 retainers without choking. The category has only existed for ~12 months in the public lexicon, so credibility comes from fast results and case studies, not legacy.

**Time to first revenue.** 3 weeks. Sell the audit (£500) first, convert to retainer.

**2026 timing.** This is purely a 2025-26 phenomenon. AI Overviews scaled in 2024, traffic damage materialised in 2025, the panic conversation is happening NOW in marketing slacks. By 2027 the big SEO agencies will have all rebranded as AEO/GEO and the wedge closes.

**Validation move (7 days).** Pick 20 UK B2B SaaS companies. For each, run one prompt in ChatGPT Pro and one in Perplexity asking "what is the best [their category]" and screenshot whether they appear. Send each marketing lead the screenshots with one line: "You are not in this answer. Your competitor is. Worth a conversation?" Reply rate of >10% means the wedge is real.

**Failure modes.** 1) LLM citation patterns become impossibly opaque. Mitigation: the deliverable is content quality, which still drives ranking. 2) HubSpot bakes citation tracking into Marketing Hub. Mitigation: own the methodology and the human reporting layer.

## Wedge 3. The "Recruitment Video Cell" for Specialist Trade Firms

**Surface.** UK construction, MEP, and skilled-trade firms in the 50-300 employee range that are losing recruitment battles to bigger competitors. CITB says the UK needs 250,000 additional construction workers by 2026. HVAC engineer vacancies jumped 77.89% between 2022 and 2026.

**What's broken.** Trade firms are competing for a shrinking pool of skilled workers. Their job ads sit on Indeed and CV-Library next to fifty identical ones. Their websites have no video. Their LinkedIn is empty. The recruitment agencies they pay 15-22% of first-year salary deliver candidates that ghost. Their actual differentiation (a good site manager, decent kit, reliable Friday pay, real apprenticeship pathway) is invisible.

**The move.** A £4-6k/month "recruitment storytelling" productised service. Operator does one half-day shoot per month at the client's main site, captures four employee stories on iPhone + Rode mic, edits with Descript and a CapCut template, and ships: 8 short vertical videos for Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn, one 90-second hero for the careers page, AI-generated transcript and structured FAQ for AEO. Bundled with a tight LinkedIn + Indeed ad spend recommendation. Apply Harrison's existing Creepers/LOR shooting workflow to a higher-volume vertical.

**Who pays and at what rate.** HR director or COO at trade firms losing money to an empty rota. Anchor £4,500/month, 3-month minimums. 5 clients = £270k/year before any out-of-scope work.

**Why a solo can win.** This is a hands-on shoot business. Big agencies will not travel to a Slough warehouse for one day a month. Recruitment agencies want commission, not retainers. The ROI calculation is brutal: one filled apprentice slot = £8-15k saved in agency fees, easy to justify the retainer.

**Time to first revenue.** 6-8 weeks. Find one believer, deliver one shoot, charge for the next.

**2026 timing.** Labour shortage is at peak political salience (the Department of Labor mandated AI training for trades in 2026 in the US, UK debating similar). Trade firms have just absorbed the wage inflation and now realise marketing is the constraint, not pay. AI editing tools cut shoot-to-deliver from 5 days to 1.

**Validation move (7 days).** Cold-WhatsApp 30 trade firm owners with one Loom video showing a competitor's careers page (empty) versus a polished version you mocked up in Figma. Two booked discoveries = wedge confirmed.

**Failure modes.** 1) Client wants "more for the price." Mitigation: contract is brutally tight on scope, anything else is hourly. 2) AI video generation makes shoots redundant. Mitigation: the moat is the actual humans being filmed, which generative AI cannot fake credibly for recruitment.

## Wedge 4. "Dead Podcast Tour" Repurposing for Already-Famous Authors

**Surface.** Authors and academics who did a major podcast tour in 2024-25 (book launch publicity) and have 30-80 hours of long-form audio sitting on YouTube channels they do not control. Examples: Adam Grant alumni, McKinsey Author Talks guests, anyone with a Penguin Random House non-fiction debut from 2023-25.

**What's broken.** This is the exact gap Harrison identified in Colin Fisher's case (see colin-fisher.md, content research notes). Massive podcast tour, zero repurposing. McKinsey Author Talks, HBR IdeaCast, Times Radio, Fox 2 News, all sitting as dead content. Fortier-style PR firms charge £8-15k/month for the launch then stop. Nobody owns the sustained content layer.

**The move.** £2,500-£4,000/month "Author Repurposing Engine." Operator ingests the existing 50+ hours of podcast appearances using Whisper + Claude, builds a thematic library of insights tagged by topic, then ships per week: 4 LinkedIn posts (text + image), 2 Substack snippets, 3 short clips, 1 monthly newsletter assembly. The IP is the ingestion + voice-modelling system. Specifically target authors who had a 2023-25 launch and are now 12-18 months out, in the dead zone before book 2.

**Who pays and at what rate.** Mid-tier non-fiction authors, professors with consulting/speaking revenue, McKinsey/BCG alumni who did one book. Anchor £2,800/month. 5 retainers = £168k/year. Use Colin Fisher as the Harrison case study and signpost the methodology.

**Why a solo can win.** This requires deep voice-fidelity work. AI agencies pump out generic LinkedIn carousels. Big content agencies will not work with a single individual at this price. The work is done by one operator with strong taste, which is exactly the wedge VC-backed comp cannot replicate.

**Time to first revenue.** 4-6 weeks. Colin Fisher confirms in evaluation period. One referral from him into the academic network unlocks the channel.

**2026 timing.** Whisper-3 + Claude 4.x finally make voice-faithful repurposing tractable for a solo. The 2023-25 cohort of book-tour authors are at the perfect "post-launch flatline" moment. PR firms have left them, visibility decays from week 12 post-launch, the data finally proves text-only LinkedIn is undermonetised. By 2027 every author will have an AI repurposing solution, but the trust and case studies are won now.

**Validation move (7 days).** Pull a list of 30 authors who appeared on McKinsey Author Talks 2023-25. For 3 of them, build a 5-post LinkedIn pack from their existing podcast tape. Send unsolicited. One reply = wedge.

**Failure modes.** 1) Big agency builds same product cheaper. Mitigation: positioning is "I work with you, not your assistant." Premium-priced. 2) Author's voice is impossible to capture. Mitigation: do the voice-sample call before contracting.

## Wedge 5. Workflow Builder for the Sub-VC-Threshold Vertical: Independent UK Optometrists

**Surface.** UK independent optometrists. ~3,500 practices outside the Specsavers / Boots / Vision Express chains. Average practice revenue £350k-£900k. Owner is the lead clinician.

**What's broken.** Practice-management software is dominated by Optix and Ocuco at the enterprise level. Independents run on a creaky combo of Hospital Practice Manager, Outlook, manual recall letters, and a phone that nobody answers. NHS GOS claims still done semi-manually. Average no-show rate 12-18%. Patient recall pulls in 60% of revenue but is the most neglected workflow.

**The move.** A productised "Front Desk AI" for one vertical. Built in n8n or Lindy or even custom Twilio + Claude. Three specific automations: (a) inbound missed-call deflection that books appointments via SMS conversational flow, (b) automated 18-month recall with personalised health context, (c) NHS GOS claim pre-population from EHR data. Sold flat at £500-£800/month per practice. No platform play, no SaaS positioning, just "we run this for you."

**Who pays and at what rate.** Practice owners. £600/month average. 30 practices = £216k/year. Margins ~80% once built because compute cost is trivial.

**Why a solo can win.** Vertical is too small for VC interest (3,500 max practices, sub-£25M TAM). Specsavers is captive to its parent group's IT. The wedge is one operator who knows the GOS form fields and can be on a video call when it breaks. Optometrists have professional bodies (AOP, FODO) where word travels fast.

**Time to first revenue.** 6-8 weeks. Build for one optometrist for free, document, sell to next 5.

**2026 timing.** Voice AI quality crossed the "answers calls without offending pensioners" line in late 2025 (see Bland, Vapi, Retell). Twilio + GPT-4o realtime API became cheap enough to make per-call costs <£0.20. Independents are simultaneously losing 1-2% market share annually to chains and need the productivity boost to survive.

**Validation move (7 days).** Pick 30 independent practices in M25. For each, call at 1pm Tuesday and at 5:30pm Friday. Log how many ring out or hit voicemail. Use the data in a cold email: "I called your practice 4 times last week, you missed 3 calls. Each missed call is worth £180 in average appointment value. Worth 15 minutes?"

**Failure modes.** 1) AOP or NHS Digital release a recommended toolkit. Mitigation: become the recommended implementation partner. 2) Practice owner risk-aversion. Mitigation: 30-day pilot, no contract.

*Note: optometrists are illustrative. The same wedge structure works for: independent veterinary practices (3,500+ in UK), single-site fitness clubs, audiologists, podiatrists, private dentists, picture framers, bridalwear shops. Pick the one Harrison has a personal entry into.*

## Wedge 6. The "Confidential Granola" for Boutique M&A and PE Advisory

**Surface.** Boutique M&A advisors, PE deal teams, fund-of-funds, family office investment teams. London cluster: 200-300 individuals across firms like Lincoln International, Houlihan Lokey UK, smaller PE shops. Average deal team is 3-8 people.

**What's broken.** Granola, Otter, Fireflies all need to be in the room. Confidential M&A calls cannot have a recording bot present, period. Yet partners spend 10-15 hours a week in calls and the analyst notes are bad. Granola's "Mac-native, no bot" approach is closest to the answer but firms still treat it as too risky for IC discussions.

**The move.** A managed-service deployment of an air-gapped, on-device transcription + summarisation pipeline. Use OpenAI Whisper local + a self-hosted Llama or Claude via AWS Bedrock with a contractual data-residency clause. Sold not as software but as "your AI Chief of Staff for deal teams," £3-6k/month per partner, including white-glove setup, custom IC memo templates, and a fortnightly review call. Sit in front of the IT/security objection by making the operator the human accountability layer.

**Who pays and at what rate.** Managing partners, heads of investments. Anchor £3,500/month per individual or £8-12k/month per deal team. 6 partners = £252k/year.

**Why a solo can win.** Granola, Fireflies, and Otter all sell horizontally. They cannot navigate the bespoke security review of a single PE shop. Big consultancies will quote £75k for the implementation. The solo is the "trusted operator" who has signed an NDA and is on WhatsApp.

**Time to first revenue.** 8-10 weeks. Long sales cycle, so seed it now. First contract by July.

**2026 timing.** Local LLM quality (Llama 3.x, Phi-4) finally matches GPT-4 on summarisation tasks for under £8k of one-off hardware. AWS Bedrock launched the dedicated capacity tier in 2025 making air-gapped Claude tractable. Deal teams are simultaneously under cost pressure (PE recovery is "durable but slow" per Withintelligence outlook 2026) so back-office productivity is on the table.

**Validation move (7 days).** Find 10 mid-market PE/M&A boutiques in London via FCA register. For 3, get a partner-level intro via Harrison's existing Laing O'Rourke / Vested Way / 180 House network. Pitch the air-gapped angle as the differentiator.

**Failure modes.** 1) Granola releases an enterprise tier with full security compliance. Mitigation: own the implementation services even if their software wins. 2) Sales cycle too long for solo cashflow. Mitigation: use cheaper Wedge 4 or 2 retainers as the cashflow base.

## Wedge 7. "AI Slop Cleanup" Editorial Service for B2B SaaS Marketing Teams

**Surface.** B2B SaaS marketing teams in the 5-30 person range, especially Series A-C venture-funded companies that have written everything with AI in 2024-25 and are now realising it all sounds the same.

**What's broken.** Content marketers complain on LinkedIn and Indie Hackers that their entire blog reads like a generated artefact, traffic is gone (see Wedge 2), and rebuilds are stuck because everyone is using the same prompts and the same model. Quote from public IH thread: "everyone has access to the same AI tools and prompts, so the only thing that can't be replicated is what you build with real humans who actually care." Big editorial agencies (Animalz, Grow & Convert) are at £15-30k/month and full.

**The move.** A "human edit pass" productised service. Operator takes existing AI-drafted content (long-form blog, white papers, sales collateral) and rewrites the top 20 highest-traffic / highest-pipeline pages each month with documented voice rules, real proprietary insight injection, and removal of the obvious AI tells. Standard scope: 10 pages/month, £4,500. Bundle with quarterly voice-audit deliverable.

**Who pays and at what rate.** Head of Content or VP Marketing at $3-30M ARR SaaS. £4,500/month. 5 clients = £270k/year. Margins 70%+ because the work is mostly editing not generation.

**Why a solo can win.** Editorial taste is the moat. The wedge is "I will sit on Zoom with your founder for 45 minutes and extract the actual insight," which agencies cannot scale. By being explicitly human-only on the rewrite layer, you flip the AI Slop fear into a value prop.

**Time to first revenue.** 4 weeks. Sell first client at a discount in exchange for the case study.

**2026 timing.** The 2024 vintage of AI-generated content is now 12-18 months old. Performance data is in and it is bad. Hiring freezes mean teams cannot just hire a senior editor in-house. Window before companies bake their voice into the AI itself (Anthropic's "writing style" features are improving fast) is ~12 months.

**Validation move (7 days).** Audit 10 Series A SaaS company blogs. For each, identify the 3 most-AI-generic posts and the 3 most-on-brand. Send the comparison + a short rewrite sample to the head of content. 2 replies = wedge real.

**Failure modes.** 1) Anthropic / OpenAI ship voice-faithful generation that solves the problem. Mitigation: pivot to "voice system" consulting, set up the prompts and processes for in-house. 2) Bad clients with no real insight to extract. Mitigation: discovery call as gate, walk away from empty ones.

## Wedge 8. Zero-CRM Lead Management for Solo Practitioners (Coaches, Therapists, Surveyors, Photographers)

**Surface.** UK solo professionals billing £80-300/hour who currently manage their pipeline in Gmail and a Notion doc. Particularly: executive coaches, ICF-certified therapists in private practice, chartered surveyors, wedding photographers, freelance architects.

**What's broken.** HubSpot Free is too complex. Salesforce is unthinkable. Carrd-style websites generate enquiries that go to a Gmail inbox and rot. 30-40% of paid enquiries never get a follow-up within 48 hours. Every "AI assistant" product is built for sales teams of 10+. Solo professionals do not need a CRM, they need someone (or something) to handle the qualification thread for them.

**The move.** A £180-£350/month "Inbox + Calendar Concierge" run by AI but presented as human. Operator deploys a simple Make/n8n agent that watches the client's Gmail for new enquiries, drafts a qualifying response in their voice, books the discovery call via Cal.com, follows up 3 times if no reply, and produces a weekly Loom-style video brief on the pipeline state. Bundle with one quarterly pricing-and-positioning audit.

**Who pays and at what rate.** Solo practitioners earning £100-300k/year who lose ~£15-30k annually to enquiry decay. £250/month average. 40 clients = £120k/year. This is the volume play.

**Why a solo can win.** The pricepoint excludes any VC-backed competitor. The trust required (you see their inbox) excludes anyone they cannot vet. SimplePractice and Calendly do not aggregate. The operator owns the integration layer.

**Time to first revenue.** 3 weeks. Sell to 3 friends-of-friends, document, sell into the relevant professional bodies (BACP, RICS, ICF, AOP).

**2026 timing.** Claude 4.x is finally good enough at email tone-matching that the responses do not need hand-editing. Gmail API + Cal.com API + n8n combo is now stable. Solo professionals just got hit by the post-pandemic slowdown so the ROI math is on the table for the first time.

**Validation move (7 days).** Post a free "Lost Enquiry Audit" offer in 3 LinkedIn groups (UK Coaches, RICS members, Wedding Photographers UK). Anyone who claims their audit gets a screen recording showing the holes. Conversion rate to paid >10% means the wedge is real.

**Failure modes.** 1) Client paranoia about AI seeing their inbox. Mitigation: explicitly human-managed positioning, even if AI is doing 90% of the work. 2) Volume play means churn matters. Mitigation: quarterly audit forces the relationship reset.

## Wedge 9 (bonus). The "Job-to-Be-Done Briefing" Service for VC-Backed Founder LinkedIn

**Surface.** Series A-C SaaS founders who post on LinkedIn but hate doing it. Specifically the cohort of YC W24 / S24 / W25 founders who got the "you must build in public" advice and are 6-12 months in.

**What's broken.** Founder ghostwriting is now crowded with 200+ agencies (per industry data). Most produce LinkedIn slop. Founders who can spot it are now rejecting agencies and posting nothing, which is worse. The wedge is not the writing, it is the briefing extraction. They do not have time to type 200 words but can give a 12-minute voice memo on Tuesday morning.

**The move.** £1,800-£2,400/month "Founder Voice Memo to LinkedIn" service. Operator sends a 3-question Loom every Sunday evening, founder records voice replies any time before Wednesday, operator ships 3 LinkedIn posts and 1 X thread by Friday. Strict 30-minute weekly time commitment from the founder.

**Who pays and at what rate.** Series A/B founders, anchor £2,000/month, 6 retainers = £144k/year. Margin 75% because the writing is fast with strong voice samples.

**Why a solo can win.** This is fundamentally about taste in extracting the right insight from a rambling voice memo. AI tooling speeds up the writing but the choice of WHICH 30 seconds of an 8-minute memo to turn into a post is the entire job. Big LinkedIn agencies do not do voice intake well.

**Time to first revenue.** 3 weeks.

**2026 timing.** Voice-to-text quality (Whisper-3, Claude voice mode) finally lets the operator skip the transcription step. Founders are tired of writing apps and tired of agencies. The 200+ ghostwriting agency count means there is also more pull-through demand than ever.

**Validation move (7 days).** DM 30 YC founders. Offer a free "1 post from your voice memo" trial. Conversion >10% = wedge real.

**Failure modes.** 1) The actual writing is bad. Mitigation: keep portfolio tight. 2) Cohort sentiment turns against LinkedIn entirely. Mitigation: pivot to X or Substack offering.

## Wedge 10 (bonus, longest tail). White-Label "Ops Agent" Build for Vertical SaaS Companies

**Surface.** Mid-market vertical SaaS companies (£5-30M ARR) in legaltech, proptech, healthtech, fintech that need to ship "AI agents" into their product to keep up with the agentic SaaS narrative but cannot recruit the engineering talent in 2026. Per a16z thesis, vertical AI is the next wave but most vertical SaaS incumbents are years behind on the build.

**What's broken.** A vertical SaaS CEO sees competitors announce "AI agent" features. Their board demands the same. Their engineering team is 8 people and tied up on platform work. Hiring an AI engineer costs £140-£200k base + equity and takes 6 months. Big consulting (Slalom, BCG X) quotes £400k for the same scope. Specialist agencies are booked.

**The move.** Productised "Embedded Agent Build" engagement. £18-25k for a 6-week build of one specific agent inside their product, with a clear scope (e.g. "auto-summarise client matter notes," "draft policy renewal emails," "QA construction submittals"). Operator uses LangGraph/Pydantic AI + Claude/GPT to ship a production-ready agent the SaaS team can extend. Optional £3-5k/month maintenance after.

**Who pays and at what rate.** CTO or CPO at a £5-30M ARR vertical SaaS. £20k average per build. 5 builds in year 1 = £100k. With one £3k/month maintenance retainer, second-year run-rate is £180-220k.

**Why a solo can win.** Boutique scope. Solo can move faster than any agency, with personal brand and deep delivery taste. The point is not to scale the agency, it is to be the named individual who ships the agent. Vertical SaaS CEOs trust other operators, not pitch decks.

**Time to first revenue.** 8-10 weeks. Need the case study to flywheel.

**2026 timing.** LangGraph + Claude 4 + cheap evals make a 6-week agent build genuinely feasible for a solo. SaaS boards are demanding agentic features now; this peaks in 2026 and saturates by 2027.

**Validation move (7 days).** Pick 10 UK vertical SaaS companies (legaltech, proptech). For each, identify one product surface where an agent could replace a manual step. Send the CTO a 3-paragraph "here is the agent you need" memo. 1 paid discovery = wedge real.

**Failure modes.** 1) Engineering depth required. Mitigation: stay in the 80%-of-the-job zone, hand the deep optimisation back. 2) Long sales cycle. Mitigation: sit on the cashflow from Wedges 1-3 while you build the pipeline.

## Cross-cutting observations

1. **The recurring 2026 unlock**: voice-faithful AI (Claude 4.x, Whisper-3) finally clears the "could a senior person hear it without wincing" bar. Anything that uses voice memos or transcription as input is suddenly a wedge.
2. **The recurring distribution unlock**: AI Overviews killed enough SEO that incumbents are exposed and mid-market is panicked. Multiple wedges (1, 2, 4, 7) compound off this.
3. **The recurring pricing pattern**: £1.5k-£5k/month productised retainer is the sweet spot. Above £5k requires enterprise sales motion. Below £1k requires volume churn management.
4. **The Harrison-specific overlay**: Wedges 2, 3, 4, 7, 9 are direct extensions of existing HWL Studio capability. Wedges 1, 5, 6, 8, 10 require a real upskill. The right portfolio is probably 2 of the first set actively sold this quarter, 1 of the second set as a learn-while-you-build side bet.
5. **What to ignore**: anything pitched as "build the AI version of [horizontal SaaS]" because incumbents will absorb it. Anything that needs >£25k upfront capital. Anything where the moat is the model.
