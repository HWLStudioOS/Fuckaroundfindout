# LOR Early Talent 2026: casting criteria

**DRAFT. NOT SENT. FOR HARRISON APPROVAL.**

We need real work on camera, not rehearsed employer-brand answers. Each person should be able to show one thing they genuinely own, explain one decision they made and connect it to what happens on the project next.

## The cast

| Person | What they need to show |
|---|---|
| Structural engineering lead | A genuine drawing review, calculation, check, mark or sign-off and what cannot continue until it is complete |
| Building-services lead | A real model, screen, system check or coordination decision and where its consequence becomes visible on site |
| Design-management lead | A real clash, revision or coordination issue, who had to align and what delay or rework was avoided |
| Procurement lead | A live package, brief, tracker or order and a real trade-off between cost, quality, lead time and certainty, with confidential pricing removed |
| Early Talent assessor | Broad advice on one positive signal, one common missed opportunity and one practical preparation tip. A cleared generic question may be used off camera as a prompt, but no question, score, candidate answer, candidate example or personal data appears in the film |
| Site day-in-the-life lead | A genuine site day with an owned task, decision, colleague interaction, problem and honest reflection |
| Project-office day-in-the-life lead | A genuine project-office day with one live tool or model, a meeting or interaction, one decision and a clear connection back to site |

One role lead can also carry a day-in-the-life story if both stories remain real and complete.

## Common criteria

Every contributor must:

- Work on the proposed project and own something filmable now.
- Explain their role in plain English.
- Name one recent decision, one consequence and one honest problem.
- Show a real proof item that can be cleared for public filming.
- Describe only the authority they genuinely hold.
- Complete a 20-minute phone pre-interview in the week before filming.
- Be available for the required filming block, site induction and release process.
- Have a named manager, site and document-clearance route.

Specificity and credibility matter more than polish.

## The project test

The hero film promises one live project passing through four roles. Before visit one, we need:

1. One named project and workstream all four roles genuinely touch.
2. One real input and output per role.
3. Three real interfaces between the four roles if Pass the Baton is used.
4. One pre-cleared drawing, model, screen, package, order, tracker or check per person.

If the four people share the project but not a neat literal chain, we use Four Decisions. We do not stage a handoff that did not happen.

## What each person brings to the pre-interview

- Their exact job title, entry route and time in role.
- The project and workstream they will discuss.
- One decision from the last working week.
- The drawing, model, screen, package, tracker, order or check that proves it.
- The input they received and the output they handed on.
- Who received that output next.
- What becomes late, wrong or blocked if their decision fails.
- One recent problem and what they did.
- One misconception they had before joining.
- One honest downside.
- The next step in qualification, responsibility or progression, using cleared facts only.
- Any privacy, access or reasonable-adjustment needs.

## Twenty-minute role pre-interview

| Time | Question |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 2:00 | What is your role, route in and actual authority on this project? |
| 2:00 to 4:00 | What are you responsible for today, in one sentence? |
| 4:00 to 8:00 | Show the real drawing, model, screen, package or check |
| 8:00 to 11:00 | What decision did you make this week, and what happens on site if it is wrong or late? |
| 11:00 to 14:00 | What input did you receive, what output did you create and who needed it next? |
| 14:00 to 17:00 | What went wrong recently, and what did you do? |
| 17:00 to 19:00 | What did you misunderstand before joining, what is one honest downside and what comes next? |
| 19:00 to 20:00 | Confirm the exact filming beat, proof item, privacy risks, access and release route |

### Role probes

- Structural: What exact item will we see you check or mark? What cannot continue until it is done?
- Building services: What does this model or screen reveal, and where does the decision become visible on site?
- Design management: What clash, revision or coordination issue did you spot, and who needed the resolved output?
- Procurement: What package decision did you influence, what was the trade-off and what happens on site next?

## Clearance before the day

For each person, Kerri or the site lead confirms:

- Full name, job title and preferred on-screen wording.
- Project and workstream.
- Manager approval and contributor release.
- Exact proof item and clearance owner.
- Screens and documents sanitised before crew arrival.
- Public use of project name, signage and visible location.
- Any external client, joint-venture, designer or supplier approval.
- Any personal data, supplier pricing, programme, security or access information to keep out of frame.
- Whether any participant is under 18 and, if so, the safeguarding and consent route.

The two visits take place on live projects. There is no Dartford filming.
