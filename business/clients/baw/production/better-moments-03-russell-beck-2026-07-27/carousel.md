## Slide copy

### 1. Cover

Eyebrow: THE WRONG AI QUESTION

Headline: AI DOESN'T DESTROY JOBS. IT DESTROYS SKILLS.

Subhead: What work actually loses when technology improves.

### 2. The wrong unit

Headlines count jobs. Technology usually changes the tasks and skills inside them first. That is where the advantage moves.

Callout: Technology doesn't destroy jobs. It democratises work by destroying skills.

### 3. The exoskeleton

Shipyard work once required the strength to wield heavy tools for hours. An exoskeleton makes the tool almost weightless.

Callout: The job stayed. Physical strength stopped being the price of entry.

### 4. The new baseline

The worker is safer. The employer gains a wider talent pool. But the skill that once made one person employable is no longer scarce.

Callout: What can the tool now do well enough for anyone?

### 5. Creativity and story

See the hidden meaning. Turn data into a clear story. Help other people understand, care and move.

Callout: The scarce skill is not producing more. It is seeing and expressing what matters.

### 6. Self-efficacy

Keep learning. Develop your resilience. Take responsibility for your career before the old advantage disappears.

Callout: How much of your lifetime learning budget did you spend before 24?

### 7. Leadership

The people and leadership gap is forecast to sit just behind technology by 2030.

Callout: A manager can affect your mental health as much as your partner.

### 8. Action

Use AI to elevate your thinking, not substitute it. If you need time to work out your opinion, create it.

Callout: Name the skill AI has made easier in your role. Then name what only you can add.
