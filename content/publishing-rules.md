# Publishing Rules

Live surfaces for the current pilot: `BET.md`. Do not treat LinkedIn, Instagram or TikTok as required cadence.

Hard rules for anything Harrison's name (or HWL Studio's name) goes on publicly.

## Hard nos

- No em dashes (—). Ever. Use commas, full stops, or rephrase. The `agents/agent-runner.sh` includes a sed safety net but cleaner LLM-side prose is better.
- No engagement bait. No "comment below" / "agree?" / "thoughts?" closes.
- No motivational fluff. No "you got this," "let's go," "I just dropped X."
- No AI slop. If a sentence could have been generated by any LLM in 2026, rewrite or kill it.
- No corporate celebration content. No box-ticking "milestones" or "team wins" posts.
- No "I just started a business 6 months ago" content. Codex H8: status risk with corporate clients.
- No "raw and real" content. The phrase itself is corporate cosplay.

## Hard yeses

- Specific over vague: named people (where consent), real numbers, real dates.
- British-inflected Kiwi voice. Short sentences. Quiet confidence.
- Personal performance with humility, not guru theatre.
- Field notes from real work, not abstract advice.
- Honest "this worked / this didn't" reflections.
- One genuine question at the end of long-form when it earns its place. Never as a hook.

## Voice DNA

See `content/voice-dna.md` for the opener / closer / phrases-used / phrases-never-used list distilled from 20 real sent emails. Match this for any outbound communication.

## Status risk handling (codex H8)

When writing about HWL Studio's work, business growth, or Harrison's journey:
- Don't overstate tenure.
- Don't undersell competence.
- Frame as "patterns observed from inside the work" not "I'm new and figuring this out."
- Examples of safe framing: "From three retainer clients in the legacy services space, the pattern is..." NOT "When I started my agency 6 months ago I didn't know..."

## Confidentiality

- No named clients without explicit prior consent for that specific piece.
- No internal strategy, board material, or commercially sensitive information.
- LOR-anchored case study material requires Korena-level approval for any external use.
- Generalised lessons + redacted examples = always safe.

## Approval surface

- Drafts in `content/pipeline.md` are okay to draft auto.
- Anything that goes "Ready to post" requires one explicit tap from Harrison before going live.
- Confirm visibility (LinkedIn personal vs HWL Studio page, X, newsletter, Substack) explicitly before posting.
