## The funnel (proposal to bring)

Two lanes, same content engine, one CTA per episode.

**Lane 1, individuals → Better Careers ($39)**
Shorts and LinkedIn (discovery) → podcast episode (trust) → free Sum Up / career-pitch template as lead magnet (email capture) → The Better Bits nurture → booklet. Career-themed episodes carry the booklet CTA. Fix the buying friction: one-tap checkout link, not a WooCommerce cart with a currency popup.

**Lane 2, organisations → speaking + Better Leadership**
This is where the money is. Cathal's LinkedIn is 40% senior decision-makers at Westpac, Goldman, CommBank, Laing O'Rourke. Leadership-themed episodes carry a "Cathal speaks / runs workshops" CTA → rebuilt speaking page (showreel, two named talks, logos, booking link) → discovery call. Better Leadership becomes the named corporate product the way Better Careers is the named individual one.

**Mechanics that make it real**
- 30-second house ads, produced like sponsor spots, one per episode, rotating between the two lanes. Recorded in the same sitting as the best-of cold opens.
- Every Sum Up is a lead magnet. Backfill the top 5 evergreen episodes first (Tupper, Eurich pull Spotify search traffic already).
- UTM links everywhere so by October there's an answer to "did the podcast sell anything".
- Test the CTAs on the summer best-of re-releases before S5. Free experiment on warm inventory.
