## 2. Writing platforms (LinkedIn + X, mid-2026)

Warning carried from the agent: this info space is polluted by AI SEO farms recycling each other's fake stats. Tiering preserved.

### LinkedIn

- 360Brew [SOLID]: LinkedIn replaced stacked recommenders with a ~150B-param decoder-only foundation model (late 2024 onward). Ranks by feeding ~2-3 months of member activity into the model. Social graph gave way to interest graph: topic consistency per account now compounds. [https://upgrowth.in/optimize-linkedin-2026-algorithm-360brew/]
- Dwell time = primary quality signal [SOLID directionally]. Comments >> likes, saves heavily weighted [direction credible, multipliers FARM].
- Formats [SOLID, Socialinsider benchmark, 1.3M posts, Mar 2026]: native documents/carousels 7.00% engagement (top), multi-image 6.45% (tops impressions under 50k followers), video 6.00% but views MINUS 36% YoY, image 5.30%, text 4.50%, polls reach-trap, links 3.25%. The "text is king again" meme is a farm artifact, ignore it.
- Video: the push cooled. Views down 36% YoY at every account size [SOLID]. Small talking-heads do NOT over-index. If posting video: native upload, 9:16, burned-in captions, hook in 2s.
- Cadence [SOLID, Buffer, 2M+ posts, Aug 2025]: 2-5 posts/week is the sweet spot. NO evidence higher frequency hurts reach. The "2x/day = 40% penalty" claim is farm junk. Minimum effective dose: 2-3x/week.
- Penalties: external links in body cost ~40-60% reach (use first comment) [SOLID directional]. LinkedIn publicly announced (May 2026) it restricts reach of content that "appears AI-generated and lacks clear perspective"; suppression is largely reader-behaviour driven (low dwell on generic prose). Voice is now an algorithmic asset. [https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/linkedin-wants-to-limit-the-reach-of-ai-generated-content/820935/] Pods detected and penalised. Hashtags irrelevant, 0-3 max.
- Conversion mechanics: ~70% of the B2B journey happens anonymously; ~95% of winning vendors were shortlisted before first contact [SOLID direction]. Cold DMs ignored (~79% claimed) and volume outreach suppresses the sender. The documented path: valuable comment on ICP posts, OP replies, DM, call. Daily commenting on the exact ICP is the underrated small-account lever.

### X

- Premium is near-mandatory [SOLID, Buffer, 18.8M posts]: Premium ~10x median reach of non-Premium; median regular account gets 0% engagement. Price of entry, not a hack. [https://buffer.com/resources/x-premium-review/]

- Reply-guy still the documented small-account path: 60-70% replies to accounts 5-20x your size, 30-40% original. [mechanism SOLID, numbers FARM]
- LLM queryability: Grok trains on and cites live X posts, the only major LLM doing so. One analysis claims Grok cites brands in 27% of answers vs ChatGPT 0.59% [UNVERIFIED]. Long-form X articles carry the durable declarative claims. Treat "found via LLM" as a real but unattributable tailwind, a low-cost bet, not a channel.

## 3. Video platforms (YouTube + IG + TikTok, mid-2026)

### YouTube

- Subscriber count is decoupled from distribution [SOLID consensus]: every upload gets tested with a potential-viewer pool regardless of channel size. Zero-sub channels get real shots.
- Hype [OFFICIAL]: 500-500k subs, in YPP, viewers get 3 free hypes/week within 7 days of publish, smaller channels get a points multiplier (strongest 500-10k), live in UK. A concrete small-creator boost the moment eligibility hits.
- Satisfaction surveys now feed ranking directly. "Quality CTR": strong CTR + weak first 15-30s retention is actively demoted. Protect the open ruthlessly.
- Test & Compare [OFFICIAL, rollout completed Dec 2025]: native A/B of up to 3 title+thumbnail packages, winner judged on watch-time-per-impression. Use on every long-form. [https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400]
- Long-form vs Shorts: SEPARATE algorithmic ecosystems since 2024. Shorts viewers convert to long-form subscribers at under 10% the rate. The "Shorts feed long-form" story is the weakest-supported claim in the corpus. Shorts = discovery + idea-testing only.
- Formats: unscripted talk-to-camera / conversation long-form is the standout growing format (YouTube is now the #1 podcast platform, ~33% of consumption). Low ceremony works at any production tier. Micro-specific beats broad for small channels.
- Niches: AI tools/automation/build-in-public = fastest-growing cluster (claimed +220-340%, FARM numbers, direction consistent). Running + London life = low-competition depth/identity niches, not accelerants.
- Cadence: 1 long-form/week on a predictable day is the documented solo floor. 8-15 min sweet spot. Return-viewer rate >10% is the early health signal.
- Service-business track: YouTube as search engine. Keyword-mapped buyer-question videos compound into evergreen inbound; documented 3-6 month lag; leads arrive pre-educated. Architecturally distinct from the identity/vlog track. Run both, don't conflate.

### Instagram

- Sends per reach (DM shares) = top ranking signal [Mosseri-confirmed]. Hierarchy: saves and shares > comments > likes. Design for "would someone DM this to a mate."
- Trial Reels [OFFICIAL]: publish to non-followers first, then decide. Free downside protection for small accounts.
- Reels under 3 min eligible for non-follower recommendation; 60-90s claimed engagement sweet spot [FARM].
- Originality crackdown [REPUTABLE, effective 30 Apr 2026]: 10+ reposts in 30 days = excluded from recommendations entirely. Watermarked/recycled content is dead. Original creators reportedly gained 40-60% reach. [https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/]
- Format split: Reels = discovery, carousels = engagement depth (saves + sends). "How I shot this" teaching carousels are the trust layer for a cinematic creator.
- Repurposing YouTube to Reels: clean NLE master, native re-cut with own hook/captions/pacing, never a raw dump, never a watermark (>50% reach cost claimed).

### TikTok

- US deal closed 22 Jan 2026 (TikTok USDS; Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX ~15% each). UK consumer app fully operational, only government devices banned. Platform viable. [https://itif.org/publications/2026/01/26/five-takeaways-from-the-tiktok-deal/]
- 2026 register: authenticity, process, BTS, personal connection over gloss [TikTok's own Discover List framing]. Restrained-cinematic works, polish is seasoning not spine.
- Photo-mode carousels: straight to FYP, less competitive than video FYP, text overlays indexed by TikTok search. A real small-account path.
- TikTok search is real behaviour (~40% of Gen Z default to social search for some queries): keywords in caption, on-screen text, and spoken words.
- Cross-posting: clean master per platform, short captions (<150 chars), 3-5 hashtags mixing broad + niche.
- Strategic: pick ONE primary platform and over-invest; spreading evenly across three from zero is the documented failure mode.

### Cross-cut findings

Post-2023 growers (Dalen, Koe, Noske) share four traits the legacy pioneers don't:
1. Value is ideas/access/POV, not spectacle. The moat moved from how it looks to what you know and what you're inside of.
2. One named repeatable format on a fixed slot. Removes the "what do I make" decision.
3. A real business is the subject. Content documents work happening anyway.
4. Product ladder, not brand-deal dependence.

Only two architectures survive on 5-8 hrs/week: Dalen's (capture the work you already do, one weekly wrapper, shorts from offcuts) and Koe's (one atomic idea per week, mechanically distributed). Everyone else's model assumes content IS the job.

Final synthesis: **Dalen's video mechanic + Koe's writing mechanic, pointed at Noske's business shape.** Person first, the real business as recurring subject, one repeatable format per side, cinematic craft rationed as quarterly positioning assets.
