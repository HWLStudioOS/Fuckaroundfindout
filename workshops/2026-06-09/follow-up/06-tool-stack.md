# The Tool Stack

Everything I named on the night, in order of how much it matters. Marked honestly. You need the first two. Everything else is optional and can wait.

## Core

**Claude Code (Anthropic) or Codex (OpenAI).** The engine. A chat in your terminal that reads and writes the files on your computer. Claude Code is the one I use daily and the one I demoed. Needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or above). Codex works with a paid ChatGPT plan. Install instructions in doc 01. If the terminal isn't for you yet, plain claude.ai or ChatGPT with Projects runs the same system (Path A).

**Wispr Flow (wisprflow.ai).** AI dictation, Mac and iPhone. You talk, it types, in your phrasing. This is the most underrated thing in my stack. Spoken prompts are longer, more specific and more you than typed ones. The interview prompt is twice as good if you dictate your answers. Free tier exists.

## Strongly worth it

**Obsidian (obsidian.md).** Free notes app that works on plain markdown files. Point it at your content-engine folder and it becomes the readable window onto your system. Not required, but it makes the folder feel like a real place rather than a pile of text files.

**Granola (granola.ai).** AI meeting notes. Sits in the background of your calls and produces notes and transcripts. Every client call becomes a source of content ideas, in the actual words your clients use. Has an MCP, so Claude can read your meetings directly.

**Buffer (buffer.com).** Scheduling. One place to queue posts for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and X. Has an MCP too, which is how my drafts go from Claude to scheduled without me touching the apps.

## Optional, my personal picks

**Superhuman.** Email. Fast, expensive, not for everyone.

**Comet (Perplexity's browser).** A browser with AI built in. Handy for research, by no means essential.

**Figma or Canva.** Design. Carousels, decks, thumbnails. Canva is the friendlier start, and it has a connector so the AI can work in it with you.

**Premiere Pro or CapCut.** Video editing. CapCut is free and enough until it isn't.

## The point

The stack is not the system. I could lose everything below the core list and the engine would still run, because the engine is the four files plus one idea a fortnight. Buy tools to remove friction you have actually felt, not friction you imagine.
