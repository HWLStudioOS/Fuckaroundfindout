## The client in three lines

Laing O'Rourke, ~£5bn construction, HQ Dartford. HWL Studio is strategic communications advisor, largest client, roughly £7-10k a month across several streams. This brief covers one stream only: the Early Talent 2026 recruitment campaign plus its Everton induction day satellite.

## The campaign in one paragraph

Recruit graduates, apprentices and school leavers into four priority roles: structural engineering, building services (protected priority, hardest to fill), design management, procurement. Campaign idea: "More than you think", proof over slogans. Objective is fewer, better applicants; last cycle drew 20,000+ applications of poor quality. LinkedIn leads, Instagram for discovery, Facebook aimed at parents. Roles go live the week of 28 September 2026 and the whole calendar anchors to that.
