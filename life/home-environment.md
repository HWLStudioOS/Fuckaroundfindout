# Home and Environment

**Canonical source:** `/Users/harrison/hwlstudio-codex/harrison-os/16-relationships-home-environment.md`

## Current setup

Desk in bedroom, London apartment (Hackney / Tudor Grove). Nice setup. 4K monitor. Desk speakers. Webcam. PlayStation. Comfortable. Feels like his because he built it.

## What helps

- Ownership.
- Comfort.
- Good equipment.
- Familiarity.

## What drags

- Too much time at home.
- Too little random luck.
- Too few ambient creative / social inputs.
- No commute or natural external exposure.
- Fewer chances to run into people, film content, create unexpected opportunities.
- Being at home all day and all night every week is becoming difficult.

## Environment rule (codex H18, engineered serendipity)

Weekly minimum target:
- One coffee shop work block.
- One Soho House work block (he has access, doesn't use it enough).
- One intentional meeting, filming opportunity, or outside-the-flat creative session.

Not indulgence. Business development, mood regulation, creative input.

## Tracking

Weekly review (`agents/weekly-review.md`) checks the three blocks above happened. If three Sundays in a row show zero, the system surfaces it as a flag.

## Mac setup

- **MacBook Pro:** main AI orchestration machine.
- **Mac Mini:** focus station for HWL production work (Premiere, Figma) and the host for unattended agents (morning-brief, weekly-review, weekly-cfo via launchd).
