## Editorial spine

1. Space feels fixed. It is one of the few things at work you can actually change, and you do not need a budget or a designer.
2. Three needs come before any design decision: agency, growth and connection.
3. A say over your surroundings is not soft. In the nursing-home study it tracked with survival, and small acts of control spill into the rest of life.
4. Your office is already sending a message. Cubicles versus corner offices, and the conference room nobody dares use, tell people how much control they really have.
5. The most damaging mistake is jumping to layout, paint and technology before the three needs.
6. Free moves: give the team the room and let them set it up, be intentional about the friendship factories, move the couch nobody sits on, and go somewhere new tomorrow.

Honesty note: the cut proves agency and connection directly. Growth is named on air but not developed in this conversation. The copy names all three needs as the book's model and lets agency carry the argument, which is what Klotz does himself.
