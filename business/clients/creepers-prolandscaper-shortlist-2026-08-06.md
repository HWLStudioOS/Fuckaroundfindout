- The advert sells the plants Creepers supply, not the design or installation side.
- Hard rule from Rob: no houses. Minimal buildings overall.
- Sky-brightening edits are allowed, so a flat sky does not disqualify an otherwise strong shot.

- Brand posture: restraint over decoration. Quality of plant material, texture, light.
