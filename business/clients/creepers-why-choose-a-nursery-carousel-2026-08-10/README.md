## Caption

The useful part of a nursery visit is the conversation before the order is fixed.

Sometimes the right answer is a different size. Sometimes it is a better-grown specimen, a more suitable variety, or simply waiting until the right plant is ready.

After 38 years, we know when to say yes, when to suggest another route and when not to sell you the wrong plant.

Send the office your list before you visit and we'll prepare the rows.

Surrey · London · New Forest
creepersnursery.co.uk

## Slide copy

1. **Why choose a nursery?**
2. **See the plant.**
   A photograph, label and pot size only tell you so much. Walk the rows and choose the plant that actually suits the job.
3. **Choose from real stock.**
   Compare form, size and condition side by side. No catalogue guesswork.
4. **Ask someone who knows it.**
   You get an honest answer on suitability, timing and what will establish well.
5. **Bring us the list.**
   We will walk the rows with you.
