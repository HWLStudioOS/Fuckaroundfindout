## Why this cut

The first rough cut carried five related ideas: hope, layoffs, reskilling, small goals and micromanagement. The result was accurate but assembled. It moved through too many arguments and ran for 09:50.

V2 stays on one line: what leaders do that creates or destroys hope.

1. Leaders need goals, more than one pathway and enough agency to move.
2. Young people cannot invest in a future they are being told will not include them.
3. Small, visible goals restore momentum.
4. Micromanagement removes agency and makes hope impossible.

The layoffs and reskilling material has been removed from the long cut. It remains available for a separate short.
