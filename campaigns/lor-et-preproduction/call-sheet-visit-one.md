## Structural shot list, P10 and P12

1. Receive the genuine input, wide.
2. Receive it in hand or on screen, tight.
3. Identify the correct drawing, model or revision.
4. Compare it with the live site condition.
5. Measure, calculate or inspect.
6. Use the actual tool or document.
7. Mark or comment on the item.
8. Complete the genuine check or sign-off.
9. Discuss the finding with a colleague.
10. Make or explain the decision.
11. Record or communicate the result.
12. Pass the real output to the next role.

**Non-negotiable:** P12 shows the actual check, mark or sign-off. Generic walking footage cannot replace it. If final authority belongs to a senior person, the film must not imply that the early-career lead owns it.

## Site day-in-the-life shot list, P16

1. Genuine arrival.
2. PPE or pre-start preparation.
3. Review the day's task or plan.
4. Move to the real work area.
5. Perform the owned task, wide.
6. Hands, tool or detail of the task.
7. Make one decision.
8. Interact with a colleague.
9. Receive an input.
10. Respond to a live or recent problem. Never stage a safety issue.
11. Create or hand on the output.
12. End-of-task transition and reflection.

## P10 continuity log

For every genuine interface:

- Capture wide, medium and tight.
- Capture both sides where they really occur.
- Record up to three takes with five-second handles only for rehearsed or non-transactional movement that can be repeated safely.
- Capture real approvals, submissions, sends and sign-offs once. Use safe cutaways before and after. Never create a duplicate live transaction for the camera.
- Log artefact name and revision, sender, receiver, hand or screen direction, camera height, framing, PPE and action start/end.
- Take a reference still for visit two.
- If the interface is digital, film the real send/receive workflow safely. Do not invent a physical baton.

If the one-project test passes but the literal chain does not, collect the structural decision and site consequence for Four Decisions. If the one-project test fails, stop any P10 claim and escalate the copy decision.

## Before each person leaves

- Clean audio.
