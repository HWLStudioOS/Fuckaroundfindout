# Dream Body Hybrid, block 1

This is the only active training plan. Every previous active endurance prescription has been deleted.

## The honest target

Starting point, 15 July 2026:

- Body mass: approximately 87 kg.
- Waist: 34 inches.
- Garmin VO2max: approximately 45.
- Historical PBs, currently untrained: squat 145 kg, bench 110 kg, deadlift 180 kg.
- Physique priorities: delts, chest, abs and arms.
- Current recovery state: D+4 after a hot 50K. Right foot pain 3/10 with recent bruising and a nearly-resolved limp. Quad and upper-hip-flexor soreness 2-3/10. No swelling, Achilles pain, calf pain, shin pain, knee pain, ITB pain, hip-joint pain, back pain, urine change or systemic weakness.

Twelve-week outcome targets:

- Lose 4.5-6.5 kg while preserving or adding lean mass. A finish around 81-83 kg is the performance-compatible target. Reaching 80 kg is possible, not mandatory, and will not be forced through an excessive deficit.
- Move the waist towards 32 inches.
- Re-establish repeatable strength training and rebuild estimated strength without testing true 1RMs during the block.
- Establish a real 5K baseline in week 4, race in week 8, and race again in week 12.
- Improve pace at a fixed easy aerobic effort. Sub-6:00/km is an output, not a pace to force.

Sub-20 for 5K means 4:00/km. It may take more than one block. Garmin VO2max rising by 15 points in 12 weeks is not a credible planning assumption. The first 5K test decides the gap.

## Priority order

1. Clear the post-ultra foot and tissue recovery gates.
2. Lose fat at a rate that does not degrade sleep, libido, strength or running quality.
3. Rebuild compound strength and add upper-body hypertrophy volume.
4. Improve VO2max, threshold and running economy while retaining a modest aerobic base.
5. Build a broad movement practice without letting novelty steal recovery from the first four priorities.

## Non-negotiable safety rules

- The right foot is currently a red light for impact. No running or loaded lower-body lifting while normal walking pain is 3/10 or a limp remains.
- Garmin readiness never clears a local tissue problem.
- Dark urine, reduced urine output, unexpectedly severe muscle pain or swelling, marked weakness, one-sided calf swelling, chest pain or shortness of breath require urgent medical assessment.
- New focal bony tenderness in the bruised foot, pain that worsens with hopping, renewed swelling, or failure to improve by D+7 requires a sports-physio or medical assessment before running.
- No routine ibuprofen around endurance training. A randomised ultramarathon trial found more acute kidney injury with ibuprofen.
- No new Marchon conditioning, HYROX work, metcons or extra running may be layered over this plan. Exercises can be substituted. Whole sessions cannot be stacked.
- If recovery delays a gate, slide the plan. Do not compress missed work into later days.

# Part 2, readiness tests

These are conservative operational tests. No study has validated one perfect post-50K return-to-run battery.

## Running clearance

All items must pass on the same day:

1. Foot pain is 0-1/10 on the first ten morning steps and during normal walking.
2. No swelling, visible limp, protective gait or focal bony tenderness.
3. Brisk flat walk for 30 minutes with pain no higher than 1/10.
4. Walk up and down one flight of stairs twice without compensation.
5. Twenty controlled single-leg calf raises per side, full comfortable range.
6. Ten controlled step-downs per side from a 15-20 cm step.
7. Twenty small double-leg pogos, then ten gentle single-leg hops per side.
8. Pain remains 0-1/10 during the test, two hours later and on the next morning.

Failure means no running. Repeat the failed test after 48 hours. Focal foot pain on hopping is a clinical-assessment trigger, not something to train through.

## Lower-body strength clearance

All items must pass:

1. Running clearance has passed or the session is explicitly non-impact and upper-body only.
2. Ten bodyweight squats to a comfortable full depth.
3. Eight reverse lunges or split squats per side.
4. Ten unloaded hip hinges.
5. Twenty calf raises and fifteen bent-knee soleus raises per side.
6. Thirty-second front plank and twenty-second side plank per side.
7. No pain above 1/10, loss of balance, altered depth or offloading.
8. No increase in symptoms later that day or the next morning.

The first two lower-body sessions use RIR 4 and two work sets. Historical PBs do not set the load.

## Daily traffic light

| Status | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Local pain 0-1/10, no swelling or limp, sleep at least 7 hours, ordinary motivation, no illness. | Complete the planned session. |
| Amber | Local pain 2/10, DOMS above 4/10, sleep below 6.5 hours, RHR about 5 bpm above baseline, or HRV trend down roughly 7% or more. | Remove intensity and reduce sets or duration by about 30%. Easy aerobic or technique only. |
| Red | Pain at least 3/10, swelling, limp, focal bony pain, illness, severe fatigue, dark urine, or symptoms worsening across 24 hours. | No impact or loaded lower work. Assess the cause. Medical red flags override everything. |

# Part 3, the 12-week hybrid block

## Weekly structure

| Day | Session | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Strength A, full body heavy | Squat and bench strength, posterior chain, chest and delts. |
| Tuesday | Easy run plus Movement A | Aerobic base, foot and ankle capacity. |
| Wednesday AM | VO2 or 5K-specific run | Central aerobic power and 5K speed. |
| Wednesday PM | Strength B, upper hypertrophy | Chest, delts, back and arms without more leg fatigue. Keep at least 6 hours between sessions. |
| Thursday | Easy bike or complete rest plus Movement B/C | Recovery, hips, spine and shoulders. Bike is optional, RPE 2 only. |
| Friday AM | Strength C, full body | Hinge strength, unilateral legs, chest, back, calves and abs. |
| Friday PM | Threshold or steady run | Sustainable speed. Keep at least 6 hours after lifting and refuel between sessions. |
| Saturday | Walk plus Movement D, or complete rest | Floor work, locomotion and skill. Never conditioning. |
| Sunday | Long easy run | Preserve the running base and improve aerobic durability. |

Race weeks replace Friday Strength C and threshold with a short primer. Sunday becomes rest or a 20-30 minute recovery jog.

## Running intensity language

- Easy and long: RPE 2-3, full-sentence conversation. Use a provisional ceiling around 145-150 bpm, but let the talk test win if Garmin zones are wrong.
- Steady: RPE 4-5, clearly controlled.
- Threshold: RPE 7, strong but repeatable, short phrases possible, never a time trial.
- VO2: RPE 8-9 during work repetitions. Form stays clean. Heart rate should rise towards 90-95% of maximum late in the set, not in the first repetition.
- Strides: relaxed acceleration, fast and smooth, full recovery, never sprinting.
- After the week-4 5K, interval pace uses the current result, not the desired 4:00/km goal pace.

## Strength sessions

RIR means repetitions in reserve. Every repetition should look repeatable. No grinders and no failure on compound lifts.

### Strength A, Monday

1. Back squat, 3 x 4-6.
2. Bench press, 4 x 4-6.
3. Romanian deadlift, 3 x 6-8.
4. Chest-supported row, 3 x 8-12.
5. Cable or dumbbell lateral raise, 3 x 12-20.
6. Standing calf raise, 3 x 8-12 with a controlled full range.
7. Ab wheel, 3 x 6-12.

### Strength B, Wednesday PM

1. Incline dumbbell or barbell press, 4 x 6-10.
2. Pull-up or chin-up, 4 x 5-8. Add load only after clean bodyweight sets.
3. Seated dumbbell overhead press, 3 x 6-10.
4. Cable row, 3 x 8-12.
5. Cable fly, 2 x 10-15.
6. Lateral raise, 4 x 12-20.
7. Incline curl plus rope pressdown, 3 x 8-15 each.
8. Active hang or scapular pull-up practice from Movement C.

### Strength C, Friday AM

1. Deadlift, preferably conventional unless the foot or back prefers trap bar, 3 x 3-5.
2. Front-foot-elevated split squat, 3 x 6-10 per side.
3. Machine chest press or weighted dip, 3 x 6-10.
4. Neutral-grip lat pulldown, 3 x 8-12.
5. Reverse cable fly, 3 x 12-20.
6. Seated soleus raise, 3 x 10-15.
7. Hammer curl plus overhead cable triceps extension, 2 x 8-15 each.
8. Cable crunch or hanging knee raise, 3 x 8-15.

### Set-volume rules by phase

- Week 1: perform two work sets for every listed exercise, RIR 4.
- Week 2: use the listed sets on main lifts, two sets on accessories, RIR 3.
- Week 3: use the listed sets, RIR 2-3.
- Week 4: halve normal work sets, RIR 4.
- Weeks 5-7: use listed sets. Final main-lift set may reach RIR 2. Accessories may reach RIR 1 on the final set.
- Week 8: halve sets and omit Strength C.
- Weeks 9-10: main lifts use one top set in the listed range at RIR 1-2, then two back-off sets with 5-8% less load. No failure.
- Week 11: reduce lower-body sets by 25%.
- Week 12: two short upper-biased maintenance sessions. No heavy lower work after Monday.

### Load progression

- First exposure: choose a load that leaves at least four clean repetitions. Historical PB percentages are not used.
- When every set reaches the top of its rep range at the required RIR, add 2-2.5 kg to upper-body barbell lifts or the smallest available increment, and 2.5-5 kg to lower-body lifts.
- Log load, reps and RIR for every work set. Estimated 1RM is calculated from submaximal sets after week 3.
- Do not test a true 1RM during this block. A 3-5RM test can be scheduled after week 12 if the next block prioritises strength.

## Concurrent-training interference controls

Concurrent work is not a reason to avoid either strength or running. It is a reason to control the dose.

- Keep at least six hours between Wednesday and Friday double sessions.
- Wednesday PM is upper-body only after the VO2 session.
- Friday lifting occurs before threshold because strength quality has priority on that double. Refuel between sessions.
- Only two sessions carry meaningful lower-body resistance volume.
- There are no metcon finishers.
- Deload weeks 4 and 8, then reduce lower volume in week 11.
- If the Friday threshold session degrades for two consecutive weeks, move it to Saturday and replace Saturday movement with rest.
- If strength stalls while body mass is falling faster than 0.7 kg per week, increase food before adding training.

# Part 4, movement practice

Movement is integrated as skill, tissue capacity and range. It is never used to create more fatigue.

## Movement A, feet, ankles and knees, 10-12 minutes

1. Toe yoga, 2 x 8 each pattern.
2. Short-foot hold, 3 x 20 seconds per side.
3. Tibialis raise, 2 x 15-20.
4. Slow single-leg calf raise, 3 x 8-12.
5. Bent-knee soleus raise, 2 x 12-15.
6. Controlled step-down, 2 x 8 per side.
7. Spanish-squat isometric, 3 x 30-45 seconds only if patellar pain returns and the position reduces it.

Foot-core training has direct randomised evidence for reducing running-injury incidence. Calf strength and later plyometrics have evidence for running economy. Spanish-squat-style isometrics are an analgesic option, not a universal tendon cure.

## Movement B, hips and spine, 10-15 minutes

1. Hip controlled articular rotation, 3 slow circles per side.
2. 90/90 hip switch, 2 x 6 per side.
3. Adductor rock-back, 1 x 8 per side.
4. Cossack squat, 2 x 5 per side, initially bodyweight.
5. Segmental cat-cow, 6 slow cycles.
6. Open-book thoracic rotation, 1 x 6 per side.
7. Controlled spinal roll-down, unloaded, 2 x 5 only if symptom-free.

Full-range resistance training can improve both strength and range. The specific flow sequence is a practical movement dose, not proof of better recovery.

## Movement C, shoulders and hanging, 8-12 minutes

1. Scapular circles, 1 x 6 each direction.
2. Wall slide, 2 x 8.
3. Assisted or full passive hang, accumulate 60-120 seconds.
4. Active hang or scapular pull-up, 3 x 5 with a short pause.
5. Ring support or push-up plus, 3 x 15-25 seconds or 2 x 10.

Ido Portal's official challenge prescribes seven accumulated minutes of hanging. This plan does not adopt that dose. Hanging starts at 60-120 seconds because shoulder tolerance is individual and direct evidence for a seven-minute health dose is absent.

## Movement D, locomotion and floor work, 20-30 minutes

Practise at RPE 2-3 with clean breathing:

1. Bear crawl, forward, backward and lateral.
2. Crab reach and controlled crab travel.
3. Ape step or low lateral squat travel.
4. Shin-box transition and shin-box get-up.
5. Technical stand-up.
6. Side roll and shoulder roll on a suitable mat.
7. Low bridge rotation only after shoulder and spine control are established.
8. Link two or three patterns into an easy 60-90 second flow, then rest.

This is Ido-inspired practice. It develops vocabulary, coordination, floor confidence and enjoyment. It is not claimed to speed muscle-damage recovery, prevent running injury or build VO2max.

## Movement progression

| Weeks | Progression |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Slow control, supported ranges, short-foot work, assisted hangs, isolated crawl patterns. No loaded spinal flexion or reactive hopping outside the run-readiness test. |
| 5-8 | Add load to calf, soleus, Cossack and step-down patterns. Build active hangs. Link two floor patterns. Add low-volume pogos twice weekly if the foot is fully quiet. |
| 9-12 | Progress pogo and hop quality, not exhaustion. Add crawling direction changes, longer floor links and controlled swing only if the shoulders are pain-free and experienced. |

## Evidence-backed versus Ido-inspired

| Practice | Classification | How it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Progressive resistance training | Evidence-backed | Primary driver of strength and hypertrophy. |
| VO2 intervals and threshold running | Evidence-backed | Primary drivers of aerobic power and sustainable speed. |
| Foot-core strengthening | Evidence-backed | Three weekly exposures, integrated into warm-ups. |
| Heavy calf work and later plyometrics | Evidence-backed | Plantar-flexor strength and running-economy support. |
| Progressive knee loading | Evidence-backed in principle | Step-downs, squats and split squats, symptom-led. Isometrics are optional analgesia. |
| Full-range loaded movement | Evidence-backed in principle | Strength and usable range are trained together. |
| Deep-squat accumulation | Ido-inspired | Optional 2-5 total minutes, varied and pain-free. The 30/30 challenge is not prescribed. |
| Passive and active hanging | Ido-inspired with plausible strength utility | Low initial dose. Progress only with healthy shoulders. |
| Locomotion and floor flow | Ido-inspired | Skill, coordination and enjoyment. Not recovery treatment. |
| Segmental spinal flow | Ido-inspired | Unloaded control first. No claim that spinal waves prevent injury. |
| Movement instead of medical assessment | Rejected | Red flags and focal injury receive appropriate assessment. |

# Tracking and review

## Daily

- Morning body mass after bathroom, before food. Use the seven-day mean.
- Sleep duration and subjective quality.
- Foot, knee and other pain before training, after training and next morning.
- Session completed, modified or skipped, with reason.
- Every lifting work set: exercise, load, reps and RIR.
- Every run: duration, distance, average pace, average HR, RPE and session type.

## Weekly

- Waist at the navel, same morning conditions.
- Seven-day mean weight and weekly change.
- Total running distance and hard-running minutes.
- Strength volume by movement and muscle group.
- Pace at easy aerobic HR, interpreted over several comparable runs.
- One front, side and back progress photo if useful. This is optional and private.

## Tests

- Week 2: 45-minute flat easy run at approximately 145-150 bpm. Compare first-half and second-half pace. Target less than 5% pace decay at similar HR.
- Week 4: baseline 5K and submaximal estimated strength from logged sets.
- Week 8: 5K B-race, waist, body-mass trend and lift review.
- Week 12: 5K A-race, waist, body mass, photos and lift review.
