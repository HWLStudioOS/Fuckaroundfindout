## Live commercial position, 18 August 2026

**The number: £3,400 a month plus VAT. Twelve months, review at the end of the first quarter.**

Why £3,400: their stated all-in annual podcast spend is £45-50k including studio. With Podcast House moving to studio hire only (room paid direct to Jack, everything else HWL), £3,400 (£40.8k a year) plus a realistic room line (~£8.5-11k) keeps them at what the podcast already costs. The pitch is "same budget, redeployed into the care you said was missing": zero budget increase to approve. It is also Harrison's floor from the 27 July range, held exactly, deployed as the confident answer rather than the retreat. £3,650 needed a stretch argument nobody asked for; £4,100 ignores the room line. £3,600 remains the stretch option (~£52-54k all-in) at the cost of the same-budget story; one-word edit in the draft if Harrison wants it.

**What the £3,400 buys (phase one, in):**

- Harrison runs the monthly Farringdon recording day as producer: timestamps, retakes, intro and outro checks in the room. Podcast House is studio hire only; whatever desk support (Joseph) comes with the room is PH's bundle.
- All eight episodes (4 Borrowed Brilliance ~30 min, 4 Shortcuts ~10 min) edited by Harrison personally with the usefulness filter, the Conor standard they named. One consolidated feedback round per episode through Sarah Massie.
- Publishing and distribution: Megaphone, Apple, Spotify, YouTube. Titles, descriptions, scheduling, Squiggly Careers as headline brand throughout. Replaces PH's £600 publishing line.
- Editorial input on formats, openings, pacing.
- Monthly performance report: plays vs downloads, watch time and drop-off, listener locations, with the Acast history (the lost 5.75M lifetime view) stitched together with Megaphone.
- Music refresh proposal in month one (same music since 2017, they called it dated but scary).
- Weekly timestamped hooks and pull-quotes to Sarah M for her podcast social slots in Airtable.
- Month-ahead buffer with explicit licence for reactive swaps.

**Out, deliberately:**

- **Thumbnails and all design.** Stays with Jen. They pulled design in-house because vendor creative kept landing off-brand; Jen is their fix and she is beloved. HWL publishes with the assets she supplies. If they ask for thumbnails back, it is a priced add per the July packaging logic, never folded in at £3,400.
- **Clips and trailers.** Their own year of testing says carousels beat clips. Phase-two experiment if ever.
- **Extra social capture or photography on recording days.** Priced separately if wanted.
- Studio hire (theirs, direct to PH), second feedback rounds, rush work from late source delivery.

**Comparison that lands it:** PH's £2,600 was eight minimal edits plus publishing, no thumbnails either, nobody in the room, no editorial, no reporting. The £800 difference buys the entire care layer.

**Workload and margin:** roughly 5 to 6 days a month all-in, £570-680 a day effective. Thin-ish by design; the margin story is the ladder, not phase one.

**The ladder (client-authored, additional money per Helen's own framing):**

- **Phase two, growth**, from the first-quarter review (~December): retained growth support built on three months of clean data. Indicative £1,000-1,500 a month on top. They asked "how do we grow" unprompted.
- **Phase three, brand and web:** their web support is a favour-in-the-evenings arrangement and Sarah E flagged interest in HWL's capability directly. Squiggly site and brand programme at BaW scale is a £10-20k project lane, plus possible retained web. BaW S5 relaunch (15 Sept) is the live portfolio piece, teased on the call and in the draft.
- Plausible relationship value by next summer: £5-6.5k a month equivalent. Phase one alone takes HWL's annualised run rate from ~£132k to ~£173k.

**Money rules:** £3,400 is the floor in any shape. Nothing extra folded in silently; scope moves with the number. VAT assumed neutral to them (established ltd), keep "plus VAT" explicit and don't raise it.
