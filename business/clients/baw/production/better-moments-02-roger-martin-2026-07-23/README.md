## Editorial shape

The edit follows one argument:

1. Strategy is what you do, not what you say.
2. Planning deals in initiatives. Strategy deals in choices.
3. "What would have to be true?" turns disagreement into a test.
4. AI gives the most frequent answer, not the distinctive one.

The hardest-task section and acquisition story are deliberately excluded. Both are good, but they pull the episode away from the strategy spine and beyond the 12 to 14 minute brief.

The social short deliberately comes from the wider Roger session rather than the 13:43 master. The AI moment and the central strategy definition have already been posted, while "What would have to be true?" is already present in the week's value carousel. The inputs-versus-outputs excerpt gives the audience a genuinely new Roger idea without changing the Better Moment itself.
