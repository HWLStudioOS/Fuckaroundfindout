## Items

- **Loop Engineering**, essay, Addy Osmani. [https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/](https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/)
  Published June 7. Named the pattern Boris Cherny (Claude Code creator) and Peter Steinberger both described: stop prompting agents turn-by-turn, design loops that do the prompting for you. Osmani defines it as "replacing yourself as the person who prompts the agent." The loop combines scheduled execution, isolated workspaces, a verifier agent, and persistent memory. The key design principle: one maker agent plus one checker agent with different instructions, because a model grading its own output is too generous. Applies directly to Harrison OS: the morning-brief, weekly-cfo, and weekly-review agents currently run as prompt sequences. The next move is /loop plus /goal plus a verifiable stopping condition checked by a separate agent.

- **The next big breakthrough will be AIs learning on the job**, essay, Dwarkesh Patel. [https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/the-next-paradigm](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/the-next-paradigm)
  Published June 26. The argument: RL on verifiable tasks produces agents competent enough to deploy in real environments. Once deployed, they accumulate experience far beyond the verifiable training domains. By end 2027, effective context lengths may span a full week of wall-clock time. You give a thumbs up or down at the end of a week; the base model distills what the agent learned during that session. Continual learning from real-world deployment eventually replaces synthetic training runs as the primary improvement mechanism. Non-obvious claim: RLVR is not the destination, it is the runway that makes real-world experience compoundable. The labs will not fully control capability improvements once deployment experience becomes the training signal.

- **Ada Palmer, Machiavelli is the most misunderstood thinker of all time**, podcast, Dwarkesh Patel. [https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/ada-palmer-2](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/ada-palmer-2)
  Published June 16. 2h 8min. Ada Palmer is a historian at the University of Chicago. The corrective to the caricature: Machiavelli was not indifferent to the means. He was obsessed with them. How you acquire power determines how stable and how fruitful that power will be. The Prince was written as a job application to the Medici regime that had just tortured and exiled him. Cesare Borgia's brand of terror-plus-justice is described not as a template but as a case study in the relationship between method and durability. Also: during the Renaissance, original ideas had to be couched in the language of antiquity to survive. Any contemporary claim you have seen dressed in classical reference is doing the same thing Machiavelli was doing. Directly relevant to Art of War, which is Harrison's current reading. Worth listening before finishing it. Where Sun Tzu says win without fighting, Machiavelli says the way you fight determines whether the win compounds or collapses.

- **SATISFY HeatCrush**, essay, Believe in the Run. [https://believeintherun.com/satisfy-heatcrush/](https://believeintherun.com/satisfy-heatcrush/)
  Published June 12. Satisfy's new hot-weather system. The fabric physics is the interesting part: HeatCrush inverts the usual relationship between effort and overheating. Sweat draws heat away from the skin; airflow amplifies the cooling response. FLIR thermography testing showed 8 degrees Celsius surface temperature drop over 30 minutes, vs 3.1 degrees for standard polyester under identical conditions. The claim is the response has no ceiling: the harder you push, the harder it works. Every piece in the capsule is hand dip-dyed in a century-old Japanese dye house. Satisfy Oakley Chapter VII collaboration dropped June 25 globally. Relevant for RTTS 50K prep in summer conditions. The broader point is that Satisfy's R&D pipeline (MothTech, AcroFuse seams, now HeatCrush) has produced genuinely engineered fabric three cycles running, which is why technical credibility has held even as adidas and Oakley have absorbed the brand into their co-branding.

## Synthesis

- **Abstraction keeps rising**: Two items from the tech stream point at the same shift from different angles. Osmani names loop engineering as the new practitioner skill: the work moved from writing code, to writing prompts, to designing loops that prompt autonomously. Dwarkesh argues the improvement mechanism in AI itself is moving from synthetic training runs to real-world deployment experience. Both are claiming the previous layer becomes table stakes and the action moves up. For Harrison OS: the agents are currently at the prompting layer. The loop engineering move would take them to the automation layer, where agents schedule and verify themselves rather than waiting for a manual trigger. The /loop, /goal, and cron infrastructure already exists in Claude Code. Sources: [https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/](https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/), [https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/the-next-paradigm](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/the-next-paradigm).

- **The means determine the stability**: Ada Palmer's Machiavelli thesis and Durant's recurring claim in Lessons of History converge on the same structural point without citing each other. Machiavelli: how you acquire power determines how stable and fruitful that power will be. Durant: wealth concentration is interrupted periodically by legislation or revolution, and the means of interruption determine whether the transition produces order or destroys it. Both are saying the quality of the outcome is baked into the method, not just the stated goal. Applies to how HWL Studio wins clients. Referral acquires at higher effort per client but compounds into relationships that stay. Cold outreach wins faster but produces fragile relationships. The means are not incidental to the outcome. They are the mechanism. Sources: [https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/ada-palmer-2](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/ada-palmer-2).

## Recall drills

Q6 and Q10 are the two drills from the Durant queue not previously surfaced in any brief.

1. Q: What did the Durants reject about race as a historical driver, and what did they offer instead?
   A: (hidden, expand after attempt) They rejected racial determinism. Civilisations rise from geographic and economic accident, not racial superiority. Geography shapes economy, economy shapes culture, culture shapes character.
   Source: The Lessons of History, Ch. 4 "Race and History."

2. Q: Durant's line on the conservatism of history. What is the warning about new ideas?
   A: (hidden) Out of every hundred new ideas, ninety-nine or more will be inferior to the traditional responses they propose to replace. Tradition is selection over time, not stupidity.
   Source: The Lessons of History, Ch. 5 "Character and History."

3. Q: Durant's two oscillating poles of political history. Name them and state the trade-off.
   A: (hidden) Freedom and equality. Gain one and you lose the other. Every era picks its emphasis and pays the cost of the other. No stable middle point exists.
   Source: The Lessons of History, Ch. 9 "Socialism and History."

4. Q: What are the three biological lessons of history per Durant?
   A: (hidden) Life is competition. Life is selection. Life must breed.
   Source: The Lessons of History, Ch. 3 "Biology and History."
