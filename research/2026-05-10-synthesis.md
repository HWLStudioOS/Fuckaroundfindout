# Path to £11k/month Semi-Passive in 12 Months: Synthesis

**Date:** 10 May 2026
**Inputs:** Three parallel research agents (archetypes, case studies, wedges)
**Goal:** £11k/month semi-passive income within 12 months as parallel income stream to existing HWL Studio consulting
**Constraints:** 6-10 hrs/wk, £15-25k investable capital, no existing prosumer audience, no employees

---

## The convergent finding

Three independent research streams converge on the same answer.

**For these specific constraints, the highest-probability path is a productised B2B retainer at £1,500-£3,000/month, sold to 5-10 customers, with AI doing 70-80% of delivery and Harrison doing the strategic and relationship layer.**

NOT a prosumer SaaS. NOT an info product. NOT a content/audience compound. NOT a high-ticket-Blueprint-only path.

Reasons in detail below.

---

## Why "starting from zero" is the wrong frame

Agent 2 verified the actual numbers behind the "solo founder hits $1m ARR" stories. Almost all collapse on verification:

- Pieter Levels had 350k+ Twitter followers when Photo AI launched
- Marc Lou was Product Hunt Maker of the Year before DataFast
- Adam Robinson built RB2B's $1m ARR in 16 weeks on a 92k LinkedIn audience from his prior company
- Tony Dinh had ~30k followers when TypingMind shipped
- The most-cited "$1m ARR in 30 days with AI" stories (Polsia / Ben Broca, "Sarah Chen", "Alex Chen") have no primary sources and are likely fiction or composites

The cleanest verified "no audience" case is Damon Chen (Testimonial.to + PDF.ai), who took 9 months to first £100k ARR with <1k followers at launch.

**Critical insight:** Harrison's existing position (LOR case study, Colin Fisher relationship, Creepers brand work, HWL Studio positioning, senior network in London tech and construction) is materially more valuable than what most verified operators had at launch. The "starting from zero" framing was wrong. The recommended path leverages this position rather than ignoring it.

---

## Why the constraints force the answer

Agent 1's quantitative analysis:

- Anything requiring 30+ customers needs platform infrastructure (18+ months to build)
- At 6-10 hrs/wk, customer count maxes around 10-15
- Therefore ACV must be £1,500+ to clear £11k/month with that customer count

This rules out:
- Prosumer SaaS at $20-50/month
- Info products / courses / cohorts at one-off pricing
- Content platforms requiring scale
- Marketplaces (network effects too slow for 12 months)

This rules in:
- Productised B2B retainers at £1.5-5k/month
- Vertical AI agents for boring industries
- Managed services with AI delivery layer
- Specialised consulting wrapped as a fixed-fee engagement

## The two AI capability shifts that actually matter for Harrison's plays

From Agent 1's capability ledger, two unlocks are load-bearing for the recommended portfolio:

1. **Voice-faithful AI cleared the senior-person quality bar in late 2025.** Whisper-3, Claude 4.x voice mode, ElevenLabs v3. This makes voice memo → polished long-form output a one-operator workflow.
2. **AI Overviews (Google) destroyed mid-market SEO + created panic budgets in 2025-26.** B2B brands are scrambling, AEO/GEO is the new keyword, agencies haven't caught up.

Both are 12-18 month windows before the major platforms close them.

## The recommended portfolio: three parallel streams

### Stream 1: The Blueprint (existing, high-ticket anchor)

Already drafted at hwlstudio-rewrite/campaigns/blueprint-offer.md. £45k fixed-fee, 6-week engagement. Sold to 3-5 LOR-grade clients per year through warm network. **No change to existing plan.**

Year-1 revenue contribution: £90-225k (lumpy, not monthly).

### Stream 2: HWL AEO (NEW primary recurring engine)

A £1,800-£2,500/month productised retainer that does three specific things and nothing else:

1. Tracks the client's brand mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews for 50-200 buyer-intent prompts weekly
2. Identifies which source URLs LLMs are citing about the client and competitors
3. Ships a monthly content + structured-data sprint that gets the client cited in those LLM answers

**Tools:** Profound (£200-500/month), Otterly (£100-300/month), or a custom Python script wrapping public APIs (£0 + Claude API tokens).

**Delivery split:** AI handles 80% of monitoring, reporting, content drafting. Harrison handles strategic interpretation, monthly review call, and the relationship.

**Target:** 6 retainers by end of month 9 = £10.8-15k/month recurring.

**First validation move:** Pick 20 UK B2B SaaS or services companies in the £5-100M range. Run "what is the best [their category]" in ChatGPT and Perplexity for each. Screenshot the results. Send each marketing lead the screenshots with one line: "You are not in this answer. Your competitor is. Worth a conversation?"

**Why now:** Google AI Overviews appear on 38% of local service queries and have cratered organic CTR by 61% on impacted queries. 73% of B2B sites lost significant organic traffic 2024-25. Brands cited in AI Overviews see 35% higher CTR. ChatGPT traffic converts at 16% vs 1.8% for Google organic. The mid-market panic is happening RIGHT NOW. Window closes when legacy SEO agencies rebrand (consensus 12-18 months).

### Stream 3: Author Repurposing Engine (NEW secondary recurring engine)

A £2,800/month productised retainer for mid-tier non-fiction authors and academics 12-18 months past their book launch.

**Inputs:** existing 50+ hours of podcast tour audio (sitting unused on third-party YouTube channels)
**Process:** Whisper + Claude ingest, build thematic library, voice-model the author
**Outputs per week:** 4 LinkedIn posts (text + image), 2 Substack snippets, 3 short clips, 1 monthly newsletter assembly

**Distribution:** Colin Fisher is case study #1 already in flight. One referral from him into the academic / non-fiction author network unlocks the channel.

**Target:** 2-3 retainers by end of month 12 = £5.6-8.4k/month recurring.

**Why now:** Whisper-3 + Claude 4.x finally voice-faithful at scale. The 2023-25 cohort of book-tour authors is at the perfect "post-launch flatline" moment, dropped by their PR firms, watching visibility decay. By 2027 every author will have a solution; the trust and case studies are won now.
