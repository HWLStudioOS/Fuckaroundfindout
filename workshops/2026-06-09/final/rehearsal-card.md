## The One-Sentence Workshop

Content feels hard because every post starts from zero. AI becomes useful when you give it a content engine: context, cadence, cascade, and calendar.

## Three Lines To Memorise

1. The AI is not the system. The context is.
2. The blank page is not noble. It is expensive.
3. Drafts are not finished posts. The engine removes the blank page. You still bring taste.

## Opening, 60 Seconds

Most founders treat content like a daily punishment.

They wake up, ask what to post, ask ChatGPT for ideas, hate the output, then post nothing.

I do not think that is a discipline problem. I think it is a system problem.

Today I am going to show you a simple content engine. The point is not to make you viral. The point is to stop starting from zero.

## The 4C Framework

- Context: what the AI reads before writing.
- Cadence: how often you make the big creative decision.
- Cascade: how one idea turns into multiple drafts.
- Calendar: how drafts become shipped work.

## Live Demo Rule

Do not over-explain the tool.

Set the frame, run the prompt, then judge the output like a practitioner.

The audience needs to see that you are not worshipping the AI. You are using it.

Lead with the flop, not the number. Open the 444k story on the deflation: "I had a post do well, the next one flopped, and the flop taught me more." Never let 444,000 be the last thing they hear in a sentence.

Hard clock rule: if the live generation passes ~3 minutes or errors mid-stream, stop and cut to dry-run-1.md. Do not wait for a total failure. If the wall clock hits ~1:02, move to slide 12 regardless.

## Q&A Defaults

If someone asks, "What tool should I use?"

Answer:

Start with the tool you will actually open. ChatGPT, Claude, or Claude Code can all work. The folder and rhythm matter more than the logo.

If someone asks, "How do I stop it sounding generic?"

Answer:

Feed it real examples of how you sound, then ban the phrases you hate. Generic AI usually means generic context.

If someone asks, "How often should I post?"

Answer:

Less often than your ambition wants, more consistently than your current system allows. Start with one useful idea per fortnight and build from there.

If someone asks, "Can I automate everything?"

Answer:

You can automate starting points. Do not automate judgement. Your taste is the moat.
