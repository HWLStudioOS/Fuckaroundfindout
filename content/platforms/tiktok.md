# TikTok

**Rebuilt 5 July 2026. Role: cold discovery plus search surface.** Fully viable: the US divestiture closed January 2026 and the UK consumer app is untouched. TikTok in 2026 rewards exactly what the old doc feared it wouldn't: authenticity register, process, personal connection over gloss. Research: `content/references/research-2026-07-05.md`.

---

## The 2026 reality

- The platform's own 2026 trend framing: unfiltered process and real people over curated perfection. Talking-head authority content is explicitly surfaced. Restrained-cinematic works as a differentiator, but realness is the register. His one-take mode fits natively here.
- **Photo-mode carousels are a real small-account path:** they go straight to the FYP, the feed is less competitive than video, and text overlays are indexed by TikTok search.
- **TikTok is a search engine now** (~40% of Gen Z default to social search for some queries). Keywords belong in the caption, the on-screen text, and the spoken words. The video is a long-tail blog post.
- Same watermark discipline as IG: clean master, native upload, per-platform caption.

## What posts

- **1-3 per week, all derived, zero extra shoots:**
  - The week's talk moment, native cut (can run slightly rawer than the IG version).
  - A photo-mode carousel: typed page, studio frames, training stills, with search-keyworded text overlays ("ai agents running a business", "one person studio", "50k ultramarathon training london").
  - Occasional craft cut from the cinematic library.
- Captions under 150 characters, 3-5 hashtags mixing broad and niche, keywords spoken out loud in the take where natural.

## Register

Same person, loosest production. TikTok gets the version closest to the raw take. No brand-sheet letterboxing here, full-frame 9:16, bigger captions.

## Engagement

Reply window matters less than IG. Filter DMs hard (real profiles only). TikTok is the funnel mouth, the relationship happens on IG, YouTube, and email.

## What we measure

Engagement rate and profile-click-through to the other surfaces. One viral outlier proves nothing, ignore spikes in both directions.

## What kills it

- Treating it as a scheduling dump for IG files.
- Trend-chasing (audio, formats, niches that aren't his).
- Investing session time here beyond the derived cuts. It earns more only when the data says so.
