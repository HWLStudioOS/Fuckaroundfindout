## When to read research vs the live system

- Designing a new offer: research is reference; the live decision happens in `business/offers/*.md`.
- Picking a side income lane: only read research after a clear trigger (e.g., LOR retainer declined, ASCE pricing test, capital deployment activation).
- Designing the next content product: read `2026-05-10-operator-deep-dive.md` (Koe / Welsh sections especially).
- Verifying a skill / MCP recommendation: read `2026-04-29-skills-and-mcps.md` (URL verification table is genuinely accurate).

## When to commission new research

When a real trigger arrives that the existing research doesn't answer. Triggers:
- LOR retainer landed, scaling productisation: new research on Blueprint productisation operators.
- LOR retainer declined: new research on warm-intro vector cultivation.
- ASCE pricing test fails at £45k: new research on price-point benchmarking in vertical comms.
- Audience question resolves: new research on the specific niche identified.
- Acquisition trigger: new research on Acquire.com / Empire Flippers specific listings.

**Important:** new research should not be commissioned to procrastinate decisions. Per codex H21 + 2026-strategy.md hard nos: "Don't spawn ten research agents to find the path. The path is in your own files. More research is procrastination."
