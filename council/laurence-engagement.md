If proposal is **£500-£1,500/month**: real decision. Need to compare against (a) one-shot quarterly review from a different perspective at ~£1-2k, (b) targeted paid project (specific deliverable) with Laurence or someone else, (c) using same budget for a paid intro to a peer-network play (RSA Fellowship + Conduit trial), (d) one-off coaching sprint with a sharper-edged operator (e.g., someone closer to Alex M H Smith / Tom Critchlow archetype).
