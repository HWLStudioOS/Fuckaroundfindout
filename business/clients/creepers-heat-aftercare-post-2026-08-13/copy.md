# Caption

The surface can look wet while a newly planted tree's rootball is still dry.

Before adding more water, move the mulch aside and scrape down a few inches at the edge of the rootball. Dry and crumbly means water slowly. Cool and damp means check again later. Wet or soggy means do not add more.

Prioritise anything planted this year, large specimens still establishing, and planting in rain shadows beneath trees, beside walls or under eaves.

Water slowly around the rootball. Aim for moisture at root depth, not just darker topsoil. Morning is best, evening is fine, and a dry rootball needs water now.

Planting takes a day. Establishment takes seasons.

Follow the project's aftercare plan where one has been supplied.
