## Editorial spine

1. Hope needs a goal, more than one route and enough agency to move.
2. Younger workers cannot invest in a future they are being told will not include them.
3. Layoffs damage the risk-taking and ideas of the people who remain.
4. Reskilling is a practical bet on a future that still includes humans.
5. Small, visible goals rebuild momentum.
6. Micromanagement removes agency, accomplishment and intrinsic motivation.
