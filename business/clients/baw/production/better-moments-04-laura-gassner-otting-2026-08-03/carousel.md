# Tuesday carousel

## Campaign idea

**A good job is not one thing.**

The carousel turns Laura's four Cs into a simple diagnostic. It should feel useful before the audience knows an episode is coming.

## Slide copy

### 1. Cover

Eyebrow: THE CAREER SCORECARD

Headline: A GOOD JOB ISN'T ONE THING.

Subhead: Score yours across calling, connection, contribution and control.

### 2. Good for whom?

Eyebrow: START WITH THE REAL QUESTION

Headline: GOOD FOR WHOM?

Body: A role can look excellent on paper and still be wrong for the person doing it. The useful question is not, "Is this a good job?" It is, "Is this a good job for me, now?"

Callout: Every person has a different definition of success. That definition changes with age and life stage.

### 3. Calling

Eyebrow: 01 / CALLING

Headline: WHAT PULLS YOU FORWARD?

Body: A leader who inspires you. A mission you want to fulfil. A problem you care about. A business you want to build. A family you want to nurture.

Callout: What is the gravitational force that gets you out of bed?

### 4. Connection

Eyebrow: 02 / CONNECTION

Headline: DOES YOUR WORK MATTER?

Body: Connection is the relevance and impact of the work. Look at your calendar, inbox and task list. Do those things move you towards what matters, or away from it?

Callout: If you called in sick tomorrow, would anybody notice?

### 5. Contribution

Eyebrow: 03 / CONTRIBUTION

Headline: WHAT DOES WORK GIVE YOU?

Body: This is not how you contribute to work. It is how work contributes to your life: money, flexibility, growth, stability and the ability to live your values.

Callout: Does the work support the life you are trying to build?

### 6. Control

Eyebrow: 04 / CONTROL

Headline: WHAT IS ACTUALLY YOURS?

Body: Your time. Your decisions. The teams you join. The metrics used to judge you. The conditions in which you do your best work.

Callout: People will choose control over power.

### 7. The dials move

Eyebrow: THE MIX IS PERSONAL

Headline: YOUR ANSWER CAN CHANGE.

Body: Children, debt, caring responsibilities, health and ambition all change what a good role needs to provide. The answer at 26 may be wrong at 36.

Callout: You do not need all four at full volume. You need the right mix for this stage.

### 8. Exercise

Eyebrow: TEN MINUTES

Headline: SCORE THE FOUR Cs.

Body: For each C, write two scores from 0 to 10: what you have today and what you need now. The largest gap is the conversation to have.

Callout: Better Moments #4 with Laura Gassner Otting lands Thursday.

## Visual system

- 1080 x 1350 Instagram set.
- 1200 x 1500 LinkedIn native-document set, separately composed rather than cropped.
- Locked paper, ink, violet, yellow, coral, green and blue primitives.
- Newsreader for editorial ideas, Manrope for body copy and IBM Plex Mono for metadata.
- The exact outlined original logo, including TM, is the only mark.
- The original sits directly on violet on the cover and end card. Interior and photographic layouts use a violet holding field.
- Cover uses the locked violet field. Each body slide uses one dominant accent.
- All text remains live in the SVG sources.
- Page numbers run 01 / 08 to 08 / 08.
