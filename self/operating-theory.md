# Operating Theory

**Canonical source:** `/Users/harrison/hwlstudio-codex/harrison-os/03-operating-theory.md`

21 hypotheses captured from the 11-round codex interview. These are working theories, not fixed truths. Load-bearing for the system. Re-read on session start when context is needed.

## The hypotheses one-liner

1. Harrison's real asset is taste plus commercial instinct.
2. The business is more fragile than the income suggests (£5k clear MRR, £11k total).
3. The main constraint is not capability, it is operating cadence.
4. AI is both the opportunity and the distraction.
5. "Passive income" may be the wrong first target. The deeper desire is freedom, not passivity.
6. The 12-month goal needs a constraint (audience, problem, promise, proof, product ladder, delivery model).
7. **Harrison is using competence to outrun financial admin.**
8. **The personal brand block is about status risk, not laziness.**
9. The real north star is family freedom, not fame.
10. **Harrison's public voice should not be a single niche yet.** Hybrid voice, narrow commercial wedge.
11. **The problem is not lack of tools, it is lack of one trusted intake.**
12. Instagram is both market research and avoidance.
13. Speed is the reputation, but reactivity is the trap.
14. Harrison's irreplaceable value is trust creation plus strategic vision.
15. The offer is not "content is hard"; it is "execution inside serious organizations is hard."
16. Harrison sells trust before he sells deliverables.
17. Harrison's standards are misallocated (too high internally, too low on communication).
18. Harrison needs engineered serendipity.
19. **Marketing is the wrapper, not the obsession.**
20. The second brain must be a recall engine, not a storage locker.
21. **Manual prompting will kill the OS.**
