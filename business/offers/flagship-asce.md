# Flagship: Augmented Strategic Content Engine (ASCE)

**Status:** working name, working description. Refinement is one of the next 30-day moves.

## What it is

HWL Studio helps serious organizations build AI-enabled content and comms systems by reviewing brand positioning, founder/executive story, organizational context, and current workflows, then designing and optionally operating a strategic content engine.

## Who it's for

Serious organizations with three or more of:
- £20M+ revenue, or equivalent institutional weight.
- 50+ employees, or work that touches that many.
- Mid-transformation: leadership change, market repositioning, M&A, capital event, generational shift in workforce.
- Already producing decent work, just not getting credit for it.
- Senior team that respects strategy and is allergic to vibes.

## What gets delivered

- Brand and positioning review.
- Founder/executive narrative.
- Organizational story mining.
- Content pillars (three to five).
- AI-enabled workflow design.
- Production process.
- Distribution and scheduling system.
- Reporting and learning loop.
- Governance and approvals (especially for corporate clients).

## Sold in three shapes

1. **Six-week Blueprint engagement:** £45,000 fixed fee. See `business/offers/blueprint-45k.md`. One-and-done. Output is a strategic document around 60 pages. Most clients run implementation in-house after.
2. **Advisory retainer:** £8,500/month (strategic advisement only) or £11,000/month (advisory + light implementation). See `business/offers/retainer-options.md`.
3. **Fractional Head of Content retainer:** £15,000/month. Strategic + production support + active implementation. See `business/offers/retainer-options.md`.
