# Routines

Sleep, diet, recovery operating rules. Default templates for the morning-brief agent.

## Sleep target

- Wake: 5:00 (aspiration), 6:30 (current realistic baseline).
- Sleep: 21:00-22:00 (aspiration), 23:00 (current realistic baseline).
- Phone in another room.
- Last activity before sleep: not social media (current pattern, to break).
- Summer-light fix: blackout blind or eye mask. Earlier waking is currently environmental, so move bedtime earlier before adding recovery interventions.

## Morning sequence (default)

1. 06:00-06:30 wake.
2. Water + salt + creatine.
3. 10-15 min outside walk (codex keystone habit).
4. Complete the day's scheduled training from `health/training-plan.md`. Morning movement may be a walk or movement microdose, not necessarily the main session.
5. Deep work block 09:30-11:00. Phone grayscale, in another room, DND.

## Default meals

- Breakfast: eggs + wraps OR yogurt bowls + berries + granola.
- Lunch: mince + rice + beans OR rice + cooked veg + lean protein.
- Dinner: similar to lunch, low-fibre options on Friday/Saturday if Sunday is a long run.
- Scale portions to the calorie and carbohydrate target in `health/fuel.md`.

## Training fuel

See `health/fuel.md`. Easy work under 60 minutes does not need intra-session carbohydrate. Runs over 75 minutes use 30-45 g/hour in this block.

## Recovery rules (HRV)

Rolling 7-day average, never single-day.
- Within 5ms of baseline: run planned session.
- Down 6-10ms with no context: easy, replace quality with Z2.
- Down 11+ms for 2+ days: skip next quality, easy only, reassess.
- Crashed but obvious context (alcohol, illness, work stress): note context, ignore the number, run planned session unless subjective feel is also off.

## Local-pain traffic light

- Green: pain 0-1/10, no swelling or limp, full plan.
- Amber: pain 2/10 or symptoms trending up, remove intensity and reduce volume about 30%.
- Red: pain at least 3/10, swelling, limp or focal bony tenderness, no impact or loaded lower work, assess the cause.

Local tissue status overrides HRV and Garmin readiness.

## Strength adherence rule

Three planned sessions per week: Monday full body, Wednesday upper, Friday full body. Two completed sessions preserve the strength dimension during a disrupted week. Missed work is not caught up on Saturday.

## Double-session rule

- Keep at least six hours between sessions.
- Wednesday second session is upper-body only.
- Refuel between sessions.
- A double is cancelled, not compressed, when recovery status is amber or red.
