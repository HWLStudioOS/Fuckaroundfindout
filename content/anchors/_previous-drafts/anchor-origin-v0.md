## What's already strong

- The visual hook (camera, mouse, mouse) is a great open. Show, don't tell.
- "Earning double… but nothing changed?" is a perfect turn. That's the essay.
- "Genuinely torturous… sometimes", the "sometimes" caveat is what saves it from victim energy.
- "I say to myself at least every week, this month is going to be the month I start making more content for myself" is the most quotable line in the whole piece. That's the recursion you want to name.
- The Tim Chalamet/adidas DP namedrop grounds the comparison trap in real specifics.

## What needs work before publishing

- The "6 months ago I went freelance" timestamp triggers codex H8 (status risk: don't do "I just started a business 6 months ago" content). Fix: drop the explicit 6-month marker, or reframe as observation not confession.
- The piece doesn't quite land. "Why not take a bet on yourself?" is the right thrust but lands soft. Needs the Yoda / Kipling weight.
- The throughline is "I'm a creative who hasn't backed himself", but the v1 hook needs to be the Dan Jones-style life-arc opener: "I'm 26 and I'm not where I thought I'd be." That elevates this from "frustrated creative" to "the bigger arc."
- The structure can become the FILM ESSAY format (Tier B), not just a talking-head reel (Tier A). The Calder Dune Meditation-on-Men template fits this perfectly: archival/sourced footage of creative work + your voiceover.
