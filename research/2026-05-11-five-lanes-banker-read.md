## 5. The retail trading lane (and the boring baseline)

**The £100/wk on a £20k account claim, decoded.** That is a 26% net annual return. The median professional long/short hedge fund made 7-9% net of fees over 2015-2024. You would need to outperform the median paid quant professional by 3x. **Possible but not where I'd bet your consulting hours.**

**The base-rate failure stats (cite-able):**
- FCA-mandated CFD broker disclosures, UK 2024-2025: 71-89% of retail clients lose money.
- ESMA pan-EU: 74-89% of retail accounts lose on CFDs/forex.
- Brazilian Securities Commission longitudinal study (~20,000 day traders): of those who persisted >300 trading days, 97% lost money. Only 1.1% earned more than the Brazilian minimum wage.
- Barber & Odean (canonical academic paper, ~66,000 US households): heaviest-trading households earned 11.4% net annually vs 18.5% for buy-and-hold-and-do-nothing.

**The boring baseline you should beat before trying anything else.** Max the £20k ISA into a global equity ETF (VWRP). Expected ~7-9% annualised, fully tax-free. On £20k that's £1,400-1,800/yr = £27-35/wk, passively, zero time investment. Any algo strategy that doesn't clear ~£35/wk net of all costs is literally losing money vs do-nothing.

**The two retail-scale strategies that have a defensible real edge:**

1. **Wheel / options-premium selling on SPY or QQQ via Interactive Brokers** (requires US options access, FX considerations). Strict position-sizing rules (≤30% capital in any single underlying, 0.10-0.15 delta puts, close at 50% profit). Expected ~6-10% annualised in calm years, -15 to -25% in bad ones. Realistic £30-80/wk over 2-3 years with discipline. UK retail can trade SPY USD options via IBKR.

2. **Crypto cash-and-carry / funding-rate arbitrage** across 2-3 exchanges. Average funding-rate-arb return ~14% in 2024, ~19% in 2025 per academic study, but only ~40% of "top" opportunities are profitable after costs, and the strategy ate face in May 2022 (LUNA, 3AC) when basis flipped. At £20k: plausible £30-60/wk operationally. Operational risk (exchange counterparty, depeg) is the real cost.

**The football / Betfair adjacent path that's actually more accessible than equity quant for an analytical generalist.**

Betfair sports trading has structurally more retail edge than US equity markets, but is brutally taxed by Betfair's Premium Charge (20-60% of net profits) once you become consistently profitable. Football modelling (xG / Poisson) can get to 5-15% ROI on staked capital. On a £20k bankroll at 1-2% per stake = 5% ROI on £20k turnover/wk = £50/wk plausible.

Caan Berry is the documented UK reference: ~£18k/month at peak by scalping horse racing pre-race, 12+ year track record. He has now pivoted to courses, which is itself a signal.

**The honest move for your trading curiosity.** Max the ISA into VWRP for the £35/wk passive baseline. Allocate £3-5k of "tuition capital" into ONE focused, written-down options-premium-selling system on SPY/QQQ via IBKR. Treat it as 18-month tuition. After that, if there's a real PnL curve, scale. In parallel, run a football-modelling Betfair side project at a £500-1k bankroll because the maths suits you, the data is free, and the edge is real in lesser markets.

Do not build crypto trading bots or run discretionary CFDs. The math is settled.

## 9. The risk this synthesis doesn't address

- A weekly review surface that compares "what you said you'd do this week" against "what shipped." Visible. Doesn't move.

- One agent that runs unattended and surfaces opportunities from the discovery lanes (acquisition deals, AEO opportunities, vertical AI competitor moves). This is the "AI working while you sleep" piece you keep asking for. Three of the four agent prompts are already drafted; one of them gets wired in Phase 4.
- The Council skill (the one thing the rewrite preserved that actually works) gets used for any decision >£5k or any structural pivot. Pre-committed.

These are architecture decisions, not strategy decisions. Phase 2 handles them.
