# How to Use AI to Run Your Content So You Can Run Your Business

90-min live workshop for Year One. Tue 9 June 2026.

## The pitch (sent to Laurence 26 May, for community post)

> Workshop. Tue 9 June, 90 min, live.
>
> **How to Use AI to Run Your Content So You Can Run Your Business**
> with Harrison Living, founder of HWL Studio.
>
> Most founders treat content as something they have to grind out. Harrison's built an AI-augmented system that runs it: a working folder, a simple weekly rhythm, the engine fires from there.
>
> In this 90-min live walkthrough he opens the folder, runs the engine on screen, and shows you how to build one for your business.
>
> No pre-work. No homework. An honest look at how one operator runs the content side of his studio without it eating his week.

## What we'll cover

- The three pieces of an AI content engine: the tool, the folder, the rhythm. Why each one fails without the other two.
- How to set up Claude Code so it thinks with you about your business, not just chats with you.
- The folder structure that codifies your business context (strategy, voice, hard rules, plan) so the AI doesn't produce generic slop.
- How to extract your "voice DNA" from your existing writing, so anything the engine outputs actually sounds like you, not LinkedIn.
- The fortnight rhythm: one big idea every two weeks, waterfalled into 7-10 posts across IG, TikTok, LinkedIn, Substack, X. Without writing five separate ideas.
- Live: one prompt fires the engine and cascades into a long-form post, a video script, a field note, and a thread, in real time.
