# Slide-By-Slide Speaker Notes

## 1. Title

Open relaxed. Most founders do content like a daily punishment: blank page, generic AI ideas, nothing shipped. Today is the alternative.

## 2. The Promise

You are not teaching AI tips. You are showing a system: folder, rhythm, cascade. Make it safe for beginners.

## 3. The Actual Problem

The pain is not only discipline. It is repeated decisions. What should I post? How should it sound? Where should it go?

## 4. The Reframe

The AI is not the system. The context is. The folder matters because it holds the instructions, examples, constraints, and checks.

## 5. The Engine

Four parts: context, cadence, cascade, calendar. Keep returning to this framework throughout the workshop.

## 6. Why Chatbots Disappoint

If the output is bland, the AI probably has no idea who you are. A one-off chat gives one-off output. A system gives continuity.

## 7. The Folder

Open with the pre-empt before you share: "You do not need a terminal for this. This is just my tool. A folder of plain text files plus any chatbot you already pay for does the same job. Watch the shape, not the software."

Screen share only the clean folder. Explain each file as a repeated decision removed. Do not open private folders or client material.

## 8. Cadence

One useful idea per fortnight is enough to begin. The point is to stop chasing new ideas daily and start building repeatable rhythm.

## 9. Cascade

One idea becomes five drafts. The draft is not the final post. The engine removes the blank page so the founder can bring taste.

## 10. Live Demo Setup

The demo idea: 444,000 views followed by almost no reach. Big number is vanity. Small number tells the truth.

## 11. What To Watch

Tell them to judge context, not clever prompting. Does it keep the voice, strategy, and usefulness?

## 12. The Human Job

AI gets you to the draft. Taste gets you to the post. Keep, cut, sharpen, ship.

## 13. Build Yours

Starter system: strategy file, voice file, waterfall file. Do this before buying another tool.

Say it straight: "You will not walk out and have this tonight. What you walk out with is the shape. The hard part was never the AI, it was deciding what you stand for clearly enough to write down."

## 14. Starter Questions

Do not write documents. Answer decisions. If a file does not remove a repeated decision, it is probably theatre.

## 15. Failure Modes

The system fails when loops do not close: too many tools, no capture, no cadence, no editing taste, no calendar.

## 16. Close

Starting from a blank page is optional now. Open Q&A around folder teardown, live prompts, and how to stop AI sounding generic.
