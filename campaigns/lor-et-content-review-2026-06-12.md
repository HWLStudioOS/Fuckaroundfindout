## B. Production briefs (the David-briefable versions)

These are the long-form briefs behind the slate one-liners. Brief format: hook / on camera / why it works.

- **03 Five routes carousel (Jul, banked).** "There are five ways into a £5bn construction company. School tells you about one." 7 frames, one route per frame, identical grid: entry requirement, day-one salary band, what year one involves, where it lands you and at what age. Frame 7: results-day dates + apply links. Built to be saved and forwarded by parents.
- **07 A-level results post (13 Aug, 08:30).** "Three letters on a page do not decide this. Mine were two grades under the offer." Current degree apprentice, phone-framed, 30-45s: the grades they got, the call they made that morning, where they are now. Carousel 03 reposted within the hour. Parent-facing caption.
- **08 GCSE results post (20 Aug).** "At 16 you don't need to know what you want to be. You need the next two years, that's all." 6 frames at parent reading level: decision tree at 16, LOR requirement at each gate, what each path pays, "the apprenticeship route still reaches chartered". PARENT IS PRIMARY READER.
- **10 What we actually look for (Aug).** "140 people apply for every graduate job here. AI wrote half of them. Here is what actually gets read." One criterion per frame, weak answer vs strong answer side by side, anonymised, signed off by Emma's team. Closer: "We'd rather you knew."
- **11 Degree without the debt (Aug).** Two-column ledger, uni vs degree apprenticeship, year by year 18 to 27: fees, maintenance, salary, qualification, running totals. Final frame: named apprentice + route link. Designed to be screenshotted into the family WhatsApp.
- **12 Launch film (Sep, 60-90s).** Cold open on black, grad VO: "You think construction starts on site." Hard cut into CEMC: "It starts here. Nottinghamshire." Module on the line → lorry → crane → installed, narrated in relay by grad, apprentice, line manager. Factory-to-site contrast IS the edit.
- **13 Grad DITL film (Sep).** "6:58am. Site induction. I'm 23 and today's pour is mine to sign off." Timestamps on screen, one honest negative stays in the cut (a delayed pour and what she does about it).
- **14 Grad verticals x3.** The 6:58 cold open / the thing that went wrong today / pay and progression answered straight. Assumption text card opens each.
- **15 Apprentice DITL film (Sep).** "My mates are in a lecture hall. I'm being paid to build the hospital they'll work in." Factory day + day-release afternoon. Closes on the ledger: zero debt, chartered track, 27.
- **16 Apprentice verticals x3.** Factory reveal ("you think construction means mud", broken at second two) / the payslip, actual number to camera / day-release in 25 seconds.
- **17 Ghostwritten grad post (Sep, posts on HER profile launch day).** "140 people applied for my job. I nearly didn't bother." 150 words, the application mistake she almost made, link to film.
- **18 Ghostwritten senior leader post (Sep).** "45 per cent of employers say they can't fill these roles. We decided to train people rather than complain." Why the factory + 70:60:30 need talent that doesn't exist yet.
- **19 How to apply, properly (Sep).** "We read 140 applications per vacancy. Most die in the first paragraph." Each assessment stage, what assessors score, one real strong answer next to the AI-slop version.
- **20 Role spotlight: Site Engineer (Sep).** "You think a site engineer builds things. She checks them. Nothing gets built until she says so." One real decision: a setting-out check that caught an error, what catching it saved. Template for 29 and 32.
- **24 First Fortnight film (Oct).** "Two weeks ago she was refreshing results-day TikTok. Today she's planning a crane lift." Diary-style, deliberately rougher than the launch film: first induction, first mistake, first payday.
- **25 First Fortnight verticals x3.** What I packed for day one / my first payday, the actual number / the thing nobody told me.
- **26 School to chartered map (Oct).** "University is not the only road to chartered. Here is the one nobody shows you." One year per frame 18-27, against your uni peers that same year. Progression companion to 11's money ledger.
- **27 Ghostwritten apprentice post (Oct).** "One month in. Here's what I got wrong about this place." Three corrections: expected mud, found a factory; expected fetching tea, got real checks; expected all men, met the actual crew.
- **28 Myth verticals x3 (Oct).** Wellies: walk CEMC in trainers, one unbroken shot. All-men: camera counts the morning meeting, no commentary. Dead end: one named progression 2019-now, dates on screen. Brief all three breaks BEFORE Shoot Day 2.
- **29 Role spotlight: Digital Engineer (Oct).** "She spent her morning in a 3D model. The building doesn't exist yet. Her job is why it will." Live federated model screen capture + the clash she found this week and the cost of not finding it.
- **30 Line manager films x2 (Oct).** "You're not applying to a company. You're applying to him." What they promise a new starter, expect in week one, the mistake they forgive and the one they don't.
