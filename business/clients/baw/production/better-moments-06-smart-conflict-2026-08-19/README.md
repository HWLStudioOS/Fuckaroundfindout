## Editorial spine

1. Conflict style is shaped by confidence and the desire to get it right or be right.
2. Enforced harmony can crowd out healthy disagreement.
3. Relational conflict is singles tennis: I am right, you are wrong.
4. Task conflict is doubles: both people on the same side against the problem.
5. Get curious, not furious, whether your default is to charge in or avoid.
