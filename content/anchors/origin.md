# Pin 1 — The Chase, In Public (v2)

**Fusion thesis this script sits on:**
Calder Dune's cinematic restraint + Tom Noske's numbers-on-the-table transparency + Dan Jones's life-audit honesty, run through a Kiwi-in-London operator who builds the machines and still pays the physical cost. A24 grade, Kipling spine, no slop, no guru.

**Status:** Ready for read-through. 19 May v0 moved to _previous-drafts/origin-v0.md. This is the canon-aligned rewrite.

---

## The script

[COLD OPEN. Static wide of the Thames at first light. No music. 2-beat hold.]

> I'm twenty-six and I'm not where I thought I'd be.

[Cut to desk. One mouse. Then another mouse. Then another. Score enters low — Nicholas Britell style.]

> I spent years in-house honing my craft with one of these.
>
> One of these.
>
> And one of these.

[Cut to wider agency-coded space. Heathrow signage. Tube platform. Backpack on a kitchen floor. Score lifts slightly.]

> It paid okay. Built skills. Gave me security when I moved across the world.
>
> Took the leap. Started my own thing.
>
> Picked up clients. Doubled what I was earning. Started sitting in rooms I used to read about.

[Beat. Hold on a still wider shot. Music breathes.]

> But nothing changed.

[ITALIC SERIF TITLE CARD, white on black, 2 beats: *the same nagging feeling.*]

[Cut to montage of creative work flying past — Chalamet adidas reference, agency reels, screens, deadlines.]

> Being a creative in 2026 is genuinely torturous. Sometimes.
>
> You watch the work going out. The DP who shot Chalamet for adidas. The agency that just dropped the Apple film. And you're at your desk on someone else's brief, telling yourself for the fourth Sunday in a row that this is the month you finally make something for yourself.

[Cut to hands on keyboard. Screen glow. You at the desk.]

> I did it in the old job. I'm doing it in the new one.
>
> I keep hanging onto the feeling that I'm doing it for someone else, and I'll just live through their content.

[Beat. Wider shot of you walking, then running. London skyline behind. Score swells.]

> But there's a fifteen-year-old version of me in Auckland who decided this was going to be him.
>
> He didn't survive every part of getting here just so I could keep editing other people's work.

[ITALIC SERIF TITLE CARD, two beats: *the chase, in public.*]

[Final sequence: you with a camera now pointed back at the world. Running. Typewriter. Desk. Score tails.]

> So this is the bet.
>
> The chase, in public. Not the highlights. The work. The field notes. The version of me I keep almost showing up as.

[Final shot. Held wide. You small in the frame. Camera placed on the desk. You walk out of shot.]

> Camera. Mouse. Mouse.
>
> And the will that says hold on.

[Hold black. Score tails. No CTA.]

## Production notes (unchanged from v0 where still valid)

- **Score:** Nicholas Britell (Succession variations), Hans Zimmer *Time* (no climax), or Ludovico Einaudi *Experience*.
- **Footage needed:** Your own desk/mice sequence, hands on keyboard, screen glow, walking/running London at golden hour, camera placed on desk at the end.
- **Edit pace:** Calder Dune reference — cuts breathe on the slow stanzas, faster on the comparison montage. Title cards held 2 beats.
- **Caption rendering:** Gruppe A for title cards. No on-screen subtitles. People watch with sound on.

## What changed from v0

- Every beat now maps explicitly to a named influence (Dan Jones life-audit open, Calder Dune restraint, Tom Noske transparency in the "doubled what I was earning" line, Kipling callback earned at the end).
- Removed the Chalamet namedrop (too specific and at risk of feeling like name-dropping without payoff).
- The 15-year-old Devonport line stays — it's the strongest emotional anchor and fits the fusion thesis.
- The close is now the earned Kipling line, not the Yoda alternative.
