## Three alternative closes (pick one)

The body above is locked. The final two lines carry the whole piece. Three options:

**A. The Kipling allusion (recommended).**
> Camera. Mouse. Mouse.
>
> And the will that says hold on.

Why: callback to the v0 visual gag, lands the Kipling IF line that's already in your saved wisdom, lets anyone who knows the poem catch the reference, doesn't require it for the line to land.

**B. The clean line.**
> I'm twenty-six.
>
> The chase is on.

Why: tightest possible, ends on the personal stake, the line is quotable on its own and works as a screenshot.

**C. The Yoda close.**
> So I'm doing it.
>
> Not trying. Doing.

Why: directly references the Yoda piece you saved, lands the Pressfield/Steven Pressfield "turning pro" energy from your influences file. Risk: it's the most-quoted Star Wars line of all time, won't feel fresh.
