# Weekend products: what got built, and what to do with them

You asked me to find the digital-product / passive-income line hiding in the files and
build it. I ran a 36-agent discovery and concept workflow across the whole system
(workshop pack, content engine, offers, funnel, money, codex, the buildable assets),
generated 18 product concepts from six strategic lenses, scored them, stress-tested the
top four for real demand, H8 status risk and weekend feasibility, and synthesised a
winner.

The winner and the thing you actually pointed at were not the same product. So I built
both. They sit on opposite tracks on purpose, exactly as the funnel doc demands.

## The empty rung

Your offer ladder is £45k Blueprint, £8.5k/£11k advisory, £15k fractional head. Every
rung is £8,500 or more. There is nothing self-serve, nothing under £8.5k, no bottom of
the ladder. That gap is H6 (the product ladder) sitting half built. Both products below
fill the bottom, from two different ends.

---

## Product 1: The Communications Legibility Diagnosis (buyer track)

**What it is.** A scored, written diagnostic that tells a serious organisation how
legible it is to its own market, across five dimensions drawn straight from the £45k
Blueprint's own spine (strategic frame, voice discipline, audience clarity, owned
assets, rhythm and measurement). Twelve weighted questions, about three minutes, no
email required to see the score. Low-legibility orgs get the gap named and are handed
the existing application gate with their score attached. High-legibility orgs are
qualified out honestly, which is itself the scarcity signal.

**Why this one.** It is the lowest-friction top of your funnel. "Apply for the seat" is
a high-commitment ask. "See how legible your organisation is to its market" is a
low-commitment, high-intrigue one. It gives the content launch a reason to click the bio
link that is not a brochure or an application form. It leans on IP only you own (the
Blueprint method), and it is pure buyer track, so it carries the lowest H8 risk of any
consumer-facing thing you could ship. A qualified application that arrives pre-diagnosed
is worth more than any low-ticket sale.

**Where it lives.** Built into the existing studio site at `~/hwlstudio-site`, on a
feature branch `legibility-diagnosis` (main is untouched).
- New `src/components/LegibilityDiagnosis.tsx` (the whole instrument, no backend, no AI:
  rule-based selection from five written diagnosis bands in your voice).
- Reachable three ways: the URL `/#diagnosis` (point the bio link here), a "Diagnosis"
  nav link, a hero link "Diagnose your legibility", and a footer button.
- `ApplyGate.tsx` now carries the diagnosis result into the emailed application, so the
  first conversation starts from what they already told you.

**Verified.** Full walk-through in a browser: hash route, all twelve questions, score
maths (correct), both result branches (gate for low scorers, honest qualify-out for high
scorers), the crossover into the application with the score attached, mobile layout, zero
console errors.

**Deploy (one tap, your call):**
```
cd ~/hwlstudio-site
git checkout legibility-diagnosis
npx vercel deploy            # preview URL first, to see it live
npx vercel deploy --prod     # promote to www.hwlstudio.com when happy
```
Then merge the branch to main. I did not deploy because pushing the live site is your
one-tap call, not mine.

**Launch (buyer surfaces only).** Re-point the IG/LinkedIn bio link at
`hwlstudio.com/#diagnosis`. Anchor one LinkedIn post in your voice, not "take my quiz"
but a POV piece ("most serious organisations are illegible to their own market, here is
how to see the gap"). Keep it off IG/TikTok as a teaching artefact. The word is
"diagnosis", never "quiz". The whole register is built to hold that line.

## Product 2: The AI Content Engine (peer track, the digital product you pointed at)

**What it is.** The Year One workshop method, productised as one self-serve static page.
The interview prompt (the core IP), the four context files explained with downloadable
starter templates, the cascade / capture / fortnight-review prompts, and the two paths
in. Everything copy-to-clipboard or download, client-side, no backend, no AI calls. It
is the recap pack you mentioned, turned into a real product instead of a PDF.

**Why this one.** It is the bottom rung from the peer end, and it is the deliverable you
already owe Year One (the promised Loom series, except better and self-serve). It is the
genuine digital-product / passive-income seed: free gift to the community now,
gateable behind an email or a Gumroad price later, with near-zero marginal effort per
user.

**Where it lives.** `~/hwl-content-engine/`, its own git repo, one commit. Single
`index.html`, same deploy pattern as the content-calendar template.

**Verified.** Hero and full layout on brand (Palette D, field-notes voice), copy buttons
work end to end (cascade prompt copied clean), all five starter files download. Only
console noise is a harmless favicon 404.

**Deploy (one tap):**
```
cd ~/hwl-content-engine
npx vercel deploy --prod --yes
```
Creates a new Vercel project. Give it a memorable home (standalone, or a subdomain).

**Launch (peer surfaces only).** Drop it in the Year One Slack as the follow-up you
promised. This is the H8 line: keep it on your personal field-notes brand and the peer
community, never on the buyer surfaces or next to the £45k Blueprint. Same voice, same
world, separate surface. The funnel doc sequences the peer/course layer to 2027, and a
contained gift to a warm community you already presented to does not break that. A public
"how I do content with AI" launch would. Hold that distinction.

**One production note.** Display font is Space Grotesk, a free stand-in for the licensed
Gruppe A. Swap to Gruppe A before any paid launch (same move as F37 Ginger → Outfit on
the LOR carousel).

## The honest strategic note

The workflow's pure-logic pick was Product 1 alone, and on revenue leverage it is right:
the buyer track is what pays the £150-200k goal this year, and a diagnosed application is
worth more than a £39 download. If you only ship one thing this quarter, ship the
Diagnosis.

But you asked for the digital product, and the content engine is the truer answer to
"passive income from the work we do." It is low-leverage on revenue and high on goodwill,
authority and optionality. Built it costs you nothing to keep; it pays back the first
time a Year One member sends someone your way. The discipline is surface separation, not
abstinence.
