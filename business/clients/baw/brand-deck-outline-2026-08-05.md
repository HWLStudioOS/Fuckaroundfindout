# Brand-system presentation deck, first-pass outline

Prepared 5 August 2026 from the Brand Playground (Figma `OMTB8YIiNcQ91Ljpzpmn2r`). Purpose: the "full presentation deck" agreed on the 5 August call. Audience: Cathal first, then reusable for guests, partners and the Christine-type opportunity. Build the slides by exporting the playground boards themselves; the system presents itself.

## Structure, 12 slides

1. **Cover.** Violet field, original mark, "Better at Work, Season 05. A system for the work around the work." (Logo page 130:250, elevation 01.)
2. **The idea.** One slide of words: every episode becomes a week of useful material, built once, from one source of truth. No new design decisions on a Tuesday.
3. **The numbers.** 23 component sets, 107 variants, 51 variables, 0 legacy marks. (Hub page 76:2 stat cards.)
4. **The mark.** "Keep the mark. Improve the way it lands." Control plus the four fields, and the three guardrails: preserve, may change, never. (130:251 board, crop top half.)
5. **Colour and type with jobs.** Newsreader says the idea, Manrope carries the body, Plex Mono runs the metadata rails. Colour roles: violet brand, paper essay, coral tension, blue quotes, yellow action, dark close. (Pull examples across pages.)
6. **Episode artwork.** The Better Moments cover system, IG and LinkedIn and YouTube variants from one master. (BM4 Laura exports as the live example.)
7. **YouTube.** "Win the click. Keep the trust." Thumbnail templates, applied trailer, banner with the guaranteed central strip. (Page 55:2.)
8. **Instagram.** "Stories with a job to do." Six story jobs, the weekly rhythm calendar, profile mark rule. (Page 59:2.)
9. **LinkedIn and newsletter.** "Make the thinking worth swiping." Carousel playbook, newsletter covers, the 25% test. (Page 62:2.)
10. **Guest launch kit.** One episode, one useful story: the seven-frame sequence, the launch rhythm (T-3 to T+3), and the "before this leaves Figma" QA gate. (Page 72:2.)
11. **Question bank.** Twelve questions, routed. "If it can be answered with a slogan, rewrite it." (Page 73:2.)
12. **What ships every week, and what's next.** The six-step production flow, then: website live after end-of-week review, Season 5 rollout, guest kit in use from BM4.

## Speaking-note spine

The through-line to say out loud: this is not a rebrand, the mark never changed. It is a production system so the show compounds: same fonts, same colour grammar, same QA gates, every week, without a designer in the loop.
