# Wednesday carousel

## Campaign idea

**Your office is sending a message.**

The carousel makes Klotz's filter usable before the audience knows an episode is coming. It moves from the three needs into what a floor plan communicates, then into moves that cost nothing. It answers Cathal's own question from 18 August (an office that gets it right for employees) without mentioning the article.

Same slide grammar as BM#5: cover, model, three numbered slides, two conditions, one action slide with the Better Moments call-out. Eyebrow, headline, body, callout on every slide.

## Slide copy

### 1. Cover

Eyebrow: A BETTER WAY TO THINK ABOUT THE OFFICE

Headline: YOUR OFFICE IS SENDING A MESSAGE.

Subhead: Start with agency, growth and connection. Not paint colours.

### 2. The filter

Eyebrow: THREE NEEDS COME FIRST

Headline: ASK WHAT THE SPACE DOES FOR PEOPLE.

Body:

- Agency: a real say over your surroundings.
- Growth: room to learn and to be somewhere new.
- Connection: ways to run into each other.

Callout: Layout, paint and technology come after this question.

### 3. Agency

Eyebrow: 01 / AGENCY

Headline: A SAY IS NOT SOFT.

Body: In one study, nursing-home residents who could change their own space were far more likely to be alive 18 months later. A say over your surroundings is one of the oldest forms of control, and it spills into everything else.

Callout: How is your space showing agency?

### 4. Growth

Eyebrow: 02 / GROWTH

Headline: MOVE TO LEARN.

Body: New rooms make ideas stick. Klotz takes his students to a different building for the lessons he most wants them to keep. Take the meeting that matters somewhere new.

Callout: Go someplace new in your working environment.

### 5. Connection

Eyebrow: 03 / CONNECTION

Headline: FRIENDSHIP IS A DESIGN OUTCOME.

Body: Shared space, shared time, shared interest is a friendship factory. It happens whether you design it or not, so be intentional about where people gather.

Callout: Give the unused corner a name and an opening.

### 6. The message

Eyebrow: WHAT THE FLOOR PLAN SAYS

Headline: CUBICLES AND CORNER OFFICES TALK.

Body: The mission statement says everybody has a voice. The floor plan puts some people in cubicles and others in corner offices. People believe the floor plan.

Callout: The conference room nobody dares use is a policy.

### 7. The mistake

Eyebrow: THE MOST DAMAGING MISTAKE

Headline: PAINT COLOURS ARE NOT THE FIRST QUESTION.

Body: Open plan or private offices. The new brand colours. The screens on the wall. Every one of them is a lesser priority if nobody has asked what the space does for agency, growth and connection.

Callout: Ask the needs first. Then choose the layout.

### 8. Action

Eyebrow: TRY THIS THIS WEEK

Headline: FIX THE OFFICE WITHOUT A BUDGET.

Body:

1. Get to the meeting room 20 minutes early.
2. Give the team the room and let them set it up.
3. Move the couch nobody sits on to where people gather.

Callout: Better Moments #6 with Leidy Klotz lands Thursday.
