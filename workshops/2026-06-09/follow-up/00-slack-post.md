Everything I promised from Monday's session, in one PDF. 👇

Eight pages, not a course:

*Start here, two ways in.* The 10-minute setup. One path with no terminal at all (most of you should start there), and the terminal version I demoed, with exact install commands for Mac and Windows.

*The interview prompt.* The most important page. Paste it into any AI and it interviews you about your business, your voice and what you refuse to post, then writes your four context files for you. This is the bit that makes everything else work.

*The folder system.* The four files plus an ideas inbox, what each one does, how to build it.

*The cascade.* The exact prompt from the live demo, the one that turned one idea into five drafts.

*The working rhythm.* The three small prompts around the cascade: capture, review, edit.

*Plugging in your tools.* MCPs explained for humans. Week-two material.

*The stack plus six videos.* Everything I use daily, honestly tiered, and six beginner videos I checked myself.

Loom walkthroughs of the install, the folder build and the cascade are coming next. Short ones.

One thing before you dive in: the AI is not the system. The context is. The interview prompt is where the context comes from, so do it properly. Half an hour, once.

Stuck on anything, post here and tag me. The install is the only fiddly part.
