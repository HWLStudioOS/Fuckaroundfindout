## Copy basis

The advice is deliberately diagnostic rather than generic: the surface can look wet while a newly planted rootball remains dry; scrape down a few inches and feel the soil; water slowly around a dry rootball; prioritise first-year planting, large specimens still establishing and planting in rain shadows. The guidance was checked against the [RHS watering guide](https://www.rhs.org.uk/garden-jobs/watering) and [RHS moisture-check guidance](https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/understanding-plants/how-plants-absorb-water).

No fixed litre claim is used. Requirements vary by specimen, soil and site. Follow the project's aftercare plan where one has been supplied.
