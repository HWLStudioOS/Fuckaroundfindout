# Influence Canon

**Status:** v0 draft, 5 July 2026, drafted by Hermes from vault evidence, awaiting Harrison's correction.

The missing keystone. Every other content file (strategy, hook library, brand sheet, wisdom canon) either studies influences tactically or cites archetypes strategically. Nothing until now names the actual stack in one place and states the fusion. This file sits ABOVE the hook library and feeds the strategy. It answers one question: who am I stealing from, what exactly do I take from each, what do I refuse, and what is the one-line recipe that is only me.

Rule of the file: name the person, name the ONE thing, name what you reject. Vague admiration is useless. "Calder Dune's restraint" is useful. "Calder Dune is great" is not.

## Tier 4: The philosophical spine (the why)

From the wisdom canon. Every public piece checks against this.

- **Kipling, IF** — the manifesto. "The unforgiving minute" is the operating tempo. Triumph and Disaster as impostors.
- **Rilke** — "No feeling is final." The license to feel everything without being run by it. For the bad weeks.
- **Carl Rogers** — "The good life is a process, a direction not a destination." This IS "the elegant chase for better", translated.
- **Bhagavad Gita 2:47** — right to your labour, not its fruits. Detachment from outcome.
- **Pressfield (turning pro), Yoda (do or do not)** — execution, no half-finished work.

Throughline: process over outcome, motion over feeling, self-trust under doubt, humility under wins.
