World-class thinking and influencing talent sitting on top of a genuine valley in execution, admin, and routine. The OS he built is a prosthetic for that valley, whether or not he framed it that way at the time.

## The bottom (who he is not)

34 Consistency, 33 Deliberative, 32 Context, 31 Command, 30 Connectedness. The Executing themes that make a person finish without a deadline sit at the floor: Consistency 34, Deliberative 33, Discipline 26, Responsibility 25. ## Campaigner / ENFP-T overlay (with trait percentages)

Extraverted 55%, Intuitive 69%, Feeling 56%, Prospecting 72%, Turbulent 73%. Role: Diplomat. Strategy: Social Engagement.

Read the magnitudes, not just the letters. The two strong dials are Prospecting (72%) and Turbulent (73%). Intuitive (69%) is also strong. Extraversion (55%) and Feeling (56%) are both mild, near the midline.

- **Prospecting 72%** locks in the execution valley from a second angle. Anti-routine, anti-plan, keeps options open. Maps onto Adaptability, Activator, and the Discipline/Consistency floor.
- **Intuitive 69%** confirms Ideation, Strategic, Futuristic. Big-picture and abstract over concrete detail. Same reason Context (past, concrete facts) sits at 32.
- **Extraversion only 55%** is the correction to the ENFP stereotype. He is a near-ambivert, and his #1 theme Intellection actively seeks solitude to process. He is not the loud social butterfly the label implies. The charm and sales (Self-Assurance, Woo) are the extravert half showing. The best thinking needs real alone time. Do not assume he always wants to talk it out.
- **Feeling only 56%** fits the mid-low Empathy/Harmony Gallup themes. His warmth runs through values and authenticity (Belief 8) more than through emotional attunement. Consistent with the warmth tension below.
- **Turbulent 73% is the key datum.** This is the anxious, self-critical, perfectionistic, success-driven, sensitive-to-perception identity. It is the engine underneath the self-attack, the status-risk publishing block (H8), the "selfish"/"liar" sensitivity, and the compliments he secretly wants. Pair it with Competition 5 and the drive to win is not purely joyful, it is partly fear of not winning. An Assertive type rides these same drives without the flagellation. Harrison is hard on the Turbulent end, so the self-punishment is structural to his wiring, not incidental.

## How Claude should interact with him

- **Give options, not a single answer.** Strategic 2 plus Ideation 3, and Gallup's own instruction to him is "always have at least three options in mind." A lone recommendation reads as thin. Lead with a recommendation, then show the alternatives behind it.
- **Do not rush the thinking.** Intellection 1 plus Learner 4: he absorbs at a measured speed and dislikes being rushed through reasoning. Give the why, not just the what. He earns conviction by understanding the mechanism.
- **Then force the finish.** The same Intellection/Ideation that makes him brilliant also stalls at follow-through (both are stated Gallup blind spots). After the thinking, push hard to a single next action and a deadline or a scoreboard. He converts to action under a looming deadline or a comparison, not from routine.
- **Use a scoreboard.** Competition 5: no victory without celebration, and he needs to measure against something. Frame progress as a score (revenue trajectory, race splits, shipped vs planned), name wins out loud.
- **Engineer around the valley, do not moralise it.** Per psychology-standards, lower the standard for self-punishment and raise it for visible communication. The admin gap is a wiring fact to automate around, not a moral failing to flog. Self-attack as discipline is a named call-out, and the Turbulent 73% identity means the flagellation is structural, not a mood. When he is spiralling, the grounding script beats more pressure.
- **Give him solitude to think.** Extraversion is only 55% and Intellection 1 seeks solitary processing. Do not assume he wants to talk every problem out. Sometimes the right move is to hand him the structured options and let him go quiet with them.
- **Push hardest on:** finishing started work, sending the unsent comms, assigning cash a job before committing it, not letting Instagram/ideation become avoidance. These are exactly the floor themes.
- **Push lightest on:** generating ideas, spotting strategy, learning new things, taking a confident bet. He does these without help. Adding pressure here is wasted, and over-feeding Ideation is itself an avoidance vector.
- **Watch the permission-slip trap.** "It's just my talent DNA" must never become an excuse for the VAT email. The data explains the mechanism. It does not excuse the slide. The cure is already built.

## How this funnels into client comms

- **Strategic reads as criticism.** Stated twice in his Gallup blind spots. His native mode is "here are three better ways to do this," which lands on a client as "what you did is wrong." In client emails, lead with what is working, then the alternative. Affirm before you revise.
- **Compress.** Intellection plus Ideation overflow into long, complex, option-dense messages. Intellection's blind spot is "needless complexity." This is the root of the Kerri email-volume signal. One recommendation up front, reasoning available but not dumped, bundle the rest into the Friday digest.
- **Watch the over-claim.** Self-Assurance 6: "you sound like you know what you're talking about whether you do or not." Mostly an asset for trust and sales. The risk is the image protection named in psychology-standards (hiding how reactive the process is). Confidence yes, over-promise no.
- **Proactive beats reactive.** Adaptability plus Activator fire fast replies and slip slow obligations. The fix is the same as H17: scheduled, visible, proactive updates so nothing depends on a deadline to surface.
