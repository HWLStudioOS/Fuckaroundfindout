# The Folder System

The folder is the system. The AI is just the engine that reads it.

This is what you saw on my screen. Mine has grown arms and legs, but the working core is four files and an inbox. Start there and resist adding more.

## The structure

```
my-content-engine/
  content/
    strategy.md
    voice-dna.md
    publishing-rules.md
    waterfall.md
  ideas/
    inbox.md
```

That's it. Five text files in two folders.

## What each file does

**strategy.md** answers "why are we posting at all". Who the content is for, what it should make happen, your three or four pillars. When the AI drifts off into generic business content, this file is what pulls it back.

**voice-dna.md** answers "how do I sound". Real sentences from you, openers you use, words you never use. This is the difference between content that sounds like you and content that sounds like everyone.

**publishing-rules.md** answers "what will I never do". Hard rules the AI must obey. Mine includes things like no engagement bait and no motivational fluff. Every draft gets checked against it.

**waterfall.md** answers "what happens to an idea". One idea per fortnight, cascaded into a longform piece, two video scripts, a LinkedIn post and a thread. One thought, a fortnight of content. You never start from a blank page.

**ideas/inbox.md** is where ideas land. A line from a client call, something you noticed on a job, a number that surprised you. One file, append only. Once a fortnight you pick the best one and run it through the waterfall.

## How to build it

If you ran the Interview Prompt in Claude Code or Codex, it already wrote these files. You're done.

If you ran it in normal Claude or ChatGPT, you have two options:

1. Stay in the chat app. Add the four files to your Project's knowledge and keep a note on your phone as the inbox. Completely fine.
2. Make it real on your computer. Create the folders, then save each file. Use a plain text editor, not Word. The free app Obsidian (obsidian.md) is the nicest way to do this. Point it at your folder and it becomes a clean window onto your system.

If you later install Claude Code, point it at the folder and say "read everything in content/ and tell me what you understand about my business". Watching it play your own strategy back to you is the moment this clicks.

## The rules that keep it alive

- Update the files when something changes. New service, new audience, new rule. Two minutes, in plain English.
- Don't add files because it feels productive. Four files, used weekly, beats forty files, abandoned by July.
- The inbox only works if ideas actually go in it. Capture in the moment, sort later.
