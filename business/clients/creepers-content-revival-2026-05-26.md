## Hook + absorption pass on the existing Hero Film concept ("38 Years in the Ground")

The 2:45 cinematic in development. Before locking the edit, run it through the skill:

- **Visual hook:** Need to confirm this exists. If the film opens on muted establishing shots without a stunning frame, the IG/LI versions won't thumb-stop.
- **Text hook:** "38 Years in the Ground" is a strong tagline but is it the first thing on screen? Should be.
- **Spoken hook (first 5 sec):** What's the first line? If it's "Creepers Nursery has been..." the absorption rate is mid because it sounds like a corporate intro. Test against: "There are two kinds of nursery in this country. We're the other one." (Direct contrast, sets industry common-belief, then flips.)

- **Question chain:** Each section should open a clear question. If the film is "section 1 craft, section 2 origin, section 3 destination, section 4 lineage" without explicit question-bridges, viewers can drop at any pillar transition.

Recommend a 30-min review pass on the cut before publishing. The Michael piece (Move 2) is the shorter, lower-risk first ship. The Hero Film follows once F2 lands.
