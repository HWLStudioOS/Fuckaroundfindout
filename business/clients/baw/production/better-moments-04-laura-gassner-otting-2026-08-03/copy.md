## Acast

### Title

The Four Things That Make Work Worth Doing | Better Moments #4 | Laura Gassner Otting

### Description

A job can look excellent on paper and still feel wrong to the person doing it.

Laura Gassner Otting spent 20 years recruiting senior leaders. The part that stayed with her was how many apparently successful people called back. They had the title, money and prestige. The work still did not fit.

In this ten-minute Better Moment, Laura gives us a more useful career scorecard: calling, connection, contribution and control.

Calling is what pulls you forward. Connection is whether the work matters. Contribution is what the work gives the life you are building. Control is the agency you have over your time, decisions and environment.

The important part is that the four dials do not need to sit at the same level. The right mix changes with the person and the life stage.

Laura is the Wall Street Journal bestselling author of *Limitless* and *Wonderhell*.
