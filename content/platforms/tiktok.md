# TikTok, GTM

The cold-audience top of funnel. Where strangers find you. Same reels as IG, recut 15% tighter, no brand sheet violations.

---

## Role in the system

- **Discovery.** TikTok's For You Page is still the most efficient cold-audience reach engine on the internet for unproven creators.
- **Sieve.** People who follow you on TikTok become the audience that finds you on IG and Substack.
- **NOT a hangout.** You won't engage deeply here. The replies, the DMs, the warmth happens on IG and email. TikTok is the funnel mouth.

---

## What's different from IG

| | IG | TikTok |
|--|----|----|
| Hook length | First 3s | First 1.5s |
| Caption length | Short stanza in body | One line max, hashtags inline |
| Edit pace | Cinematic OK | Cut 15% faster, no slow openers |
| Letterbox bars | OK for Tier B | NO. TikTok punishes letterbox visually |
| Posting cadence | 1-2x/week | 3-5x/week, same content reframed |
| Captions burned in | Word-by-word | MANDATORY, larger, more aggressive |

### The recut for TikTok

Each reel that ships to IG gets a TikTok cut. Differences:
- Trim 0.5-2 seconds off the open. The hook lands on frame 1.
- Remove any letterbox bars. TikTok full-frame 9:16, even for cinematic essays.
- Burned-in captions throughout. Bigger font, higher contrast.
- No accent colour highlight (TikTok audience reads at speed; cream-on-bright-footage gets lost).

### What never goes to TikTok

- Tier C editorial graphics. TikTok hates static motion graphics; LinkedIn wants them.
- Brand-stanza captions. TikTok audience doesn't read the caption. They watch.

---

## Posting playbook

- **Cadence:** 3-5 posts per week. The same week's IG reel posts here Tuesday, then 1-2 supplementary cuts go up Wed/Fri.
- **Supplementary cuts:** carve 15-30 second snippets from your longer cinematic essays as standalone TikToks. The whole essay lives on IG; the highlight cuts live on TikTok.
- **Best post times:** 6-9pm UK time aligns with UK audience + caught the US East Coast morning scroll.

### Hashtags

- 3-5 per post, in the caption body (not first comment like IG).
- Broad + specific mix: `#filmmaking` `#fieldnotes` `#londonlife` `#solopreneur` `#aiforcreators`
- Do not use trending hashtags you don't fit. The algorithm punishes false-signal hashtag use after 2 posts.

### Audio

- **Original audio only** for spoken-script reels. Never trending sounds for talking-heads. Algorithm doesn't care.
- For cinematic essays: original score (licensed) is fine.
- Avoid trending music remixes entirely. They tank the long-term feed.

---

## Engagement

- Reply to comments for 60 min after posting. Same window as IG.
- Don't waste time on TikTok DMs from random accounts. Filter to "people who follow me back AND have a real profile" only.

## What we measure

| Metric | 30-day | 60-day | 90-day |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Followers | 200 | 800 | 2,500 |
| Avg post views | 800 | 3k | 12k |
| Best post views | 5k | 25k | 100k |
| Profile clicks per week | 30 | 100 | 400 |
| Follow-throughs to IG (proxy: IG follower delta in 24h after a TikTok post) | 5 | 20 | 80 |

TikTok numbers are noisier than IG. One viral post can 10x the chart. Don't over-fit to it.

---

## What kills the TikTok account

- Posting once a week. TikTok needs frequency.
- Posting cross-watermarked content (IG watermark on a TikTok upload). Algorithm flags it.
- Lip-syncing trending audio.
- Following trending niches that don't fit (you're not a finance bro, don't make finance-bro content).
- Treating TikTok as "the serious platform", it isn't yet. It's the funnel.
