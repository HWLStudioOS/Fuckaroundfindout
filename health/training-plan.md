Sub-20 means 4:00/km. The week-4 test establishes how many blocks that target needs.

## What changed

The first version was too conservative. It treated a day-nine return as a rule and delayed useful upper lifting because the foot was not ready for impact.

That is not what the evidence supports. A randomised marathon study found that easy running from 48 hours did not worsen recovery through day eight. It does not prove every ultrarunner should run at 48 hours, but it removes the basis for a universal day-nine ban. Harrison's local foot symptoms still control impact. They do not automatically prohibit supported upper training.

## Priority order

1. Respect the focal foot signal and systemic red flags.
2. Start useful strength work now.
3. Preserve the running base and reintroduce quality progressively.
4. Lose fat slowly enough to retain strength, sleep and run quality.
5. Bias hypertrophy towards chest and delts.
6. Build movement skill without creating a second conditioning programme.

## Impact clearance

Run only when all relevant items pass:

1. First 10 morning steps and normal walking are 0-1/10.
2. No swelling, visible limp, protective gait or focal bony tenderness.
3. The completed 30-minute walk caused no later increase.
4. Twenty controlled single-leg calf raises per side remain 0-1/10.
5. Ten small hops remain 0-1/10.
6. Running pain stays 0-1/10 with no gait change.
7. Symptoms remain unchanged two hours later and next morning.

Focal bony tenderness, renewed swelling, worsening bruising or pain on hopping that does not settle warrants sports-physio or medical assessment.

## Strength clearance

- Supported upper work can proceed when Harrison is systemically well, even when impact is not cleared.
- Loaded lower work needs normal walking, no offloading and pain no higher than 1/10 in the chosen pattern.
- The first lower exposures use two work sets and RIR 3-4.
- Historical PB percentages are not used.

# Twelve-week architecture

## Weekly structure

| Day | Session | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Upper Physique plus Movement C | Chest and delt priority. Supported upper work has little leg cost the day after the long run. Hanging and shoulder control are integrated, not a second workout. |
| Monday | VO2 or 5K-specific run | Highest-quality running after a leg-free Sunday. |
| Tuesday | Strength A plus Movement A | Squat, bench, hinge, row, calves and abs. Feet, ankles and knees are the first 10 minutes. |
| Wednesday | Easy run plus Movement B | Aerobic volume and recovery. Hips and spine later for 10-12 minutes. |
| Thursday AM | Threshold or steady run | Sustainable speed and 5K support. |
| Thursday PM | Strength C | Lower-volume hinge and unilateral work, chest, back, delts, arms and abs. At least six hours after the run. |
| Friday | Movement D or full rest | Floor work and locomotion at RPE 2-3. No conditioning. This protects Saturday. |
| Saturday | Long easy run | Aerobic durability and running-base preservation. This is the fixed weekly anchor. |

This arrangement puts both important runs before lower-body lifting, gives Friday back to recovery, and protects Saturday as the fixed long-run day. Thursday is the only hard double. The run comes first, the sessions are separated by at least six hours, and the lower-body strength dose stays small enough to recover for an easy Saturday.

## Progression

- First exposure: select a load that honestly leaves the prescribed RIR.
- When every set reaches the top of its range at the target RIR, add the smallest practical increment next time.
- Weeks 9-10 may use one main-lift top set at RIR 1-2 followed by two back-off sets 5-8% lighter.
- No compound failure and no grinders.
- Log load, reps and RIR for every set. Baseline calculates e1RM from completed work.
- Do not true-1RM test during this block.

Concurrent training does not automatically erase strength or hypertrophy. The main interference concern here is fatigue and explosive performance when hard modes are crowded together. Thursday's threshold run therefore comes first, with Strength C at least six hours later and only two primary lower-body work sets per pattern. Friday then absorbs the residual fatigue before Saturday's easy long run.

# Movement practice

## Evidence-led elements

- Progressive resistance training.
- Foot-core work.
- Heavy calf and soleus work.
- Progressive knee and hip loading.
- Later low-dose plyometrics for running economy, only after the foot is fully quiet.
- Full-range strength work where positions remain controlled.

## Ido-inspired elements

- Passive and active hanging.
- Deep-squat and shin-box accumulation.
- Bear, crab and ape locomotion.
- Technical stand-ups, rolls and floor transitions.
- Short improvised flows.

These build movement vocabulary, coordination and enjoyment. They are not presented as post-ultra recovery treatments, injury prevention or VO2 work.

Ido Portal's official hanging challenge uses seven cumulative minutes daily. This plan starts at 60-120 seconds. There is no direct evidence that seven minutes is a universal shoulder-health dose.

The drill library and progression are carried inside each dated Baseline session. Movement never needs a separate log.
