## Everyday content strategy (what makes the days between hero pieces work)

The hero pieces are the spine. The daily content is the muscle. Without it the account flatlines between fortnights.

**Three rules:**

1. **Capture as you work.** Mike at the nursery, Harrison on shoots, every Build Week, every delivery. 90 seconds of phone footage per visit, stockpiled in a Drive folder. The PH editor cuts daily/weekly fillers from this stockpile, not from new shoots.
2. **Series-coded everything.** Every post says which series it's in (text overlay or first caption line). No untagged content. Audience learns the formats.
3. **Once a fortnight, one risk piece.** Test a new format inside one of the existing series. If it lands, fold into the formal cadence. If it doesn't, kill it without ceremony.

**What the PH editor takes off Harrison's plate (post-hire Fri 29 May):**
- All Stock Walks (template-driven; editor cuts from stockpile)
- All Specimen of the Week posts
- All Installation Snapshots
- All hero-piece derivative cuts (short cuts, carousels, LinkedIn editions)
- Harrison retains: shoot direction, hero piece edits, the long YouTube cuts

This is what makes 56-80 pieces of content over 16 weeks feasible on the current retainer.
