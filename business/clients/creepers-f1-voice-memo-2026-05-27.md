The audio-led format also matches what we actually have. Build Week B-roll only, no afters. A voice-over piece carries the "documentary, not corporate" tone better than a faux talking-head where Michael recaps a result he wasn't filmed delivering.

## What we send Michael (the brief)

Voice memo, recorded on the phone, ~3-4 minutes raw. He doesn't need to edit it. He talks like he's talking to one designer in a pub. He can record in multiple takes if he wants, we'll cut.

Six talking points below. He hits the ones that feel true. He can skip any. He can go off-script. The script is a spine, not a cage.

### Talking points for Michael

1. **The contrast.** "People watch Chelsea on TV and see a finished garden. They don't see what it actually takes to put one on the ground." Tell them what they don't see.

2. **The number, plainly.** "Two golds. A silver. Five-star trade stand." Don't dress it up. Don't say "we're delighted." Just say what we won.

3. **One specific moment from build week.** Pick one. Something physical. The day a specimen arrived. The night before judging. The thing that went wrong and got fixed. The smell of the soil at 6am. One moment, told in detail.

4. **The 38-year line.** "What you saw at Chelsea this year started in our ground decades ago. The trees we put in that garden have been growing for longer than most of the designers building it have been alive." Or his version of that. The point: the result is a backlog, not a sprint.

5. **What this means for the people watching.** Direct address to designers. "If you're building a garden that has to win, the question isn't where you get the plants. It's who's been growing them long enough to give you the ones that can." Or softer if that's not his voice.

6. **Closer, quiet.** One sentence. Not a CTA. Something a designer would repost. Could be: "38 years in the ground. That's the only shortcut there is." Or his own line. The kind of thing that lands because it's true, not because it sells.

### How to brief him

Send him the bullets in a WhatsApp note. Tell him:
- Phone voice memo, no need to be polished
- 3-4 minutes total, multiple takes fine
- Talk to one designer, not to a camera
- If he gets one good line, that's enough
- Background sound is welcome (we'll EQ it, but field texture sells the doc tone)

### What we cut

- 90 sec primary cut (@creepersinstallation Reel + LinkedIn native)
- 45 sec short (TikTok + IG Reel B + @creepersnursery)
- 30 sec teaser (cross-post, results-led)
- Long-form audio sits in archive; can be re-cut as the YouTube long-form spine if the Hero Film slips

### Three-element hook check

- **Visual hook:** Open on the best Build Week frame we have. Wide of Royal Hospital with the build in foreground if it exists, otherwise a tight on Michael's hands or a specimen being placed.
- **Text hook (first card):** "Two golds. One silver. Five-star trade stand. Here's what nobody films."
- **Spoken hook (first 5 sec, Michael):** Whatever the strongest line in the voice memo is. We pull it forward in the edit, even if he said it three minutes in.

Context-before-contrast holds: viewer knows it's a Chelsea recap (context) before Michael flips to "here's what you don't see" (contrast).
