# Golf-course flyover, rights-cleared pilot brief

**Status:** Internal concept only. No active campaign, club contact, upload or media generation.

**Relationship to prior work:** the July golf-club batch remains killed. This is a fresh offer test, not permission to send the expired five openers.

## Decision

There is a credible product here, but not the version built by scraping Google imagery and sending speculative course flyovers cold.

The viable offer is one accurate, rights-cleared signature-hole or course-story flyover built with the club's permission and source material that can legally be used in commercial promotion. Prove the production method once, then use the finished proof in a small named-human outreach batch.

## Hard rights boundary

Do not use:

- Google Earth or Earth Studio imagery in a club promotional film, advert, sales sample or paid client deliverable.
- Google Maps satellite imagery for a promotional flyover.
- Street View imagery in a promotional video.
- Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles as a resold standalone video.
- Scraped course photographs, member photographs or drone footage without written commercial rights.
- AI reconstruction intended to hide the fact that the source material was unlicensed.

Google's current Geo Guidelines say Google Earth content may not be used for commercial or promotional purposes, including promotional films and advertising. The Map Tiles API video exception is limited to videos under 30 seconds about the capabilities of the developer's own application, with attribution, and the video may not be resold. A golf-club flyover product does not fit that exception. [Google Geo Guidelines](https://about.google/intl/en-GB_ALL/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/) and [Map Tiles API policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies)

## Where Viewmax may fit

Viewmax can be an optional rendering or generation layer only after the inputs are cleared. Its license says the user retains rights to uploaded or created content, but also represents that they hold every right necessary to use and process that content. Viewmax does not turn unlicensed Google or third-party imagery into a lawful commercial asset. [Viewmax License](https://www.viewmax.io/license) and [Viewmax Terms](https://www.viewmax.io/terms)

Before using Viewmax on a club job:

1. Record every input in a rights manifest.
2. Confirm the club grants commercial processing and promotional use.
3. Confirm any generated model, stock layer, music and voice license permits client commercial work.
4. Keep the source and output reviewable for course accuracy.

## Approved source routes

### Route A, club-supplied media

- Club-owned drone footage, stills, course map and brand kit.
- Written confirmation that the club can authorise commercial derivative work.
- Best first route because it reduces access, weather and flight risk.

### Route B, new HWL capture

- Written site and landowner permission.
- Legal flight planning, airspace and operational checks completed before capture.
- A short golden-hour capture of one signature hole, clubhouse arrival and one heritage detail.
- The club approves the factual route and visible boundaries before publication.

### Route C, openly licensed geodata

- Terrain, route or elevation data with a commercial license and required attribution.
- Stylised visual treatment rather than pretending it is current photorealistic footage.
- Club-supplied photography can be layered in once rights are documented.

## Pilot product

One club. One signature hole or one simple arrival-to-clubhouse story.

Deliverables:

- One 30 to 45-second landscape master.
- One 15 to 30-second vertical cut.
- Five still frames for social or a pitch deck.
- One clean route or hole annotation layer.
- One round of factual corrections.
- A source and rights manifest retained with the project.

Out of scope for the pilot:

- Full 18-hole library.
- Live yardages or player navigation.
- Course-management software.
- Unverified terrain, hazards or hole geometry.
- Paid advertising rights unless explicitly agreed.

## Production sequence

1. Pick a club where Harrison has a genuine introduction or permission route.
2. Secure a written pilot agreement and source-rights confirmation.
3. Choose one hole with a clear visual and strategic story.
4. Build a 10-frame storyboard before any generation.
5. Capture or ingest authorised inputs.
6. Produce the flyover and preserve visible geographic accuracy.
7. Course manager or head professional checks route, naming and hazards.
8. Club approves the final and case-study use separately.
9. Only then build a three-club named-human outreach batch.

## Pilot club filter

Choose a club that has:

- A warm path to a decision-maker.
- One visually distinctive hole, building or renovation story.
- Clear ownership of source imagery.
- A course manager or head professional willing to check accuracy.
- Permission to show the result publicly as HWL proof.

If no club meets all five, do not build the pilot yet.

## Outreach after proof

The first outreach batch is three named people, not the expired July five. The opener shows the permissioned proof, names one specific story at their club and asks whether a single-hole pilot is worth scoping. It does not attach an unsolicited reconstruction of their course.

Possible wedges from the existing research, subject to fresh verification:

- A clubhouse or course renovation that deserves a before-and-after record.
- A signature hole whose design logic is difficult to understand from static photography.
- A member and visitor orientation asset that also works as social content.
- A heritage route showing how a classic course has changed over time using club-owned archive imagery.

## Commercial decision still required

Do not set a permanent price from this document. After a rights-cleared test, choose between:

- A paid standalone pilot.
- A reduced pilot credited against a wider monthly content engagement.
- A partner-funded proof where the club supplies all media and grants public case-study rights.

No free bespoke build is promised in cold outreach.

## Definition of done

- Every source asset has a recorded commercial right.
- The course route is checked by a qualified club contact.
- The output contains no Google Earth, satellite, Street View or restricted Map Tiles content.
- The club separately approves publication and case-study use.
- One proof supports three fresh, named-human conversations.
