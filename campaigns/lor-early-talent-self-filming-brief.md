# Filming your day. A quick guide

You don't need to be good on camera. You don't need to edit anything. Just film small pieces of your real day and send us the clips. We do the rest.

## The setup

- **Hold your phone sideways (landscape).** Every clip, every time.
- Wipe the lens first. Ten seconds, biggest quality upgrade there is.
- Face a window or the light. Don't stand with it behind you.
- Prop the phone or hold it at arm's length, roughly eye level.
- Find a quiet-ish spot when you're talking. Wind and site noise kill audio.

## What to film

Think of it as six or seven short clips across one normal day:

1. Your morning or commute, ten seconds is plenty.
2. Arriving at your desk, site, or office.
3. One real task, show us what you're actually looking at or doing.
4. A moment with someone you work with (ask them first).
5. Lunch, the gym, the coffee run, whatever your day actually includes.
6. One thing that surprised you about the job, said to camera.
7. End of the day, one line on how it went.

## When you talk to camera

- Keep clips short, 20 to 40 seconds.
- Say what you're doing and where you are, like you're telling a mate.
- Don't script it. First take is usually the best one.
- Mistakes, pauses and laughs are fine. That's the point.

## Please don't

- Film drawings, screens, or documents that might be confidential. When in doubt, point the camera elsewhere.
- Film anyone without asking them first.
- Film anything that shows a safety shortcut, even a small one.
- Edit, add music, or put filters on anything. Send it raw.
