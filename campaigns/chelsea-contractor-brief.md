## Shot list

### Hero, the post

**Michael Buck judging-eve walk-through.** ~3 to 5 min rolling clip, phone in landscape. Quiet, observational. Him looking at the work, touching specimens, in conversation with designers. This becomes the Friday evening or Saturday morning Nursery IG post. Caption locked: *"Michael Buck on the ground at Chelsea. Two days to judging."*

If turnaround is tight, hold for Saturday morning rather than rushing the edit.

### People moments (priority)

- Designers in conversation, tight crops. Faces, hands. No audio needed.
- Team in gear, working. Michael with the crew.
- Designers stepping back to look at finished plots, end of day.
- Catherine MacDonald, Baz Grainger, Carey duo: tight portraits if naturally available. Do not pose them.

### B-roll, build-week prep

- Final touches: hands on details, tools being placed down, last specimens being set.
- Scaffolding coming down.
- Materials moving, deliveries, crates.
- Quiet hands, single specimens (tight crops, not compositional).
- A designer pausing.
- 10 to 15 seconds at each plot of one quiet moment, for post-show use.

### Stories pace

4 to 6 across the day, spread out. Not blasted in one go. Restraint is the point. Capture more than that, just don't post more than that.

### What NOT to capture for public release

- No wide garden compositions
- No reveals of finished gardens
- No anything that telegraphs the design intent
- No BBC cameras, crew, or branding visible in shots intended for posting

## Embargo rules

BBC have exclusive on all garden content until press day, Monday 18 May. Embargo violations damage Creepers' RHS relationships permanently. This is not a soft rule.

### Safe to post (with Harrison sign-off)

- Tight people moments
- Hands, tools, specimens at close crop, not compositional
- Build-process context, scaffolding, materials moving
- "Two days to judging" energy with no garden reveal in frame

### Capture only, do not post

- Full garden compositions
- Wide plot shots
- Anything showing the finished design
- BBC cameras working, crew, or branding (no tagging, no reference under accreditation terms)

### Sign-off rule

Nothing goes to feed or stories without Harrison's explicit approval. Workflow: contractor captures, sends frames or clips to Harrison via WhatsApp, Harrison approves, Harrison posts. Contractor does not post from Creepers accounts directly.
