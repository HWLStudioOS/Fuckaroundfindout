## Negotiation risks around the price range

- **Scope creep at the floor price.** Harrison's own negotiation rule in the offer doc: do not let £3,600 become eight episodes, publishing, all launch assets, recurring clips, and Farringdon attendance. This is the single most likely risk in the room, since a three-person call gives more surface area for "can we also get X" than an email reply would.
- **Podcast House anchor.** They have an existing vendor and a performance pack to compare against. Risk they anchor the conversation on Podcast House's current rate rather than the value of the upgrade. Harrison's direct, current knowledge of that studio (via BaW) is the counter to this, not just a discount.
- **Unconfirmed attendees.** Sarah Ellis and Sarah Massie have not accepted as of 13 August. If they don't join, this becomes Helen alone, and any scope or budget questions that need a partner sign-off may stall rather than close on the call. If Sarah Massie turns out to be a finance or ops contact rather than editorial, price objections could come from an angle the offer doc wasn't written for.
- **No confirmed written buy-in yet.** Every internal file shows the range was sent and a call was booked, not that the range was accepted in principle. There is a residual risk the call opens more open than "negotiation of a deal already agreed in shape," despite that being the likely case per the task framing. Worth listening for in the first few minutes rather than assuming.
