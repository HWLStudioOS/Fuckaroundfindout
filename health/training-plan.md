Lake Bled is not happening. The old ultra plan and the 21.2 km maintenance week are retired. This block restores the useful Baseline plan: four runs, two full-body strength sessions, one proper rest day.

## Aim

Improve VO2 max, rebuild a comfortable long run, reduce body weight gradually and restore consistent strength. The goal is not new powerlifting bests during a cut. It is to move the current 160 kg deadlift, 100 kg bench and 120 kg squat back towards dependable training numbers while retaining enough muscle to look good as weight drops.

### Strength progression

- Week 1 establishes honest working loads. Historical bests are not prescriptions.
- When every rep is clean and two reps remain, add 2.5 kg to upper-body lifts or 5 kg to lower-body lifts the next week.
- Week 4 is a deload: remove one set from every exercise and keep three reps in reserve.
- Week 8 keeps normal load but removes one set from squat and deadlift. No 1RM test.
- If Tuesday's interval quality falls for two weeks, remove one lower-body accessory set before changing the run plan.

## Weight-loss system

1. Take seven morning weigh-ins, after the toilet and before food. Use the weekly average, never a single day.
2. Aim to lose 0.25 to 0.5 per cent of body weight per week.
3. Start with a 300 to 400 kcal daily average deficit. Do not deepen it until two full weekly averages exist.
4. Eat 1.8 to 2.0 g protein per kg body weight each day, spread across three to five meals.
5. Put 30 to 60 g carbohydrate in the two hours before Tuesday quality and Sunday's long run.
6. On runs longer than 75 minutes, use 30 to 60 g carbohydrate per hour and practise drinking to thirst.
7. If weight falls faster than 0.75 per cent for two weeks, strength falls across both sessions, or sleep and mood deteriorate, add 200 kcal per day.

## Readiness and knee rails

- Green: normal gait, knee 0 to 1 out of 10, no swelling and normal morning energy. Run the plan.
- Amber: pain builds during a run, changes mechanics or remains elevated the next morning. Remove the next quality session and use easy cycling or walking.
- Red: swelling, locking, giving way, sharp focal pain or altered gait. Stop running and get clinical assessment.
- If readiness is below 45, or HRV is more than 20 per cent below the rolling baseline for two mornings alongside raised resting heart rate or poor wellbeing, remove intensity. Easy movement is optional.
- Missed sessions are missed. Do not cram them into the next three days.

## Eight-week scorecard

- Four runs completed in at least six of eight weeks.
- Two strength sessions completed in at least six of eight weeks.
- Long run reaches 16 km with a quiet knee.
- Controlled 5 km improves against the first interval week's current fitness, without a taper or race effort.
- Weekly average body weight falls by 2 to 4 per cent across the block, if the starting weight and recovery support it.
- Main-lift working weights trend up while two reps remain in reserve.
