# Creepers Content Strategy — Hybrid Frame

> Research-backed draft, 9 June 2026. Spine for the client-facing plan owed Anna + Sarah Thu 11 / Fri 12 June. **Two items need verifying before client use (flagged ⚠️ in §2 and §10).**

## 1. The frame

Creepers sells expertise, not atmosphere. The hybrid thesis: every post is a documented act of plantsmanship, which is simultaneously brand-building (Rob's "Chanel of plants" restraint) and sales-supporting (the team's "show me it's working"). The restraint lives in HOW each post is shot, one plant, one hand, one moment, real sound, not in posting less. The two pages do two different jobs: @creepersnursery makes Creepers the nursery designers source from, @creepersinstallation makes the craft and the supply chain the reason HNW clients enquire. The answer to "this isn't helping us" is that documented craft and sourcing intel are the SAME content, just shot and captioned for intent (named plant, size, availability, the "why") instead of mood-film with no payload.

Default the feed to sales-supporting. Brand texture is the surplus that rides on top of the availability spine, never the other way round. If a week forces a choice, the availability post ships and the atmosphere clip waits.

## 2. Content pillars

These pillars sit UNDER the five series the audience is already being taught, they do not replace them:

- **Stock Walk** = P1 Fresh In / Ready Now (the sales spine).
- **The Specimens** = P2 Specimen Hero / Spec This.
- **Inside the Build** + **From Nursery to Medal** = P3 Grower Craft / Provenance and the @installation supply-chain lane.
- **Garden Voices** = P3 Designers We Supply on @installation.
- **Monday Minute** continues as the fixed expertise anchor.

Royal Thread (Chelsea + Windsor) retained as a HERO over-arc only, not a weekly pillar. Sandringham dead, so the line is "two Royal proofs," used in quarterly tentpoles. YouTube long-form held as a separate keep-or-cut decision due 15 June (§7).

### @creepersnursery (B2B / garden designers, ~12.8K)

**P1 — Fresh In / Ready Now (sourcing tool, the mandatory spine).** Sells availability. Visual front-end of the trade availability list. Named cultivar + size/girth + rough stock count in frame. Types: 10-15s specimen reel, "just became spec-able this week" carousel, restock macro with a "DM to reserve for your next spec" close. The non-negotiable per-visit deposit.

**P2 — Specimen Hero / Spec This (design-object framing).** Sells the plant as a design move. One architectural specimen shot product-style with scale reference, framed for how a designer USES it. Types: single-specimen carousel cut-sheet (size, habit, conditions, pairings).

**P3 — Grower Craft / Provenance (trust).** Sells quality and standards, removes the risk objection. Hands at work, hand-pruning, root-ball checks, "8 years in production," UK-grown vs bought-in plugs. Types: 20-40s no-narration craft clip, QC moment with the grower naming the standard.

**P4 — Sourcing Trips / Hidden Sources (mystique + variety).** Sells the hunt and depth of range. New Forest runs, Murray Street (Wonersh) as the "secret warehouse designers don't know exists." Types: monthly sourcing mini-doc, Murray Street reveal, "this one's going to a project" specimen put-aside.

### @creepersinstallation (HNW end-clients + spec'ing designers, ~2.3K, NDA-limited)

**P1 — Where Your Plants Come From (the ownable lane, NDA-safe default).** Sells supply-chain seriousness, needs no client sign-off because it never happens at a client site. "How a 4m multistem gets from our Surrey fields to a private garden," sourcing, root-pruning, lift, load, transport. The safe backbone of the page.

**P2 — Sketch to Sculpture / Material Palette (craft under NDA).** Sells material literacy without showing the site. Macro details of stonework, planting joins, corten patina, a plan on a board, the reasoning. Types: hard-cropped detail carousel from client iPhone stills. Gated by the NDA checklist in §6.

**P3 — Designers We Supply (the B2B-to-HNW bridge).** Sells third-party credibility. Designers who already tag Creepers become the sales channel. Per-designer, per-post written consent required, no blanket lane. ⚠️ **Start with ONE confirmed yes — identify a real designer who has credited Creepers (verify against who actually tags the page; the research named "Pollyanna Wilkinson" but that is UNVERIFIED and not in our contacts).**

**P4 — Cost / Value Education (enquiry qualifier).** Sells the premium and filters tyre-kickers. One detail image plus a breakdown of what a Creepers-grade scheme involves, benchmarked against interior spend. Pinned anchor post, quarterly, no property shown, clear enquire CTA.

## 3. The weekly posting model

Target: ~3x/week @creepersnursery, ~2x/week @creepersinstallation on grid, plus a daily Stories drip. Sustained from one fortnightly Surrey shoot + one monthly New Forest trip + client iPhone stills. No full-day crews.

**This cadence is only feasible WITH David carrying the derivatives** (§4). Without him, the realistic sustainable grid is 2x nursery + 1x install, not 5 posts plus daily Stories.

| Day | @creepersnursery | @creepersinstallation |
|-----|------------------|----------------------|
| Mon | Monday Minute (rotate host) | — |
| Tue | — | Where Your Plants Come From (process/sourcing reel) |
| Wed | Grower craft / BTS process reel | — |
| Thu | — | Sketch to Sculpture detail carousel (client stills) |
| Fri | Fresh In / Ready Now (availability) | — |

3 nursery + 2 install = 5 grid posts/week. Designer-repost and cost-education drop in monthly/quarterly as bonus, not load-bearing.

**The maths, honestly.** A focused Surrey visit is a 45-75 minute shoot (not 8-12 min), yielding ~6-8 finished nursery assets plus 15-20 stills after edit-cull.

- **Visit week:** all three nursery posts cut from the fresh shoot. Friday Fresh In uses that day's live stock data.
- **Off week:** Mon/Wed from the bank (evergreen Monday Minute + banked craft clip). Off-week Friday Fresh In is the weak point, closed by **Michael texting a stock list each Thursday** (or a pre-shot evergreen "Ready Now" format that needs no live data), never by hoping for a shoot.

Monthly New Forest yields a hero reel + 4-6 install clips + Story slides, feeding the Tuesday slot across the month. Client stills, run through David's grade, fill Thursday carousels. Stories (3-7 raw slides/day, David's job) absorb the "more, everyday" ask without inflating the considered grid.

**Banking rule.** Over-shoot in peak, tag evergreen craft by season, never hold fewer than 2 weeks banked per page. But the bankable surplus is mostly evergreen-CRAFT. Availability ("Fresh In") is perishable, cannot be banked. So the Friday sales spine is always fed live, never from the bank.

## 5. Capture checklists

### Fortnightly Surrey visit (the core deposit, 45-75 min focused shoot)

1. **Fresh In / Ready Now (MANDATORY, shoot first).** 3-5 genuinely-ready specimens, a 10-15s clip + 2-3 stills each. Walk the stock blocks with Michael, write cultivar + size/girth + rough count on the spot. The deposit that justifies the retainer, never cut for time.
2. **One process/craft clip,** 20-40s, no narration, tight crop, real tool sound. Rotate the hands, not always Michael.
3. **One Specimen Hero set,** 6 frames of one architectural specimen: clean static beauty, slow push-in or hand-into-frame, one detail macro, straight-on, scale reference (mandatory), 10s clean ambient audio.
4. **3-5 NDA-safe install macros** for @installation: bark, leaf, stone, steel, soil, root-ball. Detail only, never a placeable wide.
5. **2-3 satisfying-craft micro-clips** for the reach bank (optional, surplus only).
6. **One establishing wide** of the nursery (optional, bank only).
7. **One quiet atmosphere clip** (optional, bank only).

Items 1-4 are the non-negotiable deposit. 5-7 are surplus, shot only if time and light allow.

### New Forest / Murray Street trip (monthly)

- Hero specimen against sky; buyer's hand on bark; wide "lines of plants like soldiers"; the tag going on; the loaded trailer; one piece-to-camera, "why this one."
- Murray Street as a one-off hero reveal: rows of perennials, the trade-only feel, "the bit of Creepers most people don't know exists." Recurring small-perennial close-ups thereafter.

### Cast diversification — hard quota, not "let data decide"

Saves-first optimisation REINFORCES Michael, so performance data cannot be the diversification mechanism. Fixed quota, reviewed fortnightly:

- Max **1 Michael-fronted post/week** on @nursery.
- At least **1 faceless-hands or place-as-character post/week**.
- Rotate a **second on-camera voice monthly** (a buyer on a sourcing trip, a propagator at Murray Street).

Keep Michael on Monday Minute and the odd One-Plant reel. He is an asset, not a problem. The quota stops the page reverting to "Michael's Instagram."

### iPhone capture card for Anna / Sarah (3 rules, hand them this)

1. **Good light only.** Window light or an overcast day. No flash, no direct midday sun.
2. **Get close and hold still.** Fill the frame with one plant or one detail, tap to focus, keep steady.
3. **Send the name and size, to the folder.** Plant name + size + rough count (nursery) or material name (install). Drop to the shared Dropbox folder, not WhatsApp.

Attach one good example photo and one bad one. Polish happens in David's edit, not on the phone. ProRAW/six-angle protocols belong to Harrison's own capture, not theirs.

**Fallback if stills don't arrive.** If no compliant stills land by Wednesday, Thursday's @installation slot runs from Harrison's banked NDA-safe macros (item 4). Client supply is a bonus on a self-sufficient bank, never the only source. This avoids repeating the Chelsea access-failure pattern.

## 6. NDA gate (@installation only)

Every client-sourced asset clears all three before posting:

1. **No identifiable feature or location.** A hard crop alone is not enough, distinctive stonework/joins can still be recognisable. Generic enough to place nowhere.
2. **Metadata and geotag stripped** on every file.
3. **Named client sign-off logged** before posting.

The **Where Your Plants Come From** lane is the safe default that needs none of this. When in doubt, post supply-chain, not site-detail. The **Designers We Supply** repost lane needs per-designer, per-post written consent.

## 8. The hero / hygiene rhythm

- **Hygiene (always-on, retainer).** Everything in §5. Fortnightly Surrey + monthly New Forest + client stills carry 3x/week, David cutting derivatives + Stories. This is what £3,500/month covers.
- **Hero (tentpole, ~quarterly).** Cinematic Chelsea-grade pieces, the Royal arc, signature install reveals. Each hero chopped into 5-8 hygiene cuts so one premium shoot feeds weeks.

**The commercial boundary (written tripwire).** Retainer £3,500/month flat covers: fortnightly Surrey + monthly New Forest + client-still curation + light production + scheduling. Anything beyond = **£70/hr out-of-scope**. Any full crewed day = **separate quote, exactly as Chelsea was**. Flag any crewed-shoot request as billable BEFORE it hits the schedule.

## 9. Measurement (tied to the fortnightly meeting)

- **Single commercial success metric:** first attributable installation enquiry. Not reach.
- **Supporting:** saves on @nursery, DM/email sourcing enquiries, designer reposts.
- **Baseline:** record current saves, enquiry count, designer-tag count at the FIRST fortnightly meeting.
- **Review loop:** report against baseline every fortnight. Review the cast quota by eye at the same meeting.
- Put the trade availability-list link in bio and in high-intent captions so enquiries attribute cleanly.

## 10. What to STOP / fix

- **"Michael's Instagram" over-reliance.** Fix: the hard quota in §5.
- **Chelsea-only / pure brand mood-film.** Every post carries a named plant, technique, size, or moment. Atmosphere is the wrapper, never the content.
- **Showing installation sites.** NDA dead end. Build @installation on the supply-chain default + gated detail (§6).
- **Dumping raw client stills to the grid.** David's locked Creepers preset on every supplied still, raw goes to Stories only.
- **Measuring this on reach.** Use §9. First attributable installation enquiry is the headline number.
- **Letting the bank refill the feed with brand-craft.** The Friday sales spine is always fed live.
- **Engagement-bait / discount-urgency captions.** Field-notes calm, named plants, named people, the season, one quiet "DM to enquire."
