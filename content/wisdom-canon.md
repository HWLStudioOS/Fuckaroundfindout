# Wisdom Canon

Harrison's saved wisdom, captured from Apple Notes 2026-05-18. This is reference material for any script, post, essay, or caption draft. Every piece of public output should be checkable against this canon: would the writer of this also have saved these lines?

These pieces share a single throughline: **process over outcome, motion over feeling, self-trust under doubt, humility under wins.** That's the spine of the public voice.

---

## The frame

> The world is run by:
> - People no smarter than you
> - People who have no idea what they're doing
> - Failures, dropouts, has-beens, underdogs
>
> Always back yourself.

The outsider permission slip. Sits underneath the whole "elegant chase for better" arc. License to swing.

---

## Process over destination

> The good life is a process, not a state of being. It is a direction not a destination.
> Carl Rogers

Direct lineage to the personal tagline "I am on the elegant chase for better." The tagline is Carl Rogers translated into Kiwi.

> You have a right to your labor, but not to the fruits of your labor.
> Bhagavad Gita 2:47

Same thesis. Detachment from outcome, devotion to the work.

---

## Stoic spine

> IF you can keep your head when all about you are losing theirs and blaming it on you,
> If you can trust yourself when all men doubt you, but make allowance for their doubting too...
> If you can meet with Triumph and Disaster and treat those two impostors just the same...
> If you can fill the unforgiving minute with sixty seconds' worth of distance run,
> Yours is the Earth and everything that's in it.
> Rudyard Kipling

Kipling's IF is the personal manifesto. "The unforgiving minute" is the operating tempo. Triumph and Disaster as impostors maps directly to the codex psychology-standards file.

> Let everything happen to you: beauty and terror. Just keep going. No feeling is final.
> Rilke

The license to feel everything without being run by it. The thing to remember on the bad weeks.

## Ego checks

> Sweep the floor. Check your fucking ego every day.

Daily reset. Pair with: "if someone watched you for a week, would they believe you're serious about your goals?" Behavior is the only evidence.

> What is grief, if not love persevering?
> Vision, WandaVision

The soft side. Love and grief as one thing. Sits next to Rilke.

## How to use this file

Before any public-facing draft (script, caption, essay, bio), do one check:
1. Does this piece breathe the same air as Kipling's IF and Rilke?
2. Does it have process-over-outcome at its core, or is it status-chasing?
3. Would the person who saved these lines also publish this?

If any answer is no, rewrite.
