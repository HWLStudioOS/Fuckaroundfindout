# Health observations

Owned by the `pattern-lab` agent, per `agents/STATE-OWNERSHIP.md`.

Observations about Harrison's own Garmin data across weeks, which the daily
brief cannot see because it reports a snapshot.

**These are observations, not advice.** `health/training-plan.md` is Harrison's
file and no agent edits it. Nothing here prescribes a session, a load or a rest
day. Each entry carries a falsifiable prediction, a window and a counter-read,
and gets closed when the window passes. An observation never resolved is a
horoscope with a timestamp.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `open` | Prediction made, window still running |
| `held` | Prediction came true. Say what that means. |
| `broke` | Prediction failed. The pattern was noise. |
| `unclear` | Data gap or changed circumstances. Not a free retry. |

## 2026-08-15 | open | Easy runs drifting into zone 3

**Observation.** Across 26 July to 13 August, six runs read as designated easy or long (excluding the named tempo on 4 August, the 31 July session with 13 min in zone 5, and the 13 August VO2 session). Five of the six spent 38 to 59 per cent of their time in Garmin zone 3: 26 Jul 24 of 41 min, 28 Jul 14 of 28, 2 Aug 25 of 65, 8 Aug 41 of 79, 11 Aug 16 of 35. Average HR on those five sat in a tight band, 154 to 157. Zone 4 and 5 time was near zero, so these are not tempo runs in the threshold sense; they drift to upper-moderate. The exception is 6 August: average HR 143, 1 minute of 28 in zone 3, at nearly the same pace (6:28/km against 6:23/km on 26 Jul and 11 Aug), so genuinely easy at his normal pace exists in the data. The Build + Engine plan prescribes easy runs at conversational RPE 2 to 3 and names this exact failure mode: they never become hidden tempo sessions. The one easy run inside the block so far, Tuesday 11 August, followed the old pattern, not the prescription.

**Prediction.** If this is habit rather than noise, the Sunday 16 August 75-minute long run and the next two designated easy runs (Monday 17 and Friday 21 August) will each again show 40 per cent or more of time in zone 3 with average HR above 150.

**Window.** Closes end of Friday 21 August. If two or more of the three come in mostly zones 1 and 2, like 6 August, the pattern is noise or already self-corrected, and it closes either way.

**Counter-read.** Zone minutes are only as good as the configured zones. Max HR recorded 197 on 13 August; if the watch's set max is lower than his real max, zone 3 starts too low and these numbers overstate effort. August heat also raises HR at a given pace, inflating zone time without the legs working harder. Only Harrison can see the watch's zone settings; that single check would confirm or kill the calibration read.

**Sample.** Six runs across three weeks, five matching. Inside the block itself, one easy run, so n=1 in-block. Logged as a suspicion with a test, not a finding.

**What settles it.** Watch the next three easy or long sessions. Nothing needs changing for the test to run.

**Interim, 19 August.** Window still open, closes as scheduled. Sunday 16 August came in genuinely easy, 0 of 34 minutes in zone 3, average HR 140, but at 34 of the prescribed 75 minutes. Monday 17 August has no run in the data at all. The strict all-three prediction can no longer hold, so Saturday's close will be broke or unclear depending on Friday 21 and on how much weight a half-length long run can carry as a test.
